**The RRB ALP admit card 2026 is out.** Railway Recruitment Boards put up the e-call letters for **CBT 1 of CEN 01/2026** on their regional websites on 30 October 2026. The exam is on **3, 4 and 5 November 2026**.

This is the Assistant Loco Pilot recruitment for **11,127 posts**. Cards don't all open on the same day. Each one opens about 4 days before your exam, so check the table below before you panic about a link that "isn't working".

So if your city slip says 5 November and the link still shows nothing today, that's normal. Try again on 1 November.

## How to download the RRB ALP admit card

1. Open the official website of **the RRB you applied to** (for example RRB Chennai, RRB Secunderabad, RRB Ajmer). All regional sites are linked from [rrb.indianrailways.gov.in](https://rrb.indianrailways.gov.in/).
2. In the CEN list, find **01/2026** and click **"E-Call Letters"**.
3. Log in with your registration number and your date of birth or password (the same details you used on rrbapply.gov.in).
4. Download the PDF and print it on A4 paper. Keep two copies, one in your bag and one at home.

The login page slows down badly on the evening before each exam day, when lakhs of people try at once. Download it in the morning if you can.

**SC/ST candidates who chose free travel:** your e-call letter is also your travel authority (sleeper class pass). Carry your original caste certificate during the journey.

## Details to check on your e-call letter

Check these today, not on exam morning:

- **Name, photo and date of birth**: they must match your ID proof
- **Exam date, shift and reporting time**
- **Full venue address**: open it on Google Maps and plan your route. Many centres are far from the city centre.
- **Category and PwBD status**, and scribe details if you asked for one

If something is wrong, contact the helpdesk number printed on your RRB's website as early as you can. Don't wait until exam day.

## Aadhaar rules: do this before you leave home

The RRBs check your identity with **Aadhaar-based biometrics** at the centre before you enter the hall. Two things to do now:

- Carry your **original Aadhaar** or a printout of **e-verified Aadhaar**.
- If you ever **locked your Aadhaar biometrics** (on the mAadhaar app or the UIDAI site), unlock them before exam day.

Honestly, do the unlock tonight. It takes two minutes, and a locked Aadhaar at the gate is a problem nobody there can fix for you.

## Documents to carry and exam-day rules

Carry:

- Printed **e-call letter** (a phone screen is not accepted)
- An **original photo ID**: Aadhaar or e-Aadhaar printout, voter ID, driving licence, PAN card or passport
- A recent **colour passport-size photo**, the same kind you uploaded with your form

Rules to remember:

- Reach the centre well before the reporting time on your card. Gates close before the exam starts and late entry is not allowed.
- **No phones, calculators, smartwatches, Bluetooth devices or any electronic gadget.** Even a switched-off phone found on you can cancel your candidature.
- You'll write a short **self-declaration paragraph** and sign it in front of the invigilator. Don't write it in advance, and don't sign in CAPITAL letters.
- No request to change your centre, date or shift will be accepted.

## RRB ALP CBT 1 exam pattern 2026

CBT 1 is a **screening test**. It decides who goes to CBT 2, and its marks are not counted in the final merit list.

- **Questions and marks:** 75 objective questions, 75 marks
- **Subjects:** Mathematics, Mental Ability (reasoning), General Science and General Awareness on current affairs
- **Time:** 60 minutes (80 minutes for candidates who are allowed a scribe)
- **Negative marking:** 1/3 mark cut for every wrong answer
- **Minimum marks to be eligible:** UR and EWS 40%, OBC (NCL) and SC 30%, ST 25%

60 minutes for 75 questions is less than a minute per question. That's the real challenge in this paper, not the difficulty. Don't get stuck on one long arithmetic sum; mark it and come back.

With 3–5 days left, my suggestion: take the RRB mock test once (the "Mock Test Link" for 01/2026 is on the RRB sites) to get used to the screen. Then do one full 60-minute practice paper a day and spend more time reviewing the wrong answers than attempting new ones.

## What comes next after CBT 1

- **Answer key:** RRBs usually put up the response sheets and provisional answer key, with an objection window, a few weeks after the exam. No date is announced yet. We'll update this page when it's out.
- **CBT 2:** shortlisted candidates (based on normalised CBT 1 marks) write CBT 2, which has a general Part A and a trade-based Part B.
- **CBAT:** ALP is a safety post, so there's also a **Computer Based Aptitude Test**, a psychological test of reaction speed, attention and memory. You need at least 42 in each test battery, and there's no relaxation for any category.
- **Then:** document verification and a medical exam. ALP needs the strict **A-1 medical standard**, including eyesight without glasses, so check this early if you're unsure.

**Fee refund:** if you appear in CBT 1, general candidates get ₹400 of the ₹500 fee back, and SC, ST, ExSM, PwBD, female, transgender, minority and EBC candidates get the full ₹250 back (minus bank charges). Even if you feel underprepared, go and write the exam.
