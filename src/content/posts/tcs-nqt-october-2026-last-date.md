**TCS NQT October 2026 applications close on 7 October 2026**, and the test is on **19 October 2026**. It's open to final and pre-final year students and to freshers who passed between 2022 and 2028. The cheapest version costs ₹599.

Before you pay, know one thing. **TCS NQT is a paid test, not a job application.** The score helps you apply to companies, but it doesn't give you a job or even an interview by itself. Read the "Is it worth it?" section before you register.

## TCS NQT October 2026 last date and test date

The official TCS iON page lists **7 October 2026** as the last date and **19 October 2026** as the test date for the October cycle.

TCS iON says applications close 10 to 15 days before each cycle's exam date, so there's no late window after 7 October. If you miss it, you'll have to wait for the next cycle. TCS iON says it runs NQT every 2 to 4 weeks, so the next one won't be far away.

## Who can apply (eligibility)

According to TCS iON, you can apply if you are:

- A UG, PG or diploma student in your **pre-final or final year**, or
- A graduate who **passed out between 2022 and 2028**, with a degree and specialisation, and
- A fresher with **less than 2 years of work experience**, and
- Between **17 and 30 years** old.

Any stream is allowed. You don't need to be from CSE or IT for the Cognitive or Non Tech versions.

## Fee: which TCS NQT variant to choose

There isn't just one NQT. TCS iON sells several versions, and many students pay for more than they need. Current prices from the official page, including taxes:

| Variant | What it tests | Price now (listed price) |
|---|---|---|
| NQT – Cognitive | Numerical, reasoning and verbal ability | ₹599 (₹800) |
| NQT – Non Tech | General quantitative, verbal and mental ability | ₹599 (₹800) |
| NQT – IT | Aptitude + advanced coding + psychometric test | ₹999 (₹1,598) |
| NQT – BFSI | Financial markets and services + basic aptitude | ₹999 (₹1,598) |
| Cognitive with Prep Pack | Cognitive test + 60+ hours of prep content | ₹999 (₹1,447) |
| IT with Prep Pack | IT test + cognitive and IT Foundation courses | ₹2,298 (₹3,694) |

These are offer prices. The page says "offer ends soon", so check the price on the day you pay.

My suggestion:

- **CSE, IT or anyone aiming for a software job:** take **NQT – IT** (₹999). TCS iON describes it as the test for jobs "from top corporates, including TCS".
- **Non-IT graduates (B.Com, BA, BSc, mechanical, civil and so on):** **Cognitive** (₹599) is enough.
- **Banking or finance roles:** BFSI.
- **Skip the prep packs** unless you really need structured content. There's plenty of free aptitude material online.

## What the test covers

The Cognitive test has 3 areas, according to TCS iON:

- **Verbal ability:** English grammar, usage and reading comprehension.
- **Reasoning ability:** word and number patterns, problem solving, figure and fact analysis, and visual-spatial reasoning.
- **Numerical ability:** number system, arithmetic, basic statistics and data interpretation.

The IT version adds advanced coding and a psychometric assessment on top of aptitude.

There's **no negative marking**, so attempt every question. All tests are in English only. The official page doesn't list section-wise time limits, so check your hall ticket for the exact pattern.

## How to apply for TCS NQT October 2026

1. Go to the [official TCS iON NQT page](https://www.tcsion.com/hub/national-qualifier-test/).
2. Pick your variant and click **Buy Now**.
3. Log in to the TCS iON Digital Learning Hub, or sign up as a new user.
4. Fill in your name, email, phone number and other details.
5. Click **Click to Pay/Activate Code** and pay online. If your college gave you a licence code, use **Activate Now** instead and enter the code. You don't pay in that case.
6. You'll get a confirmation on your email and phone. The hall ticket comes by email 2 days before the test.

**Ask your placement cell first.** Many colleges buy NQT licence codes in bulk at a lower price. If yours has, you may not need to pay at all.

## What the score really gets you (the honest part)

This is what the official FAQ says, in plain words:

- **No job guarantee.** TCS iON says the score "will only increase your chances". The final decision is the recruiter's.
- **No interviews arranged.** TCS iON says it "will not arrange for any job interviews". You apply to companies yourself.
- **Apply separately, even to TCS.** You must apply on each company's own job portal and mention your NQT registration number and score.
- **No pass or fail.** There's no cut-off. You just get a score, and each company decides what score it accepts.
- **No refund if you miss the test.** If you don't appear on your date, you lose the fee and get no retest.
- **Retakes are allowed.** There's no limit on attempts. Only your best score shows on the scorecard.

What you do get: a scorecard valid for 2 years, and access to the TCS iON job listing portal, which is only open to NQT takers. TCS iON says over 4,000 companies accept the score. Many of them are small or mid-sized firms, so don't expect only big MNCs.

## Is TCS NQT worth it?

**Apply if:**
- You're a fresher with **no campus placement** and want one score you can use across many companies.
- You want a **TCS job** and are taking the IT version seriously.
- Your college is giving you a **free licence code**. Then there's nothing to lose.

**Skip it (for now) if:**
- You already have a placement offer and aren't looking.
- You haven't practised aptitude at all. A weak score stays on your scorecard until you beat it, so prepare first and take the next cycle.
- You're spending on the ₹2,298 prep pack just because it looks "complete". You don't need it.

Honestly, for most freshers without a job, ₹599 to ₹999 is a fair price for a score that many companies recognise. Just go in knowing it's a ticket to apply, not a job offer.

## How to use the next 2 weeks

- **Days 1–5:** Numerical ability. Percentages, ratios, time and work, averages, and data interpretation tables.
- **Days 6–9:** Reasoning. Series, coding-decoding, puzzles and seating arrangements.
- **Days 10–12:** Verbal. Grammar, sentence correction and reading passages.
- **Days 13–14:** Take the free practice test that comes with your variant, under a timer.

If you're taking the IT version, solve 2 coding problems a day in C, C++, Java or Python, whichever you're fastest in.

Also looking at government jobs? The [SSC CHSL 2026 last date](/ssc-chsl-2026-last-date) is also 7 October, for 12th-pass candidates.
