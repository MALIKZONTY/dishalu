The **SSC GD result 2026 is out**. The Staff Selection Commission declared the Constable (GD) CBT result on **12 October 2026**, and **1,97,408 candidates** have been shortlisted for the physical test (PET/PST).

A total of 21,56,776 people sat the exam between 27 April and 30 May 2026. So roughly 1 in 11 made it to the next round. If your roll number is in List-I or List-II, start running practice this week. The physical test is where many people drop out.

## How to check the SSC GD result 2026 (merit list PDF)

SSC doesn't give a roll-number search box for this result. It puts out PDF lists, and you look for your roll number in them.

1. Open [ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in) and tap **Results**.
2. Pick **Constable (GD)** and the 2026 result dated 12 October.
3. Download the list for you:
   - **List-I:** female candidates shortlisted for PET/PST
   - **List-II:** male candidates shortlisted for PET/PST (other than SSF)
   - **List-III:** withheld results (60 candidates)
   - **List-IV:** debarred candidates (88)
4. The lists are in roll number order. Use Ctrl+F on a computer, or the search option in your phone's PDF reader, and type your full roll number.
5. Also download the **result write-up** (the notice). It has the cut-off for your state, category and area.

The male list is very large, more than 3,600 pages, so download it on Wi-Fi if you can.

SSC hasn't put up individual marks yet. The notice says the final answer key, response sheets and marks of qualified and non-qualified candidates will come "in due course", through your login on ssc.gov.in.

Where you see 48.00, 40.00 or 32.00, that's just the minimum qualifying mark: there were fewer candidates than posts, so everyone above the minimum got through. In Telangana, 295 UR men and 234 OBC men were shortlisted this way against Naxal-area posts.

### Minimum qualifying marks

Before any cut-off, you had to cross these (without NCC bonus):

- UR: 30%
- OBC and EWS: 25%
- SC, ST and ex-servicemen: 20%

Where a reserved seat had no candidates, SSC used these minimum marks as the cut-off, so some vacancies may still get filled at later stages.

## What comes next: PET/PST

![An empty red running track with white lane lines on a curve](/images/posts/ssc-gd-result-2026-2.webp)
*Photo: Rawpixel, [CC0](https://www.rawpixel.com/image/6017346/photo-image-public-domain-free-sport)*

The CAPFs will hold the Physical Efficiency Test (running) and Physical Standard Test (height, chest, weight). This time **CRPF is the nodal force**, so call letters will come from [rect.crpf.gov.in](https://rect.crpf.gov.in), not from SSC. **No PET date has been announced yet.**

After PET/PST, the stages are:

1. Detailed Medical Examination (DME), and Review Medical if you're declared unfit and appeal
2. Document verification
3. Final merit list and force allotment, based on your CBT score and the force preference you gave

The physical test is qualifying only. It doesn't add marks. But fail it and you're out, however high your CBT score.

For reference, in recent SSC GD cycles the race was **5 km in 24 minutes for men and 1.6 km in 8½ minutes for women** (different rules for Ladakh). Height and chest rules vary by category and region. Check the 2026 notice of examination for the exact standards that apply to you.

## Documents to keep ready

Don't wait for the call letter to start gathering papers. The usual list:

- 10th certificate (proof of date of birth)
- Caste or EWS certificate in the format given in the notice, if you claimed a reserved category
- Domicile certificate (posts are given state-wise)
- NCC certificate, if you claimed bonus marks. If you can't show it, the bonus goes.
- Border-area or Naxal-area district proof, if you were shortlisted against those posts
- Photo ID and a few passport-size photos

The NCC bonus was **8 marks for 'C', 4.8 for 'B' and 3.2 for 'A'** certificates (not given to ex-servicemen). It was added "provisionally", so a missing certificate at DV can push you below the cut-off.

## If your name isn't in the list

Check the withheld list (List-III) first. Your result may be on hold for a document issue, not because you failed.

If it isn't there either, wait for SSC to release marks. Compare your score with your state's cut-off in the tables above. If you were close, the next GD cycle is a realistic target.

Meanwhile, the [Coast Guard Navik and Yantrik recruitment](/indian-coast-guard-cgept-recruitment-2026) is open till 21 October, and it's another uniformed job for 10th pass, 12th pass (Maths and Physics) and diploma holders, if you were born between 2005 and 2009. If you've passed 12th, the [SSC CHSL 2026](/ssc-chsl-2026-last-date) Tier 1 exam is coming up too, though applications for it have closed.

## My take

If you're shortlisted, the next 4 to 6 weeks matter more than your CBT score. Run 4 to 5 days a week and time your 5 km or 1.6 km on a proper track, not on a road with traffic. Every cycle, a lot of people who score well fail the race because they started practising after the admit card came.

And keep checking rect.crpf.gov.in. Your PET call letter will be there, not on ssc.gov.in.
