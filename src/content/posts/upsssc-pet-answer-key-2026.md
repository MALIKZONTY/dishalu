## How to download the PET answer key from upsssc.gov.in

1. Open [upsssc.gov.in](https://upsssc.gov.in).
2. In the **Announcements** box on the homepage, find the lines that start "विज्ञापन संख्या-16-परीक्षा/2026, प्रारम्भिक अर्हता परीक्षा-2026 ... Provisional उत्तर कुंजी".
3. Click the line for **your exam date and shift** (प्रथम पाली = Shift 1, द्वितीय पाली = Shift 2).
4. The PDF opens in Google Drive. Use the download button at the top to save it.

Not sure of your shift? It's printed on your admit card and on the district slip from 12 October.

## Master set: why your question numbers may not match

This trips up a lot of people. The key is for the **master set** (मास्टर सेट), not for your booklet series. UPSSSC shuffles the order of questions across booklet series.

So don't compare question 1 in your booklet with question 1 in the key. Search for the question text instead. A quick way: open the PDF on your phone and use "Find" with 2–3 unusual words from the question.

If you didn't bring your question booklet home, note your answers from memory as soon as possible. Your estimate will be rough, but it still tells you where you stand.

## How to calculate your UPSSSC PET 2026 score

PET has 100 questions for 100 marks. Each right answer gets **1 mark** and each wrong answer loses **1/4 mark**, as per the PET 2026 advertisement.

**Score = Right answers − (Wrong answers ÷ 4)**

| Right | Wrong | Left blank | Your raw score |
|---|---|---|---|
| 80 | 12 | 8 | 80 − 3 = **77** |
| 70 | 16 | 14 | 70 − 4 = **66** |
| 60 | 24 | 16 | 60 − 6 = **54** |
| 50 | 30 | 20 | 50 − 7.5 = **42.5** |

Two things to keep in mind:

- **This is your raw score.** PET ran in 6 shifts, so UPSSSC will normalise scores before the result. If your shift's paper was tougher, your normalised score can come out a little higher than your raw score, and the other way round.
- **Count carefully.** Mark each question as right, wrong or blank in a notebook first, then add up. People often get it wrong by 3–4 marks when they count in their head.

### How to raise an objection

1. Open the link for your shift and log in with your **roll number, registration number, date of birth** and the captcha.
2. Read the instructions, tick the checkbox and click **CONTINUE**.
3. Click **"Proceed to raise question paper objection"** and pick the question.
4. Select the answer you think is right and write a short reason in the text box.
5. Upload your proof as a **PDF file under 5 MB**.
6. Click **PAY NOW** and pay **₹100 per objection** online.
7. Use **View/Print Objection Summary** to save a printout.

Three rules from the notice that catch people out:

- **You can submit only once.** Once you pay, you can't change or add to your objections. So list all your objections first, then submit them together.
- **Question numbers follow the provisional (master set) key,** not your booklet. Find the question by its text.
- **No objections by post or letter.** Only the online link counts.

### Is it worth ₹100?

Only if you're sure and have solid proof. An NCERT book page, a government website or an official report works. A coaching app screenshot doesn't. One mark can matter in a normalised score, but paying for five weak objections is just ₹500 gone.

And ignore anyone who asks for money to "fix" your marks. Only UPSSSC can change the key.

## What comes after the provisional answer key

- **Final answer key:** after the objections that closed on 12 November are reviewed. No date yet.
- **Result and score card:** no date announced yet. Your score card will show your normalised score.
- **Score validity:** 3 years from the result date, as per the PET 2026 advertisement. If you hold more than one valid PET score, your best one is used when you apply for a mains exam.
- **Mains exams:** PET is only the gateway. UPSSSC then advertises separate mains exams for Group C posts. Right now, the open mains advertisements (such as Combined Junior Engineer Mechanical, apply till 25 November) shortlist from **PET 2025** scores. PET 2026 scores will be used for advertisements after the result.

## My take

A raw score above 70 is a comfortable place to be. That's my reading from past years, not an official cut-off, because PET has no pass mark. Each mains exam sets its own cut-off, so a higher score simply opens more doors.

If you're below 50, don't sit and wait for the result. Start preparing for central exams you can take in the meantime. The [RRB NTPC Undergraduate 2026](/rrb-ntpc-undergraduate-recruitment-2026) form is open till 13 November, and its reasoning and general awareness sections overlap with PET.
