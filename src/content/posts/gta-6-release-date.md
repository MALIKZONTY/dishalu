The **GTA 6 release date is Thursday, 19 November 2026**, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar confirmed it again with the launch trailer on 15 October. That's exactly 30 days from today.

## Standard vs Ultimate Edition: which to buy?

| Edition | What you get |
|---|---|
| Standard | Base game + Vintage Vice City Pack (pre-order bonus) |
| Ultimate Edition | Base game + Ultimate Edition Upgrade + Vintage Vice City Pack + 1 month of GTA+ + 7-day preload |

Rockstar says the Ultimate Edition adds premium vehicles, weapons, apparel and extra content across Jason and Lucia's story. Note that the free GTA+ month **auto-renews**, so turn off auto-renewal if you don't want to pay after the month.

**Which one should you buy?** For most people, the Standard Edition is enough. The extra $20 (or £20, or ₹1,500) gets you in-game items, not a different story. Honestly, wait for reviews before paying for Ultimate.

## How to preload GTA 6

1. Pre-order the digital version on the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store (or the Rockstar Store).
2. From **12 November**, the download starts. Turn on automatic downloads on your console so it begins on its own.
3. Keep enough free storage. Rockstar hasn't shared the file size yet, but a game this big will need a lot of space, so clear some early.
4. On 19 November, the game unlocks and you can play once it's fully installed.

If you buy the **physical box**, remember it only has a **download code**, not a disc. You'll still need to download the full game. On a slow or capped internet connection, start early.

## Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Not at launch. Rockstar has only announced PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Take-Two (Rockstar's parent company) hasn't given any PC date either.

History gives a rough idea, though. GTA V came to PC about 19 months after its console launch, and GTA IV about 8 months later. So a PC version in 2027 or later is possible, but it's not announced. Ignore any "GTA 6 PC download" link you see. Those are scams or malware.

## GTA 6 delays: how we got to 19 November

| Date | What Rockstar said |
|---|---|
| December 2023 | Trailer 1 released, launch planned for 2025 |
| May 2025 | Delayed to 26 May 2026, Trailer 2 released |
| 6 November 2025 | Delayed again to 19 November 2026 |
| 25 June 2026 | Pre-orders opened worldwide |
| 15 October 2026 | Launch trailer, 19 November date kept |

In its November 2025 note, Rockstar apologised for the longer wait and said the extra months were needed to finish the game with the polish players expect.

## What GTA 6 is about

GTA 6 is set in **Vice City, USA**, inside a fictional state called Leonida (based on Florida). You play as **Jason and Lucia**, two partners who get pulled into a criminal conspiracy after a simple job goes wrong. It's Rockstar's first new GTA story since GTA V in 2013.

The game is rated for mature players. It has strong violence, drugs, nudity and language, so it's not meant for kids.
