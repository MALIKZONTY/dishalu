From abroad, **Black Doves Season 2** is back with Keira Knightley, and Prime Video has **The Greatest**, the first authorised series on Muhammad Ali. Everything below is spoiler-free.

## Indian OTT releases this weekend

### I'm Game (Malayalam, JioHotstar)

Dulquer Salmaan plays Dan John, a very rich young man who lives for money and thrills. One accident turns his life upside down and drags him into a dangerous world of gambling and betting. JioHotstar calls it a fantasy-action thriller.

- **Cast:** Dulquer Salmaan, Kayadu Lohar, Antony Varghese, Mysskin, Kathir, Vinay Forrt
- **Director:** Nahas Hidhayath (*RDX*)
- **Audio:** Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Hindi
- **Producer:** Wayfarer Films, Dulquer's own banner

In theatres it made around ₹48 crore worldwide, as reported, which was below what a film this size needed. Honestly, that's often the perfect OTT pick: a large-scale action film you can watch at home with no pressure. If you liked *RDX*'s fight scenes, this one is for you.

### Haiwaan (Hindi, SonyLIV)

Saif Ali Khan plays a visually impaired man who works in a Mumbai building, and who gets pulled into the hunt for a dangerous killer. Akshay Kumar plays the other lead. It's a remake of the Malayalam film *Oppam*, directed by the same man, Priyadarshan.

- **Cast:** Akshay Kumar, Saif Ali Khan
- **Director:** Priyadarshan
- **In theatres:** 11 September 2026

Akshay and Saif are sharing the screen after many years, and Priyadarshan has remade his own film. If you've already seen *Oppam*, you know the story. If you haven't, it's the safest thriller pick of the weekend.

### Meesaya Murukku 2 (Tamil, SonyLIV)

Hiphop Tamizha Adhi writes, directs, composes and stars in this sequel, playing three roles. It follows three generations of one family trying to get respect for gaana, the street music of North Chennai, in the mainstream music industry.

- **Cast:** Hiphop Tamizha Adhi, Ketika Sharma
- **Audio:** Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada and Hindi (as reported)
- **In theatres:** 25 September 2026

It's light, full of music, and easy to watch with family over Diwali.

### Dilwali Diwali (Hindi, Netflix)

Ansh Sikka (Mihir Ahuja) comes home after years of working abroad, just in time for Diwali. His family has its own idea of what happiness and "home" mean, and that's where the fun and the fights start.

- **Cast:** Mihir Ahuja, Manoj Pahwa, Neelu Kohli, Aurra Bhatnagar
- **Made by:** the team behind *Greater Kalesh* and *Musafir Cafe* on Netflix (as reported)

If you liked *Greater Kalesh*, expect the same small-family, festive mood. Manoj Pahwa playing a Delhi dad is reason enough for me.

### Blind Game (Hindi, ZEE5)

A suspended police officer, Sikander (Arjun Rampal), starts digging into some missing money and a group of young people who are hiding something. It's a Hindi remake of the Malayalam thriller *7th Day*, written and directed by the late Sangeeth Sivan.

- **Cast:** Arjun Rampal, Priyamani
- **Where:** ZEE5's **Free 5** line-up, so no paid plan needed (with ads)

### Nooru Kodi Vaanavil (Tamil, ZEE5)

A young couple's relationship gets tested by family expectations and misunderstandings. It's a quiet romantic drama from director Sasi, who made *Pichaikkaran*.

- **Cast:** Harish Kalyan, Siddhi Idnani
- **Streaming:** 6 November on ZEE5 (as reported)

## International OTT releases this weekend

### Black Doves Season 2 (English, Netflix)

Keira Knightley's spy Helen is still passing secrets to the Black Doves, but her husband is now set to become Prime Minister, and the line she walks gets thinner. Ben Whishaw is back as Sam, and Neve Campbell and Ambika Mod join. All 6 episodes are out on **5 November**.

If you haven't seen it, Season 1 is also only 6 episodes, so you can finish both over the long weekend.

### The Greatest (English, Prime Video)

Prime Video calls it the first authorised scripted series on Muhammad Ali. Newcomer Jaalen Best plays Cassius Clay, the young man who becomes Ali, with Omari Hardwick as his father and Michael Ealy as Malcolm X. Ali's wife Lonnie Ali is an executive producer. All **8 episodes** came out on 4 November.

Prime Video's India listing shows English plus Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam audio (as reported).

### Also new

- **Possible Love (Netflix, 6 Nov):** a Korean film about two couples whose ambitions and unspoken feelings get in the way (as reported).
- **Campus Drivers (Prime Video, 6 Nov):** a 6-episode romantic series with Carmen Kassovitz (as reported).
- **Taken: Natalee Holloway (Netflix, out 4 Nov):** a 3-part true-crime documentary on the 2005 Aruba disappearance.
