The big one, the **MacBook Pro with a touchscreen OLED (PROLED XDR) display**, starts at ₹3,49,900 and reaches buyers from **9 November**. All prices below are from Apple India's own press releases.

## iPad mini with A20 Pro: what's new

This is the biggest change to the iPad mini in years, and Apple's own press release calls it the "biggest update ever".

- **8.5-inch OLED display.** The screen is bigger than before (up from 8.3 inches) because the bezels are 24% thinner, but the iPad is the same size overall. Peak brightness is 1,200 nits, double the last model.
- **A20 Pro chip,** built on a 2-nanometre process. Apple claims up to 60% faster multithreaded CPU performance, 2.7x faster graphics and 4.5x faster AI performance than the previous iPad mini.
- **5.4 mm thin,** in two colours: midnight and starlight.
- **IP67 water and dust resistance** (up to 1 metre for 30 minutes).
- **Storage:** 128GB, 256GB or 512GB.
- **Apple Pencil:** works with Apple Pencil Pro (₹11,900) and Apple Pencil USB-C (₹7,900).
- Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6, and Apple's C2 modem in the cellular model.

The catch is the price. At ₹82,900, the iPad mini now costs more than many full-size tablets. Add a Pencil Pro and a Smart Folio (₹8,500) and you're past ₹1 lakh.

## 14-inch MacBook Pro with M6

This is the "regular" MacBook Pro, with the same Mini-LED screen and design as before but a new chip.

- **M6 chip:** 12-core CPU and 12-core GPU (2 more cores each than M5). Apple claims up to 30% faster graphics and 25% faster AI work than M5.
- **16GB unified memory** as standard, configurable up to 32GB.
- **Liquid Retina XDR display** (Mini-LED, up to 1,600 nits HDR). Nano-texture glass is an option.
- Up to **24 hours** of battery life.
- Ports: 3 Thunderbolt 4, HDMI, SDXC card slot, MagSafe 3, headphone jack.
- Colours: space black and silver.

Note: no touchscreen here. Touch is only on the PROLED XDR models.

## MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display (touchscreen)

This is the headline launch and the first Mac with a touchscreen.

- **Tandem OLED display** with built-in touch, up to 2,000 nits, 120Hz.
- **Dynamic Island** comes to the Mac.
- **M5 Pro or M5 Max** chips (not M6), up to 128GB memory and 16TB storage.
- Thinner and lighter: Apple says the 14-inch model weighs 3 pounds (about 1.36 kg) and the 16-inch 4 pounds (about 1.81 kg).
- Up to 24 hours of battery, 50% charge in 30 minutes.
- 3 Thunderbolt 5 ports, HDMI, SDXC, MagSafe, headphone jack.

At ₹3,49,900 to ₹5,89,900, this is for professionals who edit video, photos or 3D for a living. For everyone else, it's a showpiece.

## iMac with M6

- **24-inch 4.5K Retina display.**
- **M6 chip** with up to 12-core CPU and 12-core GPU.
- Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6 and 2.5 Gigabit Ethernet.
- Seven colours: blue, green, pink, yellow, purple, orange and silver.

## Which one should you buy?

Honestly, most students and regular users in India don't need any of these.

- **College students:** If you need a Mac for coding or design work, the MacBook Air or an older M-series MacBook Pro on a sale will do the job for far less. The M6 MacBook Pro is worth it only if you do heavy video editing or run big local AI models.
- **Note-taking and reading:** The iPad mini is lovely, but ₹76,900 (education) is a lot for a small tablet. A regular iPad does the same note-taking work for much less.
- **Families:** The iMac is a good all-in-one for a home study desk, but ₹1,74,900 is steep for browsing and video calls.
- **Professional editors and creators:** This is where the PROLED XDR MacBook Pro makes sense. Wait for early reviews of the touch feature before you spend over ₹3.5 lakh.

**Tip:** Always check the education price first if you're a student or teacher, and compare it with bank card offers at Apple Authorised Resellers. Apple's own offers change often, so check apple.com/in on the day you buy.

## What's still not clear

- **Card offers and cashback:** Apple India's MacBook Pro page currently mentions no-cost EMI and instant cashback on some cards. These offers change, so check the exact terms on apple.com/in before you pay.
- **Education price for the cellular iPad mini:** Apple's press release gives the education price only for the Wi-Fi model.
- **Real-world battery life and touch experience:** Apple's figures are lab claims. Wait for independent reviews.

## My take

The OLED iPad mini is the most interesting launch for regular buyers, and also the most overpriced for its size. If you're a student, don't stretch for any of these. Wait for festive or year-end offers, or look at last year's models, which usually get cheaper once new ones arrive.
