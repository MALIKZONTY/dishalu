## Eligibility

### Qualification and experience

Experience is the real filter. It must be **after** your qualification and in the exact area the advertisement names; anything else won't count.

| Post | Qualification | Experience after qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Deputy General Manager (Mech/Elec/Civil) | Full-time or part-time B.E./B.Tech in the branch | 19 years in the power sector, with 5 years in thermal project work |
| Executive Engineer (Mech/Elec/Civil/C&I/Chemical) | B.E./B.Tech in the branch | 5 years in the named area (thermal plant of 200 MW+ units, mines, HEMM, or materials management for Projects posts) |
| Executive Engineer (Mining) | Mining Engineering degree + **First Class Mine Manager Certificate** (Coal Mine Regulations, 2017) | 5 years in an opencast or underground mine |
| Executive Engineer (Environmental) | Environmental Engg. degree, or any engineering degree with a PG degree/diploma in environmental engineering or science | 5 years in a mine or pollution control board work |

Two more rules catch people out:

- **Private sector applicants must already earn a minimum CTC:** at least 50% of the E2/E3/E4 CTC, 60% of the E6 CTC, or 70% of the E7 CTC. You'll upload pay slips, Form-16 and ITRs as proof.
- **PSU and government employees** need at least 2 years in the immediate lower pay scale (1 year for Deputy Manager HR and Legal), and an NOC at document verification.

## Selection process

This is where the "CBT 80 + interview 20" you may have read gets confused.

- **Deputy Manager (Legal) only:** an **80-mark computer based test** on professional knowledge, then a **20-mark interview**. You need 50% in the CBT. Shortlisting for interview is 1:6, and the final list is on the total out of 100.
- **All other posts:** selection is by **personal interview** only. If a post gets more than 6 applications per seat, NLC can hold a screening CBT and call the top candidates in a 1:6 ratio. Minimum interview marks are 50% for UR/EWS and 40% for SC, ST, OBC (NCL) and PwBD on reserved seats.

Then comes a medical test and a 1-year probation, and you can be posted to any NLC unit, subsidiary or joint venture. The advertisement gives no syllabus or CBT date; shortlisted candidates are told by email, SMS and the NLC website.

## How to apply for NLC India recruitment 2026

1. Go to [nlcindia.in](https://www.nlcindia.in/website/en/careers/jobs/currentopenings.html), open Careers → Current Openings and find **Advt. No. 11/2026**.
2. Read the full advertisement. Match your experience area line by line with column (e) for your post.
3. Register from **20 November 2026, 10 AM** with an active mobile number and personal email. All updates come by SMS and email.
4. Fill in the form and upload: photo (taken after 1 June 2026, JPEG up to 50 KB), signature, date of birth proof, Aadhaar, degree and mark sheets, experience certificates, CTC proof (private sector), and category certificate if any.
5. Pay the fee through SBI e-Collect by **19 December 2026, 11:45 PM**.
6. Submit the form (by 22 December, 5 PM, if you registered and paid in time) and keep the printout for document verification.

A few practical points from the advertisement:

- **One post per registration.** If you apply for two posts, use a different email ID and pay separately for each.
- Put all degree and mark sheet copies **into one PDF in date order**. If you upload a second PDF, it overwrites the first.
- Experience letters must show the plant or mine name, period, duties and details like unit capacity (MW) or mine type. A vague letter can get you rejected.
- Queries go to **help.recruitment@nlcindia.in**.

## Should you apply?

**If you're a mechanical or electrical engineer with 5+ years in an opencast mine or on heavy earth moving machinery, yes.** 60 Executive Engineer posts in Mines alone is a big number for a lateral PSU drive, and E4 pay at about ₹20 lakh CTC is a solid step up for most private sector engineers. Selection is mainly by interview, so your documents and your actual site experience matter more than exam prep.

Mining engineers: you need the First Class Mine Manager Certificate under the Coal Mine Regulations. Without it, don't apply.

The tricky part for private sector people is the CTC rule. If you earn less than half the E4 CTC (about ₹10 lakh), you're not eligible, even with the right experience.

If you're a fresher or have only 1 to 2 years of engineering experience, this isn't for you.
