The National Testing Agency (NTA) is taking applications for **CSIR NET December 2026** (Joint CSIR-UGC NET) on csirnet.nta.nic.in. The **last date is 5 November 2026**, and the exam is on **19, 22 and 27 December 2026**. Miss the date and you can still apply till 9 November, but with ₹500 extra.

If you have (or are finishing) an MSc in chemistry, physics, maths, life sciences or earth sciences, this is the exam for JRF, Assistant Professor eligibility and PhD admission. Here's everything from the official information bulletin.

## CSIR NET December 2026: key details

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | National Testing Agency (NTA) for CSIR and UGC |
| Apply online | 7 October to 5 November 2026 (till 11:50 PM) |
| Late application (₹500 extra) | 6 to 9 November 2026 |
| Correction window | 10 to 12 November 2026 |
| Exam dates | 19, 22 and 27 December 2026 |
| Shifts | 9 AM–12 noon and 3 PM–6 PM |
| Mode | Computer Based Test (CBT), 3 hours, MCQs |
| Medium | English and Hindi |
| Subjects | Chemical, Earth/Atmospheric/Ocean/Planetary, Life Sciences and Biotech, Mathematical, Physical Sciences |
| Fee | ₹1,150 (General), ₹600 (EWS/OBC-NCL), ₹325 (SC/ST/PwD/third gender) |
| JRF stipend | ₹37,000 per month (₹42,000 as SRF) |
| City slip, admit card, result | To be announced |
| Official site | [csirnet.nta.nic.in](https://csirnet.nta.nic.in/) |

The bulletin says the dates are tentative. NTA moved the [UGC NET December 2026](/ugc-net-december-2026-registration) exam dates once already this cycle, so keep checking the site.

## What you qualify for after CSIR NET

The result puts you in one of three categories. Which one you land in depends on your marks and on the cut-offs set by the results committee.

| Qualified for | JRF | Assistant Professor | PhD admission |
|---|---|---|---|
| JRF and Assistant Professor | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Assistant Professor and PhD | No | Yes | Yes |
| PhD admission only | No | No | Yes |

For the second and third categories, your NET marks count for 70% at PhD admission and the university's interview counts for 30%. These marks stay valid for PhD admission for one year. The Assistant Professor eligibility certificate has no expiry.

## Eligibility for CSIR NET December 2026

### Educational qualification

- **Master's degree** (or equivalent) with at least **55%** marks for General and EWS, or **50%** for OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwD and third gender. No rounding off: 54.9% is not 55%.
- **Final-year students can apply.** If you're in your Master's or waiting for your result, you're admitted provisionally. You must pass with the required percentage within two years of the CSIR NET result (one year if you qualify for PhD only).
- **Four-year bachelor's degree holders** can apply with at least **75%** marks (5% relaxation for reserved categories). They can get JRF and PhD admission, but **not** Assistant Professor eligibility. Students in the last year of a 4-year degree can also apply provisionally.
- With a 4-year degree, you can choose any CSIR NET subject you want to do a PhD in, even if your degree was in a different discipline.

### Age limit

| Category | Upper age limit |
|---|---|
| JRF | 30 years on 1 December 2026 |
| JRF: OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwD, third gender, women | Up to 5 years relaxation (35) |
| JRF: research experience | Relaxation equal to research period, max 5 years |
| Assistant Professor | No upper age limit |
| PhD admission only | No upper age limit |

So if you're above 30 (or 35 with relaxation), you can still write the exam. You just won't be considered for JRF.

## JRF stipend and fellowship

According to the bulletin, a JRF through CSIR NET gets **₹37,000 per month** for the first two years, plus a contingency grant of **₹20,000 a year**. After two years, if you're registered for a PhD and a committee approves your progress, the fellowship moves to SRF at **₹42,000 per month**.

The number of fellowships per subject is limited and changes every cycle. For Life Sciences, some fellowships are funded by the Department of Biotechnology (DBT-JRF) through this same exam.

## CSIR NET exam pattern 2026

One 3-hour CBT paper with three parts and no break. **Part A** (general aptitude: reasoning, graphs, numerical ability, puzzles) is common to all subjects. **Part B** has standard subject MCQs. **Part C** has harder, analytical questions. Every subject is out of **200 marks**.

| Subject | Questions (A+B+C) | You attempt | Marks per correct (A/B/C) | Negative marking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chemical Sciences | 20+40+60 = 120 | 15+35+25 | 2 / 2 / 4 | 25% in all parts |
| Earth Sciences | 20+50+80 = 150 | 15+35+25 | 2 / 2 / 4 | 25% in A, B; 33% in C |
| Life Sciences | 20+50+75 = 145 | 15+35+25 | 2 / 2 / 4 | 25% in all parts |
| Mathematical Sciences | 20+40+60 = 120 | 15+25+20 | 2 / 3 / 4.75 | 25% in A, B; none in C |
| Physical Sciences | 20+25+30 = 75 | 15+20+20 | 2 / 3.5 / 5 | 25% in all parts |

**Don't answer more than the limit.** If you attempt more questions than allowed in a part, only the first ones you answered (up to the limit) are checked. Extra answers don't help, and a wrong one among the first can cost you.

In Maths Part C, a question can have more than one correct option, and you get marks only if you mark **all** of them. There's no negative marking there, so attempt what you can.

## How to apply for CSIR NET December 2026

1. Go to [csirnet.nta.nic.in](https://csirnet.nta.nic.in/) and click "Online Application for Joint CSIR UGC-NET December 2026". You can also sign in through MeriPehchaan (DigiLocker/Aadhaar single sign-on).
2. Register with your own email ID and mobile number. Note down the application number and password.
3. Fill in personal details, subject, education details and exam city choices.
4. Take the live photo (webcam or phone via QR code), and upload a passport photo (10–200 KB, JPG, white background, ears visible, no mask) and signature (10–50 KB, JPG).
5. Upload a category certificate or Result Awaited Attestation Form if it applies to you (PDF, 50–300 KB).
6. Pay the fee by UPI, card or net banking before 5 November, 11:50 PM.
7. Download and save the confirmation page.

If no confirmation page appears after payment, check with your bank first. If the money was deducted, contact NTA so they can regenerate the page. Don't keep paying again.

Fill only one form. NTA keeps only the first form for a subject, and appearing under two application numbers is treated as unfair means.

**Helpline:** 011-40759000 / 011-69227700, csirnet@nta.ac.in

## Should you apply?

If you're an MSc student or graduate in one of the five subjects, yes, even if you feel underprepared. ₹1,150 is small, and many people clear JRF on their second or third try. The first attempt teaches you the exam's pace and the Part C traps.

Honestly, with about 7 weeks left, focus on **Part A** first. It's 30 marks of aptitude that most science students ignore, and it's the easiest way to push your score past the cut-off. Then do the last 5 years' Part B papers for your subject.

If you're above 35, you can't get JRF, but Assistant Professor eligibility is still worth it if you want to teach.

Apply before 5 November to save the ₹500 late fee. Then watch for the city slip, which NTA says it will announce later.
