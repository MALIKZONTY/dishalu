### Qualification and experience: Grade A General stream

This is the post with 53 of the 68 seats, so here's the rule in plain words.

**Qualification (any one):**
- Graduation in Commerce, Economics, Maths, Statistics, Business Administration or Engineering with **60% marks** (50% for SC/ST/PwBD)
- CS, CMA (ICMAI) or CFA
- CA
- Full-time 2-year MBA/PGDM in any discipline

**Experience (any one):**
- **2 years** as an officer in lending/credit, risk, treasury, audit/compliance, or VC/PE/debt investment at SIDBI, NABARD, EXIM Bank, NHB, NaBFID, a scheduled commercial bank or an RBI-regulated NBFC, fintech or MFI. Experience only in personal, education, vehicle or home loans **does not count**.
- 2 years of core work in a SEBI-registered investment banking or AIF entity, an RBI-registered ARC, a bank subsidiary in MSME lending, or a DPIIT-recognised BFSI startup. Consultancy work also counts, but only in firms at least 10 years old and only on government or government-institution projects.
- **3 years** as a clerk in the same areas at RBI, SEBI, an all-India financial institution, a bank or a regulated NBFC.

**No experience needed if you have a CA or a full-time 2-year MBA/PGDM.** Final-year MBA/PGDM students can also apply, but must show the final marksheet and degree certificate at joining.

Experience and qualification are counted as on **11 December 2026**.

## SIDBI Grade A selection process and exam pattern

There are three phases. **Phase I and Phase II are on the same day, in one sitting.** SIDBI checks your Phase II papers only if you clear Phase I. Up to 25 candidates per vacancy (1:25) can qualify for Phase II evaluation.

**Phase I (Paper I): objective, 60 marks, 60 minutes**

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reasoning Aptitude | 15 | 15 | 15 min |
| English Language | 15 | 15 | 15 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 15 | 15 | 15 min |
| General Awareness (banking, financial sector, economy, computers) | 15 | 15 | 15 min |
| **Total** | **60** | **60** | **60 min** |

**Phase II: 200 marks**

| Paper | Type | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper II: English (essay, précis, comprehension, business letter) | Descriptive, typed | 75 | 60 min |
| Paper III: Stream subject | Objective (50 questions) | 75 | 60 min |
| Paper III: Stream subject | Descriptive, typed (attempt 4 of 10) | 50 | 60 min |

For the General stream, Paper III covers MSME policy and laws, finance and management, MSME loans (due diligence, ratios, term loan and working capital), NPAs and IRAC norms, NBFC financing, credit risk, investment banking, AIFs and ARCs.

**Negative marking:** 1/4 of a question's marks is cut for every wrong objective answer, in both phases.

**Phase III: interview of 100 marks**, in Hindi or English, tentatively at Chennai, Lucknow, Mumbai and New Delhi. Final merit uses Phase II plus interview marks.

Phase I/II exam centres are spread across India, including Hyderabad, Warangal, Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada/Guntur, Chennai and Bengaluru.

## How to apply for SIDBI Grade A and B 2026

1. Go to [sidbi.in → Careers](https://www.sidbi.in/en/careers) and open the Grade A and B 2026 notice. Click **Apply Online**.
2. Click **New Registration**, enter your name, phone and email. Note the provisional registration number and password.
3. Fill in your education and experience details exactly as they appear on your certificates. Give marks as a percentage, worked out over all semesters.
4. Upload your photo, signature (not in capital letters), left thumb impression and the **handwritten declaration in English**. The declaration text is given in the notification.
5. Choose your exam centre and interview centre. You can't change them later.
6. Preview the whole form, then click **Complete Registration**. You can't edit anything after this.
7. Pay the fee. Wait for the e-receipt. Don't press back or refresh during payment.
8. Print the application form and the e-receipt.

If something goes wrong, raise a complaint at [cgrs.ibps.in](http://cgrs.ibps.in/) and write "SIDBI Officer in Grade 'A' & 'B' (General and Specialist Stream)" in the subject.

**Keep your experience proof ready now.** At the interview stage, SIDBI asks for experience certificates with exact dates and the nature of your work. If you can't prove relevant lending, risk or treasury work, your candidature is cancelled, even after you clear the exam.

## Should you apply?

**Yes, if you're a CA or hold a full-time MBA/PGDM.** You skip the experience rule, and very few posts in banking pay this much at entry level. With only 53 General seats across India, competition will be tough, but SIDBI's experience rule keeps lakhs of fresh graduates out, so the applicant pool is far smaller than for [IBPS PO](/ibps-po-mains-result-2026).

**Yes, if you're a bank officer with 2+ years in credit or risk.** This is a clear step up in pay and in the kind of work you do. A retail-only loan profile (home, car, personal loans) does not count, so check your role honestly before paying ₹1,100.

**Skip it if you're a fresh B.Com or B.Tech graduate without CA or MBA.** You simply aren't eligible this time, however well you prepare. Look at openings like [NICL AO](/nicl-ao-recruitment-2026) or [Bank of Baroda](/bank-of-baroda-recruitment-2026) instead.

My honest take on preparation: the 27 December date leaves about 5 weeks. Phase I is only 15 questions per section, so it's a speed test you can pass with regular bank-exam practice. The real work is Phase II. Practise **typing** essays and letters on a computer (most people lose marks here because they've only ever written by hand), and revise MSME finance: working capital assessment, NPA norms and SIDBI's own schemes.
