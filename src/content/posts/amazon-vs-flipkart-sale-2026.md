## Amazon Great Indian Festival 2026 dates and early access

Amazon has confirmed that the Great Indian Festival starts on **8 October 2026**. Its newsroom also says "Early Deals are already live across categories", so some prices have already dropped.

Some websites say Prime members get early access from 7 October. **Amazon's own announcement doesn't say this.** What Amazon does say is that Prime members get "up to 10% extra" on featured categories. If a Prime early access window opens, it will show in the Amazon app.

## Flipkart Big Billion Days 2026 dates and early access

The main sale opens for everyone on **9 October 2026**. Early access starts at **midnight on 8 October**, a full day before.

Flipkart's sale page lists Flipkart Plus members, VIP members and Flipkart credit card holders for early access. Some news reports also mention Flipkart Black members. The easiest check: open the Flipkart app on 7 October and see whether your account shows an early access banner.

A few blogs say BBD runs until 18 October. Flipkart hasn't confirmed this, so don't plan around it.

## Amazon and Flipkart sale 2026 bank offers

This is the part that decides your final price. The same phone can cost ₹2,000 to ₹5,000 less on one site just because of your card.

| Offer | Amazon | Flipkart |
|---|---|---|
| Main bank card | SBI credit, debit and credit card EMI: **10% instant** | Axis Bank and ICICI Bank: **up to 10%** |
| Co-branded card | Amazon Pay ICICI credit card: 5% unlimited cashback (Prime), 3% (non-Prime) | Flipkart credit card: early access |
| Pay Later | Amazon Pay Later: 10% cashback, 3 interest-free EMIs on carts of ₹1,500+ | Flipkart Pay Later: No Cost EMI plus flat ₹500 off |
| EMI | No Cost EMI on select products | Flipkart EMI: No Cost EMI plus flat ₹1,000 off |
| UPI | Amazon Pay cashback on select purchases | Supermoney: 10% off first transaction; 5% off on digital gift card via UPI |

**Every bank offer has a maximum limit**, like "up to ₹1,500" on a single order. A 10% offer on a ₹90,000 laptop is not ₹9,000 off. Open "Offers" on the product page and read the cap before you count on it.

If you pay by UPI, there's no extra charge to you. The new [UPI fee from 15 October](/upi-charges-above-2000-from-october-15) is paid by the seller's side, not the buyer.

## Does the price really drop in these sales?

Yes, for some things. Not for everything.

**Usually real cuts:** earbuds and headphones, TVs, and laptops or phones that launched 6 to 18 months ago. The Sennheiser, Bose and Galaxy Book4 above are good examples.

**Usually no cut:** phones launched in the last 1 to 3 months. The iQOO Z11 and Galaxy Z Fold8 are at their launch prices. The only saving is the bank offer, which you'd often get in a normal week too.

**Watch out for raised prices.** The Galaxy M47 launched at ₹25,999, went up to ₹32,999, and is now "on sale" at ₹30,999. It's cheaper than last week, but still ₹5,000 more than at launch. Many phone and laptop prices have gone up in 2026 because memory chips cost more, so the old launch price may not come back.

Honestly, if a new phone shows no real cut and you don't have the right bank card, there's no rush. Its price may drop later anyway.

## 6 things to do before you buy

1. **Note today's price** of what you want, so you can tell if the sale price is really lower.
2. **Check the exact variant.** "From ₹79,999" usually means the lowest storage or RAM option.
3. **Add your card and address now**, so checkout is fast when the deal goes live.
4. **Read the bank offer cap** and whether it works on EMI, debit card or only credit card.
5. **Check the return policy.** Many phones and laptops only get replacement for a defect, not a refund if you change your mind.
6. **Buy only from the main seller** with good ratings, and check the warranty is Indian (brand warranty, not "seller warranty").
