The **IBPS PO Mains result 2026 is out**. IBPS put up the "Result Status of Online Main Examination for CRP-PO/MTs-XVI" on [ibps.in](https://www.ibps.in/) on **20 November 2026**. You log in to see whether you've qualified for the next round.

If you have, two steps are left: a **personality test** and then an **interview**, both tentatively in **November/December 2026**. Your final rank is **80% Mains, 20% interview**, so the Mains is still most of your score.

| What | Details |

The schedule above is from the official CRP PO/MT-XVI notification. Exact personality test and interview dates aren't out yet.

## How to check the IBPS PO Mains result 2026

1. Open [ibps.in](https://www.ibps.in/).
2. In the latest updates list, find **CRP-PO/MTs-XVI, posted on 20 November 2026**: "Result Status of Online Main Examination".
3. Click it. It opens the result login on ibpsreg.ibps.in.
4. Enter your **registration number or roll number** and your **password or date of birth**, as the login page asks.
5. Fill in the captcha and log in. Your qualifying status shows on screen.
6. Take a screenshot or save it as PDF. You'll want it later.

Lost your registration number? Search your email for the message IBPS sent in July 2026 when you registered. The "forgot registration number" option on the login page also works.

Some coaching sites report the result link stays open only until **27 November 2026**. IBPS hasn't said this on the homepage, so check yours now rather than wait.

## Your Mains score isn't shown yet. Here's why

People often get confused here. IBPS says in the notification that **Mains scores are not shared with shortlisted candidates until the interview process is over**. So if you qualified, you'll only see "qualified" for now, not your marks.

Candidates who didn't qualify usually get their scorecard and the cut-off a little later on ibps.in.

Mains marks also decide your final rank, together with the interview. The Mains was out of **225** (200 objective + 25 descriptive), with a **0.25 negative mark** for each wrong answer in the objective tests.

## What comes next: personality test and interview

![Bank of India main branch in Mumbai, an old stone building with arched windows](/images/posts/ibps-po-mains-result-2026-2.webp)

**1. Personality test (self-report).** This is a new round in the 2026 cycle. You answer questions about yourself. It is **non-qualifying**, so there's no pass mark. But you **must take it**, or you can't sit the interview. Your personality profile goes to the interview panel. IBPS may run it online with proctoring or at test centres.

My advice: answer honestly and consistently. These tests ask similar questions in different ways, and a profile that contradicts itself looks worse than an ordinary one.

**2. Interview.** Interviews are run by the participating banks, with a nodal bank in each state or region and IBPS coordinating. Call letters go up on ibps.in. IBPS won't change your date or centre on request.

- Out of **100 marks**
- Minimum to qualify: **40 marks**, or **35** for SC, ST, OBC and PwBD
- Final score = Mains and interview in the ratio **80:20**

**3. Provisional allotment (January 2027, tentative).** After interviews, you're placed in one bank based on your rank and the bank preferences you gave. The vacancy numbers in the notification are **indicative**. Allotment goes by what each bank actually reports.

## Documents to keep ready for the interview

You'll also need to **upload PDFs** of your documents through a link IBPS will give. Then you bring originals and self-attested photocopies to the interview. The list in the notification includes:

- Printout of your **interview call letter**
- System-generated printout of your **CRP PO/MT-XVI application form**
- **Date of birth proof** (birth certificate or Class 10 certificate)
- **Photo ID**: Aadhaar, PAN, passport, driving licence, voter ID or similar
- **Graduation marksheets or certificate**, with proof that your result came on or before **21 July 2026**
- **Caste certificate** in the central government format (SC/ST/OBC). OBC must be non-creamy layer and on the central list
- **EWS income and asset certificate** for the right year, if you applied as EWS
- PwBD certificate, ex-serviceman papers, NOC from your employer if you're working, as applicable

**Check the graduation date first.** The most common problem at verification is a degree result declared after the cut-off date. If your university's marksheet doesn't show the result date, get a letter from your college that does.

## How to use the next 2–4 weeks

Most people relax after the Mains. Don't. The interview is only 20%, but at the top the gap between ranks is a few marks, and that decides your bank.

- **Know your graduation subject and your home state.** Expect questions on your district, the local economy and the main crops or industries.
- **Basic banking:** CASA, NPA, repo rate, priority sector lending, UPI, financial inclusion schemes like Jan Dhan. Also read the last two or three RBI monetary policy decisions.
- **Why banking, and why PO?** Have a real answer. "Job security" alone sounds weak.
- **Your work gap or job**, if you have one. Be ready to explain what you did.
- **Do 2–3 mock interviews**, ideally with someone who'll actually push back.

If you've also taken other bank exams this season, like [IBPS Clerk](/ibps-clerk-admit-card-2026) or [Bank of Baroda](/bank-of-baroda-recruitment-2026), keep their dates in front of you so nothing clashes.

## My take

Qualifying for the IBPS PO interview this year puts you in a small group, and most of the competition is now over. Two things matter from here: **don't miss a deadline** (personality test, document upload, interview date) and **don't throw away interview marks** on nerves or loose answers.

Check ibps.in every 2–3 days for the personality test notice. That notice is the next thing to come.
