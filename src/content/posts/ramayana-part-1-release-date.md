## Ramayana release date: 5 November, not 6

You'll still see **6 November** on some pages. That date was on the early international trailer from Sony Pictures.

On 11 October, the first day of Navratri, the makers shared a new poster on the film's official Instagram account (The World of Ramayana) with the line "Yeh Ram Ke Yug Ka Aarambh Hai". They confirmed the film "will arrive in cinemas worldwide on November 5, 2026". That's the final date.

Some reports also talk about **paid previews on 4 November** in India and North America. The makers haven't confirmed this. Don't book a "4 November preview" unless your theatre's own listing shows it.

## Ramayana advance booking and ticket prices

Bookings opened on Tuesday, 20 October, on BookMyShow and District, more than two weeks before release. That's early for an Indian film, and it shows how much the makers are counting on the first week.

It didn't open everywhere on the first morning, though. Trade reports said the North India distributor, Dharma Productions, asked cinemas for a bigger share of first-week ticket money than usual, and only a few theatres opened bookings at first. The makers later announced that bookings were open across India.

What the trackers reported after the first day (these are trade numbers, not official figures):

In the US, tickets are on sale through Fandango, with IMAX and 3D shows at selected theatres.

## Is IMAX worth it for Ramayana?

Honestly, yes, if it fits your budget. The makers have said from the start that the film was made for IMAX, and the trailers lean heavily on scale: the forests, Lanka, the battle scenes.

But don't pay ₹2,000+ just for a recliner. A regular IMAX or 3D seat in a good theatre gives you the same picture. And if IMAX is sold out near you, a well-maintained 2D screen with good sound is a better choice than a bad 3D show.

## Runtime and certificate

Trade reports say the film runs **3 hours 15 minutes** and has a **U certificate**. Sacnilk itself says the CBFC record for the full film wasn't public when it reported this; the public record covers the July trailer, which got a U certificate.

So treat 195 minutes as likely, not final. Your booking app will show the certificate and runtime once the theatre lists it. Plan for a long show with an interval, especially if you're going with kids or older family members.

In the US, the film is reported to be rated PG-13 for strong violence and action.

## What the film covers (no spoilers)

You know the story, and the makers aren't trying to surprise you with it. Part 1 sets up Rama's world and his clash with Ravana, and the full story is split across two films. Part 2 is planned for **Diwali 2027**.

The film had its world premiere planned for 27 October at the Academy Museum of Motion Pictures in Los Angeles, in 3D. That's unusual for an Indian film, and it shows Sony is selling this to audiences outside India too.

## When will Ramayana come on OTT?

No OTT platform or date has been announced. Big Hindi releases usually reach OTT 6 to 8 weeks after the theatre release, so don't expect it before the end of December 2026. Any site giving you a "Ramayana OTT date" right now is guessing.

## My take

Book early if you care about the format. The IMAX numbers say the big screens will be full on the first weekend, and the regular shows will be easier to find in week two.

The 3-hour-15-minute runtime is the real question. Dangal proved Nitesh Tiwari can hold attention for a long film, but a mythology epic that is mostly setup for Part 2 is harder.
