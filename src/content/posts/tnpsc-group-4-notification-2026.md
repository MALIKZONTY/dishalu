**TNPSC Group 4 notification 2026 is out for 6,574 posts, and you can apply till 5 November 2026 (11:59 PM).** The exam is on **Sunday, 10 January 2027**. For Village Administrative Officer, Junior Assistant and most other posts, a **10th pass (SSLC)** is enough.

It's one exam, one paper, no interview. Below are the facts from the official notification (No. 11/2026, dated 6 October 2026), and then the part most pages skip: where the marks actually come from.

## Eligibility: qualification

| Post | What you need |
|---|---|
| VAO, Junior Assistant (most), Bill Collector, Tax Collector, Revenue Assistant | A pass in SSLC (10th) |
| Typist | SSLC, plus Government Technical Examination in typewriting (Tamil and English, in the grade combinations listed in the notification) |
| Steno Typist, Grade III | SSLC, plus Government Technical Examination in both typewriting and shorthand |
| Junior Assistant in some corporations (SIDCO, Police Housing, Co-optex and others) | A degree. The power corporations ask for B.Com. |
| Forest Guard with Driving Licence | Higher Secondary pass, plus the licence and experience conditions in the notification |

For most Typist and Steno Typist posts you also need the Certificate Course in **Computer on Office Automation**. If you don't have it, you can still apply and finish it within your probation period.

**You must know Tamil.** If you studied Tamil as a language in SSLC, HSC or degree, or studied in Tamil medium, you're covered. Otherwise you have to pass TNPSC's Second Class Language Test in Tamil within two years of joining.

## Eligibility: age limit (as on 1 July 2026)

| Category | Most posts | VAO |
|---|---|---|
| Others (general) | 18 to 30 | 21 to 30 |
| BC, BCM, MBC/DC | 18 to 32 | 21 to 40 |
| SC, SC(A), ST | 18 to 35 | 21 to 40 |
| Persons with benchmark disability (general) | Up to 40 | Up to 40 |
| Ex-servicemen (general) | Up to 48 | Up to 48 |

The upper limit means you should **not have completed** that age on 1 July 2026.

A rule many people miss: for most posts, BC, BCM, MBC/DC, SC, SC(A) and ST candidates who have a qualification **higher than SSLC** (HSC, diploma or degree) have **no maximum age limit**. In practice that means up to 60. This relaxation does not apply to VAO or the forest posts.

One restriction for general-category candidates: if you already have five years or more of service in a central or state government job, you can't apply, even if you're within the age limit.

Executive Officer, Grade IV is different: the age is 25 to 35 for general candidates, and only candidates professing the Hindu religion can apply. The two Waqf Board posts are open only to Muslim candidates.

### Can candidates from other states apply?

Reservation and community-based age relaxation are only for people who belong to Tamil Nadu. The notification says candidates from other states are not treated as SC, ST or BC even if their community is on the list. So you'd be counted under "Others", with the 30-year limit.

The bigger hurdle is the exam itself. Half the paper is Tamil, set only in Tamil. If you can't read and write Tamil well, this isn't the exam for you.

## Exam pattern

One paper, three hours, OMR sheet, all questions objective and of SSLC standard.

| Part | Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Tamil Eligibility-cum-Scoring Test | 100 | 150 |
| B | General Studies | 75 | 150 (B and C together) |
| C | Aptitude and Mental Ability | 25 | |
| | **Total** | **200** | **300** |

- **Part A is a gate.** You need at least **60 out of 150** (40%) in Tamil. If you don't, Parts B and C are not even evaluated.
- **Overall minimum is 90 out of 300**, the same for all communities.
- Part A questions are in Tamil only. Parts B and C are in both Tamil and English.
- Your rank is based on the total of all three parts.
- The notification doesn't mention negative marking.

## Where the marks come from (syllabus weightage)

This is from the unit-wise syllabus in Annexure III of the notification. TNPSC says the split is indicative and can shift slightly.

| Part | Unit | Questions |
|---|---|---|
| A | Tamil grammar | 25 |
| A | Tamil vocabulary | 15 |
| A | Writing skills | 15 |
| A | Reading and comprehension | 15 |
| A | Literature, Tamil scholars and service to Tamil (including Thirukkural) | 15 |
| A | Technical and official terms | 10 |
| A | Simple translation | 5 |
| B | Indian Economy and Development Administration in Tamil Nadu | 20 |
| B | History, Culture, Heritage and Socio-Political Movements of Tamil Nadu | 20 |
| B | Indian Polity | 15 |
| B | History, Culture of India and Indian National Movement | 10 |
| B | General Science | 5 |
| B | Geography | 5 |
| C | Aptitude (simplification, percentage, HCF/LCM, ratio, interest, area, volume, time and work) | 15 |
| C | Reasoning (puzzles, dice, visual reasoning, number series) | 10 |

What this tells you:

- **Tamil is half the exam.** 100 of 200 questions. No other subject comes close, and it also decides whether the rest of your paper is read at all.
- **40 of the 75 General Studies questions are about Tamil Nadu**: its economy and welfare schemes, and its history, literature and social movements, including Thirukkural. Your school Samacheer Kalvi textbooks are the base for this.
- **General Science and Geography together are only 10 questions.** Don't spend a month on them.
- **Aptitude is 25 questions of SSLC-level maths.** These are the most "fixed" marks in the paper if you practise daily.

## Selection process

1. **Written exam** on 10 January 2027.
2. **Onscreen certificate verification**, for about 3 times the vacancies in general and 2 times in reserved categories. You upload your certificates.
3. **Physical certificate verification and counselling**, where you choose a post and department by rank.

There's no interview.

Forest Watcher and Forest Guard candidates also face a physical standards test and an endurance test in Chennai: a walk of 25 km in four hours for men, and 16 km in four hours for women and third gender candidates.

**If marks are tied**, the candidate with the higher qualification ranks first, then the older candidate, then whoever applied earlier. For Typist and Steno Typist posts, the higher typewriting or shorthand grade is looked at first. So applying early can count, in a small way.

## Fee and concessions

| What | Amount |
|---|---|
| One-Time Registration (OTR), valid 5 years | ₹150 |
| Examination fee | ₹100 |

Who doesn't pay the ₹100 exam fee:

- **SC, SC(A), ST:** full exemption
- **Persons with benchmark disability, destitute widows:** full exemption
- **MBC/DC, BC, BCM:** three free chances
- **Ex-servicemen:** two free chances

Free chances are counted across all your TNPSC applications, not only Group 4. If you've used them up, choose "No" for fee concession and pay. A wrong claim can get your application rejected and you debarred for a year.

Payment is online only: net banking, credit or debit card, or UPI.

## How to apply for TNPSC Group 4 2026

1. Go to [tnpscexams.in](https://www.tnpscexams.in/).
2. New user? Complete **One-Time Registration** first (₹150). Linking Aadhaar to your OTR is mandatory.
3. Log in and open the application for **Combined Civil Services Examination IV (Group IV Services)**.
4. Fill in your details and choose your **post option**: "All the Posts", or "Posts other than Forest Guard with Driving Licence / Forest Watcher".
5. Choose **two exam centres**. You'll get one of them. (Candidates with benchmark disability choose one.)
6. Upload your photo and signature.
7. Pay the fee, or claim your concession, and submit. Note your application number.

Mistakes that get applications into trouble:

- **Old photo.** The photo must be taken **on or after 6 October 2026**, in a studio, on a white background, with your name and the date printed at the bottom.
- **Wrong post option.** Pick "All the Posts" only if you're ready for the forest posts' 25 km or 16 km walk.
- **Pressing Back or Refresh during payment.** If payment fails, check the transaction status before paying again.
- **Leaving it to a browsing centre.** TNPSC says you are responsible for errors, not the centre. Check the preview yourself.

You can edit everything till 5 November, and again in the correction window from 9 to 11 November. After that, nothing can be changed.

**Help:** toll-free 1800 419 0958 (10 AM to 5:45 PM, working days). For registration and application problems, write to helpdesk@tnpscexams.in.

TNPSC also warns about agents who promise jobs. Selection is purely on marks. Don't pay anyone.

## How to use the 92 days

From today, 10 October, to exam day is 92 days. A simple split that follows the weightage:

- **Every day:** 1.5 to 2 hours of Tamil. Grammar is the largest Tamil unit (25 questions), so start there.
- **Every day:** 30 minutes of aptitude. Ten sums a day adds up to more than 900 by January.
- **Weeks 1 to 6:** Tamil Nadu units (economy and administration, history and culture) and Indian Polity. That's 55 of the 75 General Studies questions.
- **Weeks 7 to 9:** Indian history, then science and geography.
- **Last 3 to 4 weeks:** one full 200-question paper every two or three days, on an OMR sheet, in three hours.

## Should you apply?

**Apply if** you're 10th pass or above, comfortable in Tamil, and want a permanent state government job without an interview. For ₹100, or free if you're eligible, it's the best-value exam in Tamil Nadu.

**Think twice if** you can't write Tamil comfortably. Without 60 marks in Part A, nothing else you score is counted.

Be realistic about the odds. Lakhs of people write this exam for a few thousand posts, and the paper is 10th standard level, so cut-offs run high. What separates people is Tamil accuracy and not losing marks in aptitude.
