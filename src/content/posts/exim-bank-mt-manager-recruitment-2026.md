## Eligibility for Exim Bank MT and Manager 2026

The rule that cuts most people out: **60% aggregate (or equivalent CGPA) is needed almost everywhere.** Distance, part-time and online PG courses are not accepted.

**Management Trainee: main qualifications**

| Area | What you need |
|---|---|
| Banking Operations (60 posts) | 3-year graduation with 60% **and** a 2-year full-time MBA/PGDBA/PGDBM/MMS in Finance, International Business or Foreign Trade with 60%, **or** CA, **or** CMA (CA needs ICAI membership) |
| Legal | LLB with 60% (integrated, or 60% in both graduation and LLB) |
| Digital Technology | B.E./B.Tech in CS, IT or ECE with 60%, or 60% in both graduation and MCA |
| Cyber Security | B.E./B.Tech/M.Tech/M.E./MCA in CS, IT or ECE with 60% |
| Compliance | ACS (ICSI associate member) plus graduation with 60% |
| HR | Graduation plus 2-year MBA in HR/Personnel, 60% in both |
| International Relations | Graduation plus 2-year MBA (Finance/IB/Foreign Trade) or MA Economics, 60% |

Administration needs a PG in Hotel/Hospitality or Facilities Management. ESG needs a PG in Management or Environmental Science. Corporate Communications needs a Master's in Mass Comm, Journalism, Advertising or Marketing. All need 60%.

**Final-year students can apply** if they will finish by May 2027. You must show your degree by the joining date (no later than 30 June 2027).

**Manager (MM-II):** the same kind of degree as the MT post in that area, plus **at least 4 years of post-qualification experience** in the matching field (trade finance, recovery, credit, audit and so on). Some posts need extra skills, like Finacle for Documentary Credit or CISA for the Information Security audit role.

## Exim Bank MT exam pattern and selection process

Selection has two steps: a **written test** and a **personal interview**.

| Test | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Professional Knowledge (subjective) | Part I: 1 compulsory question/case study. Part II: answer any 6 of 8, all from your chosen specialisation | 100 | 2 hours 30 minutes |

- There is **no sectional cut-off**. Interview shortlisting is on written test marks.
- **Final merit: 70% written test + 30% interview** (both out of 100).
- Interviews are in Mumbai and/or New Delhi.
- Exim Bank has posted area-wise syllabi on its careers page for earlier drives. Use them to see the depth they expect.

This is not a bank PO exam. There's no reasoning or quant section. You write answers, like a university exam. If you're used to only MCQ tests, practise writing 2–3 structured answers a day on your subject before December.

## How to apply for Exim Bank recruitment 2026

1. Go to [eximbankindia.in/careers](https://www.eximbankindia.in/careers) on or after **27 November 2026** and open the "Apply Online" link for Advertisement HRM/MT/M/2026-27/05.
2. Click "New Registration", enter your name, email and mobile, and note the registration number and password.
3. Fill in your education, marks and experience. Pick your area carefully; the exam is on that specialisation.
4. Upload a photo, signature (not in capital letters), left thumb impression and a **handwritten declaration in English**, as per Annexure III of the advertisement.
5. Pay the fee online by **11 December 2026**. Without payment, your form is not registered.
6. Save the final form and payment receipt.

Reserved category candidates can opt for free pre-recruitment training in the form.

## Should you apply?

If you're a final-year or fresh MBA (Finance), CA or CMA under 28 with 60% throughout, **apply**. 60 Banking Operations seats is a lot for Exim Bank, and the pay beats most private bank MT roles.

Law and B.Tech (CS/IT) graduates also have a fair shot with 10 Legal and 9 Digital Technology seats. The 60% rule and the descriptive exam keep the applicant pool much smaller than a bank PO exam.

Skip it if your PG is distance or part-time, or if you scored below 60% in graduation. You'll be rejected at document check even if you clear the exam.

If you have 2+ years of experience in a bank or NBFC, also look at [SIDBI Grade A and B recruitment](/sidbi-grade-a-b-recruitment-2026), which closes the same day, 11 December.
