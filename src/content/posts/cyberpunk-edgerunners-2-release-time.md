## What is Edgerunners 2 about?

From Netflix's own description: it follows "a fresh group of hired guns" in Night City, told in "a reimagined animation style". Netflix calls the 10 episodes "a raw chronicle of redemption and revenge in a city that thrives on violence".

The show's line on the teaser is short: **"New crew. Same hell."**

It is **not** a direct sequel. Season 1 (September 2022) followed street kid David Martinez and the netrunner Lucy. That story is not continued. Edgerunners 2 only shares the city and the rules of the world: cyberware, corporations, mercenaries for hire.

Who should watch it:

- Season 1 fans who want more of Trigger's fast, colourful action.
- *Cyberpunk 2077* players. You'll spot places, gangs and gear from the game.
- New viewers. This is a clean starting point, which is rare for a "Season 2".

Who should skip it: anyone who doesn't like graphic violence. Season 1 was rated for adults, and the Edgerunners 2 teaser leans hard into body horror. Check the age rating on the Netflix title page before you watch with family.

## Who made it: Trigger is back, with a new director

Studio Trigger animates the series again, working with CD PROJEKT RED, the studio behind *Cyberpunk 2077*. The big change is in the director's chair.

- **Kai Ikarashi** directs. This is his first time directing a full series. He worked on Season 1 as a storyboard artist, animation director and animator, and he directed its sixth episode, "Girl on Fire".
- **Bartosz Sztybor** from CD PROJEKT RED returns as showrunner, writer and producer.
- **Masahiko Otsuka** writes the screenplay.
- **Ichigo Kanno** is the lead character designer, which is why the new crew looks noticeably different from David's gang.

Season 1 was directed by Hiroyuki Imaishi (*Kill la Kill*, *Promare*). Some fans are nervous about the change. Honestly, Ikarashi directed one of Season 1's best-liked episodes, so I'd give him a fair chance.
