## Which one should you buy?

**X Fold 6:** only if you really need a phone that opens into a tablet, for reading, documents or multitasking. At ₹2,19,999 it costs about ₹40,000 more than the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 (₹1,79,999 at launch). The big battery and 200MP camera are strong points, but for most people this is a lot of money for a phone.

**V80:** a camera-focused phone with a big battery and long software support. But ₹62,999 for a Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 phone is high. At this price you're close to phones with flagship chips. Buy it for the cameras and the 6 years of updates, not for gaming power.

**S2 FE:** the most practical of the three. A 10,000mAh battery, IP69 and a 1-year extra warranty for ₹38,999 is good for people who travel, use maps all day, or just hate charging. The Dimensity 7300e is a mid-range chip, so it's fine for daily use but not for heavy gaming.

Honestly, prices of many phones have gone up this year because memory chips cost more. So don't expect quick price cuts on these.
