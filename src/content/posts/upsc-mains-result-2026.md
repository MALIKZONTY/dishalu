The **UPSC Mains result 2026 is out**. The Union Public Service Commission declared the Civil Services (Main) Examination 2026 result on **5 November 2026**, and **2,526 candidates** have qualified for the Personality Test (interview).

If your roll number is on the list, your next deadline is the **DAF-II form, open from 11 to 25 November 2026 (till 6 PM)** on upsconline.nic.in. Miss it and UPSC won't send you an interview call.

## How to check the UPSC Mains result 2026

The result is a simple PDF list of roll numbers. There's no login and no scorecard at this stage.

1. Go to [upsc.gov.in](https://www.upsc.gov.in).
2. Click **What's New** on the home page.
3. Open **"Civil Services (Main) Examination, 2026: Written Result"**.
4. Open the PDF ([direct link to the result PDF](https://www.upsc.gov.in/sites/default/files/WR-CSM-2026-Engl-RollList-05112026.pdf)).
5. Press Ctrl+F on a computer, or use "Find in document" in your phone's PDF reader, and type your 7-digit roll number.
6. Save the PDF. You'll want it handy while filling the DAF-II.

The roll numbers are listed in increasing order, so you can also scroll to your number if search doesn't work on your phone.

**Tip:** If you can't find your number, check the digits against your Mains e-admit card before you panic. Several people mistype one digit and lose an hour to worry.

## From 13,343 to 2,526: how tough this stage was

Here are the official numbers for CSE 2026 in one place:

| Stage | Candidates |
|---|---|
| Qualified Prelims (24 May 2026) and shortlisted for Mains | 13,343 |
| Qualified Mains and called for the interview | 2,526 |
| Vacancies notified | 1,016 |

So roughly **1 in 5** Mains candidates made it to the interview, and now about **2.5 candidates are competing for each vacancy**. For comparison, UPSC's own note says 14,161 candidates were shortlisted for Mains last year (CSE 2025) against 1,087 vacancies.

If you didn't clear it this time, you're in the same boat as over 10,800 others who wrote Mains this August. More on that below.

## UPSC DAF-II 2026: what to fill and by when

This is the most urgent part of the notice. Every qualified candidate has to log in to [upsconline.nic.in](https://upsconline.nic.in) between **11 November and 25 November 2026 (6 PM)** and submit the Detailed Application Form (DAF-II).

What UPSC asks you to fill or update (wherever it applies to you):

- **Proof of passing your degree**, if you had marked your qualification as "appearing" at the Prelims stage. This is compulsory. If you don't upload it, your candidature is cancelled.
- Postal address (correspondence and permanent)
- Higher educational qualifications
- Achievements in different fields, if any
- Employment details and service experience
- Service already allotted through an earlier CSE, if any
- Marital status, parental details, earlier exam and attempt details
- OBC/EWS annexure (if you claim it)
- Socio-economic questionnaire
- **Service preference and home cadre**
- Disclosure for the Pratibha Setu portal

Even if you have **nothing new to add**, you still have to log in, check your details and click final submit. UPSC says clearly that no e-summon letter will be issued otherwise.

After you submit, a unique reference number comes to your registered email and mobile. Keep a screenshot.

### Take service preference seriously

Most people rush this. Don't. Your service and cadre preferences in the DAF-II decide where you'll spend your career if you're selected, and you can't change them later. UPSC says it won't accept requests to change anything in the application form, apart from address and contact details (to be sent by letter or email to csm-upsc@nic.in within 7 days of the notice).

Spend an evening on it. Talk to someone already in service if you can.

## Documents to keep ready for the interview

UPSC wants the **original certificates** at the interview, so start collecting them now:

- Age proof (Class 10 certificate)
- Degree certificate and mark sheets
- Caste certificate (SC/ST/OBC), EWS certificate, or PwBD certificate, if you claim reservation
- Ex-servicemen documents, if applicable
- TA form, for travel allowance

**Important:** Reservation and relaxation certificates must be issued **on or before 27 February 2026**, the last date of the Prelims application. A certificate issued after that date can cost you the reserved seat. If yours is newer, speak to the issuing office now.

## When is the UPSC interview 2026?

UPSC hasn't given the dates yet. Here's what the notice does confirm:

- Interviews will be held at **UPSC's office, Dholpur House, Shahjahan Road, New Delhi 110069**.
- The schedule and **e-summon letters** will be put on upsc.gov.in and upsconline.nic.in. No paper letters are sent.
- Requests to change your interview date and time are "ordinarily" not accepted.
- If you can't download your e-summon letter, contact UPSC on **011-40303444 / 011-24041001** or email **csm-upsc@nic.in**.

You'll also have to fill an **Attestation Form** online on the DoPT portal (cseplus.dopt.gov.in) between the start and end of the interviews. DoPT's helpline for it is doais1@nic.in, usais-dopt@nic.in, or 011-24010454 / 24010413.

In past years, UPSC's CSE interviews have usually started in December or January and run for a few months. Treat that only as a rough guide until UPSC puts out this year's schedule.

## What about the cut-off and marks?

Not yet. UPSC releases the Mains cut-off and individual marks only **after the final result**. The notice says mark sheets will be uploaded within 15 days of the final result and will stay online for 30 days. So if you're seeing a "UPSC Mains cut-off 2026" figure online right now, it's a guess.

## How to prepare for the UPSC interview

Here's what actually helps in the next few weeks:

- **Read your own DAF line by line.** Most interview questions start from it: your hometown, your degree, your hobbies, your job. If you wrote "reading" as a hobby, know the last three books you read.
- **Follow the news daily** from now till your interview date. Panels often ask about events from that very week.
- **Do 3 to 4 mock interviews,** not 15. Too many mocks make answers sound rehearsed.
- **Know your optional subject basics** and your home state's current issues.
- **Be honest when you don't know.** "I'm not sure, sir/ma'am" is a better answer than a made-up fact.

## Didn't clear the Mains? What next

It hurts, especially after a year of work. But be practical:

- **CSE 2027 is the next chance.** Watch upsc.gov.in for the notification. Check how many attempts and how much age you have left.
- Your **Mains marks** will come out after the final result. Use them to see which paper pulled you down, instead of guessing.
- Look at other exams that use the same preparation: state PSC exams like [APPSC Group 1](/appsc-group-1-recruitment-2026), the UPSC CAPF exam and others that reuse your GS preparation. Many CSE aspirants clear a state service first.

## My take

If you're on the list, congratulations, but don't relax too long. Do the **DAF-II in the first week** of the window (11 to 17 November), not on 25 November when upsconline.nic.in usually slows down. Then put your certificates in one folder and start mock interviews.
