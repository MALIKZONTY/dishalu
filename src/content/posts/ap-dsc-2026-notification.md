**The AP DSC 2026 notification is out for 2,721 teacher posts in Andhra Pradesh.** The School Education Department issued the detailed notifications on 5 November. **Apply online from 11 to 30 November 2026** on tet2dsc.apcfss.in. The computer based test (CBT) starts from **20 December 2026**.

That's a much smaller DSC than the 2025 Mega DSC. Most posts are concentrated in a few districts (Kurnool, Prakasam, Kadapa, Nellore), so check your district's number before you get your hopes up.

## Eligibility

### Qualification

You must hold the qualification **by 5 November 2026**, the date of the detailed notification. No relaxation is given later.

- **SGT:** Intermediate with 50% (45% for SC/ST/BC/PwBD) plus a 2-year D.Ed / D.El.Ed, or a 4-year B.El.Ed. Special education D.Ed holders (RCI) are also eligible.
- **School Assistant (subjects):** degree with the subject, usually 50% (40% for SC/ST/BC/PwBD), plus B.Ed with that subject as methodology. Integrated B.A.B.Ed / B.Sc.B.Ed and B.E./B.Tech routes are also allowed for some subjects, with different percentages.
- **School Assistant (Physical Education):** B.P.Ed or M.P.Ed. No TET needed.
- **Societies posts (Principal, PGT, TGT, PD, PET):** qualifications are listed post by post in the Information Bulletin and G.O.Ms.No.50.

The full subject-wise rules are in **G.O.Ms.No.52 (4 November 2026)**, which is on the official website. Read your subject's line in it before paying the fee.

### TET requirement

For SA and SGT posts you need a pass in **APTET or CTET** in the right paper and medium:

| TET | Max marks | OC/EWS pass | BC pass | SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-servicemen pass |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| APTET | 150 | 90 | 75 | 60 |
| CTET | 150 | 90 | 75 | 60 |

For SA (Languages), only the language-specific APTET papers from 2017 onwards count. The old combined APTET score won't be used.

## Exam pattern and selection

For **SA and SGT**, the final score is out of 100: **80 marks from the TRT (CBT) and 20 marks from your TET score.** For SA (Physical Education), the 100 marks come only from the CBT.

**School Assistant (languages and subjects): 2 hours 30 minutes**

| Part | Section | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | General Knowledge and Current Affairs | 20 | 10 |
| II | Perspectives in Education | 10 | 5 |
| III | Classroom implications of Educational Psychology | 10 | 5 |
| IV | Subject content | 80 | 40 |
| IV | Subject methodology | 40 | 20 |
| | **Total** | **160** | **80** |

**SGT: 2 hours 30 minutes, 160 questions, 80 marks.** It has GK and current affairs (16 questions), Perspectives in Education (8), Educational Psychology (16), and then 24 questions each (16 content + 8 methodology) for Language-I, English, Maths, Science and Social Studies.

**SA (Physical Education): 3 hours, 200 questions, 100 marks.**

Minimum qualifying marks on the combined score: **40% for OC/EWS, 35% for BC, 30% for SC/ST/PwBD.** Multiple shifts will be normalised. The paper is in English plus your chosen medium.

## How to apply for AP DSC 2026

1. Go to [tet2dsc.apcfss.in](https://tet2dsc.apcfss.in/) (open from **11 November** to **30 November**). Read the [Information Bulletin](https://tet2dsc.apcfss.in/DSC2026/info_bulletian/School%20Education%20General%20Posts%20Inforamtion%20Bulletin.pdf) first. Separate bulletins for Special Education and Societies posts are on the same site.
2. Pay **₹1,000 for each post** you want through the payment gateway.
3. Fill in the online form: personal details, local status, category, qualifications and TET details.
4. **Mark your post and management preferences in order**, for example 1st SA Maths, 2nd SA Physical Science, 3rd SGT. Once you're selected for a higher preference, your name is removed from the other lists.
5. Check everything before final submission. **There is no edit option after you submit.** The only change allowed later is a one-time reorder of preferences for 7 days after the merit list comes out.
6. Download and save the application.

If you apply for more than one post, use **one application** and pay ₹1,000 extra for each additional post. Don't fill a second form.

**Helpline (School Education Department):** 8121947387, 7995649286, 8125046997, 7386282388, 6281704160, 7995789286. Working days, 10 AM to 5:30 PM, as per the Information Bulletin.

Upload your certificates in your login **within 7 days of the merit list**. EWS candidates need an FY 2025-26 income certificate issued on or after 1 April 2026.

## Should you apply?

If you're TET-qualified and your district has seats in your subject, **yes.** Some subjects have fewer than 50 posts in the whole state (Maths 41, Physical Science 45), so every mark counts, and **the 20% TET weightage means a high TET score is a real advantage**. If yours is just at the pass mark, writing the next APTET to raise it may help in future DSCs.

Be realistic if you're in a district like Vizianagaram with almost no posts in your subject. The 5% open quota is small, and you'll be competing with local candidates from the bigger districts too.

For prep, you have about 6 weeks. Spend most of it on **content and methodology for your subject (60 of 80 marks for SA)**. GK and psychology together are only 20 marks. Use the official suggestive syllabus on the website, and solve the 2025 Mega DSC papers in timed sets.

Next step: check your subject and district in the vacancy list on 11 November, keep your TET marks memo and caste/EWS certificate ready, and apply in the first week. If you're also looking at other teacher jobs, see [UP TGT recruitment 2026](/up-tgt-recruitment-2026).
