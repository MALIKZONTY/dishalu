**The CISF Constable admit card 2026 comes out on 27 November.** CISF will send your exam city, state, date and shift by **email and SMS on 17 November 2026**, and the e-admit cards open on [cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in](https://cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in/) from **27 November 2026**.

This covers four recruitments in one go: **Constable (Fire)-2024, Constable (Tradesmen)-2024, Constable (Driver & DCPO)-2024** and the departmental **ASI/Exe (LDCE)-2023**. The computer based exam (CBE) is on **30 November and 1 December 2026**, across centres all over the country.

## CISF exam date 2026 and shift timings

The 13 November notice fixes one shift for each post. Note the **entry time** and the **gate close time**. The gate shuts an hour before the paper starts, which is earlier than many people expect.

| Date | Post | Exam time | Entry from | Gate closes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30 Nov 2026 | ASI/Exe (LDCE)-2023 | 2:00 PM to 5:30 PM | 12:30 PM | **1:30 PM** |
| 1 Dec 2026 | Constable (Fire)-2024 | 9:00 AM to 11:00 AM | 7:30 AM | **8:30 AM** |
| 1 Dec 2026 | Constable (Tradesmen)-2024 | 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM | 11:30 AM | **12:30 PM** |
| 1 Dec 2026 | Constable (Driver & DCPO)-2024 | 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM | 3:30 PM | **4:30 PM** |

On 26 October, CISF had put out two notices calling these dates "tentative". The 13 November notice now gives the full shift plan, so treat these as the working schedule unless CISF posts a change.

If you're in the Fire shift, the 8:30 AM gate close is the one to worry about. If your centre is in another town, honestly, travel the evening before. A 7:30 AM entry after an overnight bus with no buffer is how people miss exams.

## CISF exam city slip: there's no download link this time

Unlike SSC or RRB, CISF hasn't announced a separate city slip page. The notice says the **city, state, date and shift timing will reach you over email and SMS on 17 November 2026**.

So today, do this:

- Check the **email inbox and the spam/promotions folder** of the email ID you gave in your application.
- Check SMS on the **mobile number you registered with**.
- Once you know the city, look at train and bus options now. Bookings around 30 November and 1 December may fill up, because four exams run back to back.

The message gives only the city. The full centre address comes on the admit card on 27 November.

## How to download the CISF admit card 2026

From 27 November 2026:

1. Open the official CISF recruitment website, [cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in](https://cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in/).
2. Click the **Login** option at the top of the home page.
3. Log in with the details you created when you applied (CISF hasn't yet said which fields the admit card login needs).
4. Open the e-admit card for your post and download the PDF.
5. Print it on A4 paper. Keep one extra copy at home.

Don't wait for the evening of 29 or 30 November. Four recruitments share one website, and government sites get slow when everyone logs in on the last day.

## Details to check on your CISF admit card

Check these the same day you download it:

- **Name, photo, signature and date of birth**: they must match your photo ID
- **Post name**: Fire, Tradesmen, Driver & DCPO or ASI/Exe (LDCE)
- **Exam date, shift, entry time and gate close time**: compare with the table above
- **Centre address**: open it on Google Maps and work out how long it takes to reach
- **Category** and any special instructions printed on the card

If anything is wrong, contact CISF through the contact details given on [cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in](https://cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in/) as soon as you can. Don't leave it for exam morning.

## What to carry and exam-day rules

CISF will print the exact instructions on the admit card, so read that page fully. Follow what your card says over anything here. In CBEs of this kind, you'll usually need:

- The **printed e-admit card**
- An **original photo ID** (Aadhaar, voter ID, driving licence, PAN or passport), with your name and date of birth matching the card
- Any extra photo or document that the admit card asks for

And the basics that apply in almost every recruitment exam hall:

- **Reach before the gate close time.** After that, you won't be let in, however good your reason.
- **No phone, smartwatch, Bluetooth device or calculator** inside. Leave them at home if you can; many centres have no safe place to keep them.
- Don't carry notes, chits or printed material other than what the card allows.

## Beware of fake job calls

CISF's own recruitment site carries a **caution notice about fraudulent recruitment and appointment offers**. Nobody can get you selected for money. If someone calls saying they can "fix" your CBE result or your posting, it's a scam. Only trust notices on [cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in](https://cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in/).

## What comes next after the CBE

CISF hasn't announced the answer key or result dates yet. Its notices only ask candidates to keep checking the recruitment website for the next stages. We'll update this page when CISF puts out the answer key or result.

My suggestion for the next 10 days: don't start any new topic. Take one timed practice paper every day at **the same hour as your shift**. If you're in the 5 PM Driver & DCPO shift, practise at 5 PM. Your brain gets used to working at that hour, and that matters more than one extra chapter.
