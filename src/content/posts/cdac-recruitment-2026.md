**CDAC recruitment 2026 is open for 858 contract posts across 12 C-DAC centres.** Online applications started on 5 October and close on **26 October 2026**. There's **no application fee**, and most posts need a B.E./B.Tech, M.E./M.Tech, MCA or M.Sc.

Two things to know before you apply. These are **contract jobs, not permanent government posts**. And the "951 posts" number you see on many sites doesn't match the official portal. I counted every centre's list on careers.cdac.in on 9 October, and it adds up to 858.

## Eligibility: qualification and experience

The rules below are from the official advertisement terms, which are common across centres.

- **Degree:** B.E./B.Tech or equivalent, or M.E./M.Tech, or a post graduate degree in Science or Computer Application (M.Sc, MCA) in a relevant field, or a Ph.D. Bengaluru asks for **60% or equivalent CGPA** in B.E./B.Tech and PG degrees for Project Engineer and above.
- **College:** must be AICTE/UGC approved or a recognised university.
- **CGPA holders:** you need a proof of percentage issued by your university. A self-made conversion won't do.
- **Final-semester students** can apply, but you join only after passing with the required score.
- **Experience** must be after your qualifying degree. Internships, college projects and teaching don't count unless the post says so.
- You need experience certificates showing period, salary and post held. Experience without papers is not counted.

Each post also has its own domain: quantum computing, VLSI, embedded systems, cyber security, AI/ML, full stack development, HPC and so on. Hyderabad's list is heavy on cyber security. Bengaluru has many quantum computing roles.

## Age limit and salary

C-DAC gives the age limit and pay against each post. These are the figures in the **Bengaluru advertisement** (C-DAC/BLR/03/2026). Other centres follow a similar ladder, but check your own post page.

| Post | Experience | Upper age | Starting CTC per year |
|---|---|---|---|
| Project Associate | Fresher or experienced | 30 (fresher), 45 (experienced) | ₹3.6 lakh to ₹5.04 lakh |
| Project Engineer | 0 to 4 years | 30 (fresher), 45 (experienced) | ₹4.49 lakh to ₹7.11 lakh |
| Senior Project Engineer / Module Lead / Project Lead | 4 to 7 years | 40 | ₹8.49 lakh to ₹14 lakh |
| Project Manager / Programme Manager | 9 to 15 years | 56 | ₹12.63 lakh to ₹22.9 lakh |

A few points on these numbers:

- The pay shown is for the **minimum** experience. More experience inside the bracket gets extra increments as per C-DAC policy.
- Age and experience are counted as on the **last date of application**, which is 26 October 2026.
- SC, ST, OBC (non-creamy layer), PwD and ex-servicemen get age relaxation as per Government of India norms. Government employees on a pay scale get 5 years.
- I couldn't confirm the pay for Project Technician from the portal today. See the post page for it.

## Selection process

There's no single all-India exam. Each centre runs its own selection.

1. **Screening** of your application on academic records and experience.
2. **Written or skill test**, only if the centre decides to hold one.
3. **Interview.**

Meeting the minimum eligibility doesn't mean you'll get a call. If too many people apply, C-DAC can raise the cut-off and shortlist only those with higher marks or more experience. So fill in **everything** you have, not just the minimum.

Call letters come **only by email**. No TA/DA is paid for the test or interview. Dates haven't been announced.

## How to apply for CDAC recruitment 2026

1. Open [careers.cdac.in](https://careers.cdac.in/). You'll see the 12 centres with their advertisements.
2. Click your centre's advertisement and read the job list.
3. Click **View Details & Apply** against the post you want. Check the age, qualification, experience and location.
4. Log in with your **mobile number and OTP**.
5. Fill the form. If you applied to C-DAC before, you get a pre-filled form that you can edit.
6. Upload your **photo (.jpg, up to 400 KB)** and **resume (PDF, up to 500 KB)**.
7. Submit and note the application number. Keep a printout for yourself.

Applying to more than one post? Each post needs a **separate application**. Don't send any hard copy to C-DAC.

If you work in a government office or PSU, send a signed printout through proper channel to the centre's HRD section, or bring an NOC to the interview.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Old phone number or email.** Everything comes by email, so use the ones you check daily.
- **Counting internship as experience.** It will be rejected at document verification.
- **No percentage proof for CGPA.** Ask your university for it now. It can take days.
- **Paying an agent.** C-DAC says it has not authorised any agent or agency, and the form is free.
- **Waiting for the last day.** The OTP login can be slow when traffic is high.

## Should you apply?

Honestly, it depends on where you are in your career.

**Apply if** you're a fresher or have 1 to 3 years of experience and want real project work in areas like HPC, quantum, VLSI or cyber security. C-DAC on your resume helps, the form is free, and the work is closer to R&D than most service-company jobs. Freshers should look first at Chennai (15 Project Associate posts), Kolkata (9 fresher Project Engineer posts) and Bengaluru.

**Think twice if** you want job security. This is a contract of up to 3 years, tied to a project. It can be extended, but the advertisement says clearly that it gives no claim to a regular post. You can also be moved to any project location in India.

If a permanent central job is what you're after, look at [RRB NTPC Graduate 2026](/rrb-ntpc-graduate-recruitment-2026) (apply by 6 November) or keep [GATE 2027](/gate-2027-registration-last-date) in your plan for PSU jobs. And if you're comparing this with private IT hiring, read our note on [TCS NQT](/tcs-nqt-october-2026-last-date).

Your next step: open the portal today, shortlist 2 or 3 posts that match your degree and domain, and get your percentage proof and experience letters ready before 26 October.
