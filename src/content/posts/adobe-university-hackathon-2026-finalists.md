## How the rounds worked

This was a long hackathon. It started with an online test in August and ends with the finale in October. Here is every round, with dates from the official Unstop page.

| Round | What it was | Dates |
|---|---|---|
| Round 1 | Online assessment (MCQ + coding), 1 hour | 9–10 August 2026 |
| Round 2 | Case study on brand visibility, 45 minutes | 16 August 2026 |
| Round 3 | Development round: build the solution and submit code | 27 August – 13 September 2026 |
| Live launch session | Problem briefing by Adobe for shortlisted teams | 3 September 2026 |
| Round 4 | Prototype showcase: working interface + demo video | 21–27 September 2026 |
| Finale confirmation | Shortlisted teams confirm they will attend | 2–5 October 2026 |
| Round 5 | Grand Finale at Adobe, Noida | 16 October 2026 |

In Round 3, teams had to submit a ZIP of their project with a short README. In Round 4, they had to build a working prototype that users can actually click through and explore, and send a demo video of **5 minutes or less**, plus an optional "reproduction" text file so judges could re-run their work.

So the last two rounds were not about slides. They were about building something that works.

Also note the words "a chance". The top 50 are not promised an internship. They get a chance at an interview. Still, a ₹1,10,000 monthly stipend is among the highest you'll see for student internships in India, which is why lakhs of students registered.

## My take: should you try next year?

Yes, if you're in 2nd or 3rd year of B.Tech and can give it 6 to 8 weeks. Round 1 is a normal MCQ and coding test, so DSA practice helps there. But the later rounds reward people who can build and ship a working product, not just solve coding questions.

Two things I'd do differently from most students:

- **Build one real project before August.** Something with a working interface that others can use. Rounds 3 and 4 are exactly this.
- **Practise explaining your project in 5 minutes.** The Round 4 video had a hard 5-minute limit. Most people lose marks by running out of time before showing the main feature.

And if you're from a smaller college thinking "this isn't for us", look at Team Nexora again. Register next year.
