**BHEL has opened recruitment for 415 trainee posts: 200 Engineer/Executive Trainees (Advt. 02/2026) and 215 Supervisor Trainees (Advt. 03/2026). Apply online on careers.bhel.in from 25 November to 28 December 2026 (11:45 PM).** The Computer Based Exam is tentatively on **27 January 2027**.

The big news: **no GATE this time.** BHEL is running its own exam for Engineer Trainees, so final-year B.Tech students and graduates without a GATE score can apply. Diploma holders and B.Com/BBA graduates get their own 215 Supervisor Trainee posts.

## Eligibility for BHEL Engineer and Supervisor Trainee 2026

**Engineer Trainee (Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, Civil, IT/CS)**
- Full-time B.E./B.Tech, or a 5-year integrated/dual degree in engineering, in the matching branch.
- No minimum percentage is written for Engineer Trainees in the advertisement.
- Allied branches count. For example, Production, Industrial, Mechatronics and Thermal can take the Mechanical exam; EEE, Instrumentation and Power Electronics can take Electronics; AI & ML and AI & Data Science can take IT/CS. Check the full list in the PDF.

**Executive Trainee (Finance):** qualified CA or CMA.

**Executive Trainee (HR):** full-time bachelor's degree with **60%**, plus a 2-year full-time PG degree/diploma in HR, Personnel Management, Industrial Relations or Social Work, or an MBA, with **55%**. Social Work and MBA candidates need an HR or labour welfare elective in the final year.

**Supervisor Trainee**
- Technical: full-time regular **Diploma in Engineering** (Mechanical, Electrical, Civil or Electronics) with **65%** aggregate (60% for SC/ST).
- HR: full-time BBA, BSW, BBM, BBS or BMS with 65% (60% for SC/ST).
- Finance: full-time **B.Com** with 65% (60% for SC/ST).

**Final-year students can apply** for both posts, but you must show your pass certificate and marksheets at document verification or interview.

## BHEL selection process 2026

**Engineer/Executive Trainee**
1. **Computer Based Exam (CBE)**, marked out of 100. Minimum qualifying marks: **40% for UR/EWS, 30% for OBC/SC/ST.**
2. **Interview**, for candidates shortlisted 1:5 against the vacancies in each discipline and category.
3. **Final merit = 75% CBE + 25% interview.**

**Supervisor Trainee**
1. **CBE**, with the same qualifying marks (40% / 30%).
2. **Document verification**, for candidates shortlisted 1:3. This is only qualifying.
3. **Final merit is on CBE marks alone.** No interview for Supervisor Trainees.

The CBE is bilingual (Hindi or English), except the English section. The detailed syllabus and section-wise pattern aren't in the advertisement yet. BHEL says details will be on careers.bhel.in. The advertisement does confirm an English section. I'll add the full pattern here once BHEL publishes it.

**Important:** the exam for the same discipline in ET and ST is on the same day, and you can appear only once. If you're a B.Tech graduate, apply for ET, not ST.

### Two things to read before you apply

- **Service bond:** 3 years after training. If you leave early, you pay **₹5 lakh (ET)** or **₹3 lakh (ST)**, and you need a surety bond for the same amount.
- **10-year posting rule:** you must serve at your allotted unit or Power Sector region for at least 10 years. No transfer requests in that time. Choose your unit preferences in the form carefully.

## How to apply for BHEL recruitment 2026

1. Go to [careers.bhel.in](https://careers.bhel.in/) and open the 2027 ET or ST recruitment link under Current Openings.
2. Read the detailed advertisement for your post (02/2026 for ET, 03/2026 for ST).
3. Register with a valid email ID and mobile number. Keep the email active for a year; BHEL sends updates only by email.
4. Fill in your details exactly as in your Class 10 certificate. **Name, category, post and email can't be changed later.**
5. Choose up to 5 exam cities and your unit/site preferences.
6. Upload your photo, signature and documents (OBC-NCL certificate issued on or after 1 January 2026, EWS certificate for 2026-27, PwD certificate if needed).
7. Pay the fee online and download the final form.

If you submit more than once, only the last application counts. Government and PSU employees must apply through proper channel or bring an NOC at the interview or document verification.

## Should you apply?

If you're a B.Tech graduate or final-year student under 27, **yes**. BHEL hiring Engineer Trainees without GATE is rare, and ₹12 lakh CTC with a PSU tag is a strong first job. Mechanical candidates have the best odds with 80 posts. IT/CS has only 10 seats, all in Delhi, so expect very high cut-offs.

Diploma holders should look hard at the Supervisor Trainee posts. 103 Mechanical seats with ₹7.5 lakh CTC is better than most private diploma jobs. But check the **65% diploma aggregate** first; many people miss this.

Be honest with yourself about the 10-year posting rule. You might land in a power plant site far from home. If that's a deal-breaker, don't apply only to quit and pay the bond.
