**RRC Northern Railway has notified 4,651 Act Apprentice seats, and the online form opens on 11 November 2026 on rrcnr.org.** The last date is **11 December 2026 (11:59 pm)**. You need **10th with at least 50% marks plus an ITI** in the matching trade.

The best part: **there is no exam**. The merit list is made from your 10th and ITI marks. So if your marks are good, this is one of the easiest railway training seats you can get this year.

**Important:** if you apply in a cluster or unit that has no seats for your trade, your form is rejected and the fee is not refunded.

## Eligibility for Northern Railway Apprentice 2026

### Qualification

- Passed **10th (or equivalent) with at least 50% aggregate** marks from a recognised board, **and**
- Passed **ITI in the relevant trade** (NCVT or SCVT).

Both must be passed **by 6 November 2026**, the notification date. If your 10th or ITI result is still awaited, you can't apply this time.

### Age limit (as on 11 December 2026)

| Category | Age |
|---|---|
| General / EWS | 15 to under 24 years |
| OBC | up to 3 years extra |
| SC / ST | up to 5 years extra |
| PwBD | up to 10 years extra |
| Ex-Servicemen | service period + 3 years (up to 10 extra) |

In simple terms, a General candidate should be born between 12 December 2002 and 11 December 2011.

## Selection process: how the merit list is made

There is **no written test and no viva**. Selection goes like this:

1. **Screening of applications.**
2. **Merit list:** RRC takes your 10th percentage and your ITI percentage and averages them, with equal weight.
3. **Document verification** at the division, with originals.
4. **Medical certificate** from a government doctor (Assistant Surgeon or above), in the format under the Apprentices Act.

**Example:** 10th = 78%, ITI = 86%. Your merit score = (78 + 86) ÷ 2 = **82%**.

If two candidates have the same score, the **older** one gets preference. If the date of birth is also the same, the one who passed 10th first goes ahead.

A few things trip people up:

- **CGPA boards:** convert your grades to marks before you enter them. For grade-based boards, RRC uses the midpoint of each grade range and treats each subject out of 100. If the marks you enter don't match your marksheet at document verification, you are rejected.
- **ITI marks:** use the marks in your consolidated mark sheet for all semesters, or in your provisional National Trade Certificate.

## Stipend and training period

The notice only says the training period and stipend are **"as per extant rules"**, meaning the rates under the Apprentices Act. It doesn't give a rupee figure, so I'm not putting one here.

Two honest points:

- **This is training, not a job.** The notice clearly says finishing the apprenticeship gives you no right to a railway job.
- **No hostel.** You have to arrange your own stay near the unit for the whole training period.

Still, hands-on work in a railway shed or workshop, plus the Act Apprentice certificate, is useful when you later apply for RRB Group D or ALP. Check our [RRB ALP admit card post](/rrb-alp-admit-card-2026) to see how the ALP exam works.

## How to apply for RRC NR Apprentice 2026

The link goes live on **11 November 2026**. Keep these ready first:

- 10th certificate and marksheet (also used as DOB proof)
- ITI consolidated marksheet / provisional NTC with marks
- NCVT/SCVT trade certificate
- Caste certificate in the Central Govt format (OBC: non-creamy layer certificate issued on or after 1 January 2026)
- PwBD or Ex-Servicemen certificate, if you claim it
- Photo, signature and **thumb impression**, each in **JPG, 10 to 50 KB**

Steps:

1. Go to [rrcnr.org](https://rrcnr.org) and open the "Engagement of Act Apprentice" link.
2. **Register** with your name, father's name, community, mobile number, email and date of birth. You get a password by email and SMS.
3. **Log in** and fill the application form. Pick your cluster, units and trade carefully.
4. **Pay ₹100** online (if you have to pay).
5. **Upload** your photo, signature, thumb impression and certificates.
6. Submit and **print** the application and the fee receipt.

You **can't edit after the final submission**, and you can apply **only once**. If you send more than one form, all of them are rejected. The notice also asks you not to wait for the last few days, because the site slows down.

## Should you apply?

**Yes, if you have 10th + ITI and you are under 24.** It costs ₹100 (or nothing), there is no exam, and Lucknow, Delhi and Ambala together have over 3,800 seats.

Your chances depend almost fully on your marks. Somebody with 85%+ average in Fitter or Electrician is in a strong position. If your average is in the 60s, still apply, especially in a smaller trade or unit (Painter, Blacksmith, Mason, Machinist), where fewer people compete.

**My tip:** apply in the cluster you can actually live in for the training period. There's no hostel, and you can't change the division later. A seat at Jagadhari doesn't help you if you can't stay in Yamuna Nagar.

If you have an ITI and are looking at other options this month, [URSC (ISRO) Technician B](/ursc-recruitment-2026) closes on 13 November, and [RRB NTPC Undergraduate](/rrb-ntpc-undergraduate-recruitment-2026) is for 12th pass.
