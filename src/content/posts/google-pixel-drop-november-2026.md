Google's **November 2026 Pixel Drop started rolling out on 10 November**. The headline is that **Gemini Intelligence features from the Pixel 11 now come to the Pixel 10 series**, and older Pixels down to the Pixel 6 get a smarter search.

Google also put its AirTag rival, **Pixel Tag, on sale on 11 November**. In India it costs **₹3,799**, or ₹12,900 for four, on the Google Store.

Here's what's new, which phone gets it, and what actually works in India.

| Detail | Information |

| Announced | 10 November 2026, on Google's Keyword blog |
| Rollout | Started 10 November, in stages |

| For older phones | Pixel Search (Pixel 6+), new icon styles (Pixel 6a+) |

So if you have a Pixel 7, 8 or 9, this drop gives you Pixel Search and new icons, plus summaries on the Pixel 9. The Gemini features are for the Pixel 10 and Pixel 11.

## The Gemini features, explained simply

**Rambler.** When you type by voice, it removes "um" and "uh" and turns rambling speech into clean text. If you send long voice-typed messages on WhatsApp, this is the one you'll notice every day.

**Multi-step tasks.** You ask Gemini to do a job across apps, like ordering groceries, booking a ride, ordering takeout or reserving a table, and it goes through the steps for you. Google lists **India** among the first three countries, with the UK and Japan. Which Indian apps it works with best, Google hasn't listed. Try it with the apps you already use and check every order before it's placed.

**Proactive suggestions.** The phone offers things before you ask, like sharing your ETA when you're heading somewhere, or dinner ideas. Useful for some people, noise for others. You can turn suggestions off.

**Custom widgets.** You describe a widget in plain words ("a countdown to my exam", "my monthly spending") and Gemini builds it. Google says it can pull in flight and booking details on its own.

**Conversation summaries.** Long chats get a short summary at the top. **Hindi** is one of the new languages, so this is one of the few features in this drop that Indian users get with their own language.

## For older Pixels: Pixel Search and icons

**Pixel Search** is the most useful change for the most people. From Pixel 6 up, you can search your phone in normal language and it looks through photos, emails, messages, calendar events and settings together. "Photos from Goa trip" or "Wi-Fi hotspot setting" should both work.

**Custom icon styles** (Pixel 6a and newer) let you pick a style that changes all your home screen icons at once, so they match. Small, but people have asked for this for years.

## Pixel 11 only: Call Screen, Pixel Solver, HiLight

- **Call Screen upgrade:** Gemini talks to unknown callers in a more natural way and shows you a live transcript. Google lists this for the US.
- **Pixel Solver:** the Calculator app can now solve maths problems step by step with AI, in select languages. Students on a Pixel 11 might find it handy for checking work, but don't use it in place of learning the method.
- **HiLight:** on the Pixel 11 Pro models, the back glows when a favourite contact messages you while the phone is face down.

**Gemini making calls for you** (to book a table or check if a shop has something in stock) is **US and English only** for now. If you see Indian posts saying it's live here, it isn't.

## Pixel Tag: price and features in India

Pixel Tag is Google's first item tracker. You stick it on keys, a bag or a wallet, and find it through Google's **Find Hub** app.

| Pixel Tag | Details (Google Store India) |
|---|---|
| Price | **₹3,799** (1-pack), **₹12,900** (4-pack) |
| Colour | Fog |
| Battery | User-replaceable CR2032 coin cell, over a year |
| Water and dust | IP67 (1 metre, up to 30 minutes) |
| Weight | 11.8 g with battery |
| Works with | Android 9 and later |
| Precision finding | Needs a phone with Ultra-Wideband (UWB) |
| Warranty | 1 year |

The Find Hub network uses over a billion Android phones to spot your tag when it's far away. Close by, you can ring it, and phones with UWB show you which way to walk. It also warns you if you leave something behind.

Is it worth ₹3,799? If you lose keys or a bag often and own an Android phone, yes. It's thin enough for a wallet and the battery is a coin cell you can replace yourself, not a throw-away. If you just want a tracker for luggage and don't need precision finding, compare it with cheaper Find Hub tags from other brands first.

## How to get the November Pixel Drop

1. Open **Settings > System > Software updates**.
2. Tap **Check for update** and install if one is waiting.
3. Some features (like Rambler and widgets) also come through Play Store app updates, so update Gboard, Google and Phone apps too.

Google rolls these out in stages. If a feature isn't on your phone yet, it isn't broken. Give it one or two weeks.

## My take

If you're on a **Pixel 10**, this is a big update. You get most of what Google sold as Pixel 11 features, a year early. That's a good reason not to upgrade yet.

If you're on a **Pixel 7, 8 or 9**, the drop is modest, but Pixel Search alone makes it worth installing.

And if you were thinking of buying a Pixel mainly for AI calling features, wait. The best bits are US-only right now.
