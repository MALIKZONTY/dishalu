**Jailer 2 has opened big.** Rajinikanth's sequel earned about **₹59.70 crore net in India** on day 1 (Thursday, 15 October 2026), and about **₹125 crore gross worldwide**, going by trade tracker Sacnilk's estimates as of 10:43 PM IST. That's well ahead of the first Jailer's opening.

Below: the Jailer 2 box office collection day 1 by language and state, footfalls, how it compares with Jailer (2023), what audiences are saying, and where Ranabaali stands before its Friday release.

**Note:** all numbers here are trade estimates. Sun Pictures hasn't shared an official day 1 figure yet. Final numbers usually settle the next morning, and I'll update this post then.

| Detail | Jailer 2, Day 1 (15 Oct) |
|---|---|
| India net | **₹59.70 crore** |
| India gross | ₹70.25 crore |
| Overseas gross | ₹55 crore |
| Worldwide gross | **₹125.25 crore** |
| Footfalls (India) | **About 37.47 lakh** |
| Shows tracked | 16,599 |
| Overall occupancy | 55.1% |
| Day 1 advance booking | About 18 lakh tickets, ₹37.6 crore gross |
| Official figure from makers | Not out yet |

*Source: Sacnilk, data as of 10:43 PM IST, 15 October 2026. Estimates, not producer figures.*

## Jailer 2 box office collection day 1: language-wise

Tamil carried the film, as expected. Telugu was strong too, even with Ranabaali opening the next day.

| Language | Net collection | Shows | Occupancy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tamil | ₹43.35 crore | 9,326 | 70.8% |
| Telugu | ₹12.50 crore | 2,792 | 56.2% |
| Hindi | ₹3.50 crore | 4,371 | 21.0% |
| Kannada | ₹0.35 crore | 110 | 54.0% |
| **Total** | **₹59.70 crore** | **16,599** | **55.1%** |

The weak spot is Hindi. "Rajini The Jailer 2" got more than 4,000 shows but filled only about 1 in 5 seats. The Tamil version is doing the heavy lifting in Karnataka and Kerala too, which is why the Kannada version's number looks tiny.

## State-wise gross and footfalls

| State | Day 1 gross |
|---|---|
| Tamil Nadu | ₹24 crore |
| Andhra Pradesh + Telangana | ₹14.50 crore |
| Karnataka | ₹14.50 crore |
| Kerala | ₹12.50 crore |
| Rest of India | ₹4.75 crore |
| **India total** | **₹70.25 crore** |

**Kerala is the surprise.** ₹12.50 crore is more than double what the first Jailer made there on day 1. Mohanlal's role is the most likely reason.

**Footfalls:** about **37.47 lakh people** watched Jailer 2 in Indian theatres on day 1, as per Sacnilk. Roughly half of them had booked in advance: pre-sales for the first day closed at about 18 lakh tickets (Koimoi puts it at 17.94 lakh, leaving out block seats).

## Jailer 2 vs Jailer: day 1 comparison

The first Jailer (10 August 2023) was a huge hit, so this is the comparison fans care about. Both sets of numbers below are Sacnilk's.

| Day 1 | Jailer (2023) | Jailer 2 (2026) |
|---|---|---|
| India net | ₹48.35 crore | **₹59.70 crore** |
| Tamil Nadu gross | ₹23.40 crore | ₹24 crore |
| AP + Telangana gross | ₹12.50 crore | ₹14.50 crore |
| Karnataka gross | ₹11.80 crore | ₹14.50 crore |
| Kerala gross | ₹5.90 crore | ₹12.50 crore |

So Jailer 2 opened about **23% higher in India**. Tamil Nadu is almost flat. The growth came from Kerala, Karnataka and the Telugu states.

## Jailer 2 public talk and reviews

The word of mouth is **mixed to positive**. Here's what most first-day viewers agree on (no spoilers):

**What's working**
- Rajinikanth's screen presence and mass scenes, especially the interval block
- Anirudh's background score, which gets the most praise of anything in the film
- Nelson's comedy in the first half
- The reported surprise cameos, which drew big cheers in theatres

**What isn't**
- The second half slows down and loses focus, a common complaint
- The villains feel weak next to the first film
- Some hero-elevation scenes feel familiar

**Spoiler warning:** cameo names are already trending on social media. If you want to be surprised, mute "Jailer 2" keywords until you watch it.

## Ranabaali day 1: where it stands before release

Vijay Deverakonda's **Ranabaali** releases tomorrow, **Friday 16 October**, so there's no collection figure yet. Here's its advance booking, as per Sacnilk at 9:48 PM IST on 15 October:

| Ranabaali, Day 1 pre-sales (India) | Figure |
|---|---|
| Gross (with block seats) | **₹3.89 crore** |
| Gross (without block seats) | ₹3.32 crore |
| Tickets sold | 2.44 lakh |
| Shows | 2,643 |
| Telangana | ₹1.91 crore |
| Andhra Pradesh | ₹1.51 crore |
| Top city | Hyderabad, ₹1.36 crore |

Overseas, TrackTollywood puts Ranabaali's worldwide pre-sales at about ₹8.5 crore gross, and Koimoi reports USA sales past **$200K** including premieres.

Put that next to Jailer 2: the Rajini film's **Telugu version alone made about ₹12.50 crore net** on day 1. Ranabaali's full day 1 pre-sales are less than a third of that. Telugu films do sell a lot of tickets at the counter on release day, so expect the final day 1 figure to be much higher than its pre-sales. But Jailer 2 has clearly taken the first round in the Telugu states.

The real test for Ranabaali is tonight's USA premiere talk and Friday's first shows. A period drama like this needs good word of mouth more than a big opening. ## What to watch for next

- **Day 2 (Friday):** Jailer 2 faces Ranabaali in the Telugu states, but it's a working day elsewhere, so some drop from the opening day is normal. How small that drop is will tell you more than the day 1 number.
- **Opening weekend:** the Dussehra holiday week should help both films. Jailer 2's long weekend total will show whether the mixed second-half talk is hurting it.
- **Official numbers:** Sun Pictures usually posts collection posters on its social accounts. When it does, treat those as "as claimed by the makers".

Planning to watch? Book only on official apps or at the theatre counter. "FDFS tickets available" offers on WhatsApp and Instagram are a common scam during big releases. For the trailer links, cast and certificate, see our [Jailer 2 release date and trailer post](/jailer-2-release-date-trailer).

## My take

A ₹125 crore worldwide start in a crowded week is a strong result. The occupancy says Tamil Nadu and Kerala audiences turned up in full, and Hindi didn't.

What decides the final total now is the second half. If you go in expecting a fun Rajini outing and not a tighter film than the first Jailer, you'll probably enjoy it. If you were on the fence about Ranabaali, wait for Friday afternoon's talk before booking. Shows will be easy to get.
