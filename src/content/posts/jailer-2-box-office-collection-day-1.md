**Update (19 Oct, 8:30 AM):** Jailer 2 has **crossed ₹400 crore worldwide in 4 days**. Sacnilk puts day 4 (Sunday) at about **₹55.85 crore net in India**, up 18% from Saturday. The 4-day India net total is about **₹199.90 crore**, just short of ₹200 crore, and the worldwide gross is about **₹424.25 crore**. Day 4 details are [below](#jailer-2-box-office-collection-day-4). Monday (day 5) is the next big test.

**Jailer 2 has opened big.** Rajinikanth's sequel earned about **₹59.70 crore net in India** on day 1 (Thursday, 15 October 2026), and about **₹127 crore gross worldwide**, going by trade tracker Sacnilk's final day 1 estimates. That's well ahead of the first Jailer's opening.

Below: the Jailer 2 box office collection from day 1 to day 4, by language and state, footfalls, how it compares with Jailer (2023), what audiences are saying, and where Ranabaali stands.

**Note:** all numbers here are trade estimates. Sun Pictures hasn't shared an official collection figure yet.

**What Sunday tells you:**
- **Tamil occupancy hit 80%.** That's as good as day 1, so the mixed talk about the second half isn't keeping Tamil audiences away.

- **Hindi is still small but rising every day.** ₹5.75 crore is its best day yet.

**What the drop tells you:** India net fell about 40% from day 1. That sounds big, but a day 1 that was boosted by fan shows and an 18-lakh-ticket advance always falls hard on a working Friday. **The Telugu number matters most.** On the day Ranabaali released, Jailer 2's Telugu version still made about ₹7.25 crore net with 54% occupancy. That's a solid hold against a local star's film.

Overseas is the other big story. ₹97 crore in 2 days means foreign markets are giving almost as much as India gross.

## Jailer 2 public talk and reviews

The word of mouth is **mixed to positive**. Here's what most first-day viewers agree on (no spoilers):

**What's working**
- Rajinikanth's screen presence and mass scenes, especially the interval block
- Anirudh's background score, which gets the most praise of anything in the film
- Nelson's comedy in the first half
- The reported surprise cameos, which drew big cheers in theatres

**What isn't**
- The second half slows down and loses focus, a common complaint
- The villains feel weak next to the first film
- Some hero-elevation scenes feel familiar

The worrying sign: Ranabaali **dropped about 21% on Saturday**, a day when most films go up, and grew only about 3% on Sunday. On the same Sunday, Jailer 2's Telugu version alone made ₹9.50 crore, more than Ranabaali's full India number.

Counter sales did lift it: a ₹9.25 crore net day 1 is more than double its pre-sales. But Jailer 2 has clearly taken the first round in the Telugu states.

Sunday was supposed to be Ranabaali's comeback day, and it barely moved. Unless Monday holds close to ₹5 crore, the second week will be hard. A period drama like this needs good word of mouth more than a big opening. ## What to watch for next

- **₹200 crore India net:** at ₹199.90 crore after Sunday, Jailer 2 will cross it early on Monday.
- **Monday test (day 5, 19 Oct):** the first working day after the weekend shows the real hold. A drop of 50% or less from Sunday's ₹55.85 crore (so ₹28 crore or more) would be good.
- **Opening weekend:** the Dussehra holiday week should help both films. Jailer 2's long weekend total will show whether the mixed second-half talk is hurting it.
- **Official numbers:** Sun Pictures usually posts collection posters on its social accounts. When it does, treat those as "as claimed by the makers".

Planning to watch? Book only on official apps or at the theatre counter. "FDFS tickets available" offers on WhatsApp and Instagram are a common scam during big releases. For the trailer links, cast and certificate, see our [Jailer 2 release date and trailer post](/jailer-2-release-date-trailer).

## My take

A ₹127 crore worldwide start, and ₹424 crore in 4 days, is a strong result in a crowded week. The occupancy says Tamil Nadu and Kerala audiences turned up in full, and Hindi didn't.

What decides the final total now is the second half. If you go in expecting a fun Rajini outing and not a tighter film than the first Jailer, you'll probably enjoy it. If you were on the fence about Ranabaali, shows are easy to get, so there's no rush. Go by what friends who've seen it say.
