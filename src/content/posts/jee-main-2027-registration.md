**Update (13 November 2026):** NTA's information bulletin is out. The exam dates have moved slightly from the September calendar (it said 22–30 January; now it is 20–28 January). Don't wait for the late-fee window. It costs ₹500 more, and the site is slowest in the last few days.

## Documents to keep ready

NTA pulls your **name, date of birth, gender, photo and address directly from Aadhaar**. If your Aadhaar doesn't match your Class 10 certificate, you'll have trouble at the form stage.

Check these before you fill the form:

- **Aadhaar card:** name and date of birth exactly as in your Class 10 certificate, with a recent photo. Updating Aadhaar can take a couple of weeks, so start now if anything is wrong.
- **Category certificate (EWS / SC / ST / OBC-NCL):** must be valid and current. An old OBC-NCL or EWS certificate is a common problem.
- **UDID card** (for PwD candidates): valid and renewed.
- **Class 10 marksheet or certificate:** scanned as a PDF.
- **Class 12 roll number:** if you're writing boards in 2027, you'll enter your roll or registration number later.
- **Your own mobile number and email:** last year these could **not** be changed in the correction window. Don't use a cyber café's email.

### Photo and file sizes

| File | Format | Size |
|---|---|---|

| PwD / UDID certificate | PDF | 50–300 KB |

You also have to take a **live photo** with your webcam or phone camera while filling the form. Last year, students whose live photo didn't match UIDAI's record had to get a certificate attested by their principal or a gazetted officer before the exam. Use good light and a plain wall.

## Eligibility: who can apply

From the JEE Main 2027 information bulletin:

- **No age limit.** Your Class 12 passing year matters, not your age.
- You can apply if you passed Class 12 in **2025 or 2026**, or are **appearing in 2027**. If you passed in **2024 or earlier**, you can't.
- "Passing year" means the first year you were declared pass. An improvement exam doesn't reset it.
- A 3-year diploma recognised by AICTE or a state technical board also counts as a qualifying exam.

Individual colleges (NITs, IIITs, state colleges) can have their own age or marks rules for admission. That's separate from sitting the exam.

## Application fee

The 2027 fee is the same as last year. This is **per session**, for centres in India:

| Category | Paper 1 only (BE/BTech) | Paper 1 + Paper 2 |
|---|---|---|
| General male | ₹1,000 | ₹2,000 |
| General female | ₹800 | ₹1,600 |
| EWS / OBC-NCL male | ₹900 | ₹2,000 |
| EWS / OBC-NCL female | ₹800 | ₹1,600 |
| SC / ST / PwD (male or female) | ₹500 | ₹1,000 |
| Third gender | ₹500 | ₹1,000 |

Processing charges and GST are extra. Pay online by UPI, card or net banking. **Pay only once.** NTA cancels the result if one candidate submits more than one form.

## How to register for JEE Main 2027 (step by step)

1. Go to **jeemain.nta.nic.in** and click "Online Application Form for JEE (Main) 2027 – Session 1".
2. Register with your own mobile number and email. Verify with Aadhaar. You'll get an **Application Number**. Note it down; it's used for both sessions.
3. Log in and fill the form: personal details, paper (BE/BTech, BArch or BPlan), qualification details and **4 exam cities**.
4. Take the live photo, then upload your photo, signature and Class 10 PDF.
5. Pay the fee and download the confirmation page. Keep it until results.

**Choosing cities:** you can only pick cities in the states of your present or permanent address. Put the nearest one first. NTA tries to follow your order but can allot another city.

**Session 2:** if you apply for Session 1 now, you just log in and pay the Session 2 fee between 5 January and 3 February 2027. You can also register only for Session 2 in that window.

## Common mistakes that cause trouble later

- **Name mismatch** between Aadhaar and Class 10. Fix Aadhaar before you apply.
- **Wrong category.** Ticking OBC-NCL with an expired certificate creates problems at counselling.
- **Blurry or dark photo.** It's the top reason for live-photo mismatch.
- **Leaving it to the last 2 days.** The site slows down near the deadline, and NTA didn't extend it last year.
- **Assuming you can fix everything later.** The correction window is only 27 to 29 November, and some fields can't be changed at all.

## JEE Main exam pattern (Paper 1, BE/BTech)

As per the information bulletin:

| Subject | Section A (MCQ) | Section B (numerical) | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Physics | 20 | 5 | 100 |
| Chemistry | 20 | 5 | 100 |
| Mathematics | 20 | 5 | 100 |
| **Total** | **60** | **15** | **300** |

- **+4** for a correct answer, **−1** for a wrong one, in **both** sections. Numerical answers also carry negative marking now.
- 3 hours. Shifts are 9 AM–12 noon and 3–6 PM.
- Paper 2A (BArch) and 2B (BPlan) have Maths (25 questions), an aptitude test (50 questions) and either drawing or planning questions.

## Should you write both sessions?

Yes, if you can. NTA takes the **better total score** of the two sessions for your rank, so Session 1 costs you nothing extra except the fee.

Here's how I'd look at it. Treat January as a real exam, not practice. But if it goes badly, you know your weak areas with two months still left before April. Students who skip January lose that feedback.

The only case for skipping Session 1 is if your Class 12 board preparation is far behind and January would hurt your boards. Even then, register in Session 1 if the fee is not a problem for your family; you can decide later.

If you're aiming for IITs, remember that JEE Main is also the qualifying exam for JEE Advanced. Four years from now, the same NTA-style process will come back with exams like [GATE 2027](/gate-2027-registration-last-date) for M.Tech and PSU jobs.
