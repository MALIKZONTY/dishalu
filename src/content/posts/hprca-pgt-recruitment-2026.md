Himachal Pradesh Rajya Chayan Aayog (HPRCA), Hamirpur has released **Advertisement No. 14/2026** for **2,423 subject teacher posts** in government CBSE-affiliated schools of Himachal Pradesh. The online form opens on **10 November 2026** and closes on **5 December 2026 at 11:59 PM**.

You need a master's degree in your subject, a B.Ed and a TET pass for most subjects. But read this first: **these are not regular PGT lecturer posts.** They're 5-year, scheme-based engagements on a fixed ₹30,000 a month. More on that below.

## What kind of job is this? (Read before applying)

- **Scheme-based, not regular.** The posts come under the HP government's scheme for engaging teachers in CBSE-affiliated government schools on a fixed honorarium. English and Maths fall under a separate sub-scheme.
- **Temporary for 5 years,** not against any sanctioned post, with **no right to regularisation**.
- **Not a government employee.** CCS/CCA rules, leave rules, pension rules and government medical reimbursement don't apply.
- **Paid for 10 months.** The ₹30,000 is a fixed honorarium paid for 10 months in an academic year.
- **Posting anywhere** in CBSE-affiliated or other government schools.

So think of it as a 5-year contract teaching job with a decent monthly amount, not a permanent lecturer post.

## Eligibility

### Age limit

You must be **between 18 and 45 years on 1 January 2026**. The advertisement doesn't list any category-wise age relaxation for these posts, so don't assume one.

### Himachal school rule (important for outsiders)

The notice says you're eligible for appointment only if you **passed Matriculation and 10+2 from a school or institution in Himachal Pradesh**. This condition doesn't apply to bonafide Himachalis.

Reservation (SC, ST, OBC, EWS, BPL, WFF) is only for bonafide residents of Himachal Pradesh. Reserved category candidates from other states are treated as General.

### Educational qualification

**English and Mathematics:**

- M.A./M.Sc. in the subject plus B.Ed (integrated B.A./B.Sc./B.Com B.Ed accepted), with the subject compulsory in B.Ed.
- 50% in graduation (including B.Ed) and **55% in the subject at graduation** (SC/ST/OBC: 45% and 50%).
- TET pass from HP Board of School Education, Dharamshala, or another government-authorised agency.

**Hindi, Political Science, History, Economics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Sanskrit, Commerce:**

- Master's degree in the subject with at least 50% marks.
- B.Ed with at least 50% marks and a TET pass (HP Board or another authorised agency).
- Proficiency in teaching Hindi and English.
- Up to 5% relaxation in minimum marks for SC/ST/OBC/PWD of Himachal Pradesh.

Some subjects accept related master's degrees:

- **Economics:** Applied or Business Economics.
- **Physics:** Electronics or Nuclear Physics.
- **Chemistry:** Biochemistry.
- **Biology:** Botany, Zoology, Life Science, Microbiology, Biotechnology, Molecular Biology or Plant Physiology, provided you studied Botany and Zoology at graduation.
- **Commerce:** Accountancy, Financial Accountancy and Income Tax, or MFC.

**Music:** Master's in Vocal or Instrumental music with 50%, plus B.Ed (50%) and TET.

**Computer:** Any one of these, with 50% marks:

- B.E./B.Tech in CS or IT.
- B.E./B.Tech (any stream) plus a PG Diploma in Computer Science/Applications.
- M.Sc. (CS) or MCA.
- PG in Maths or Physics plus a PG Diploma in Computer Science/Applications.
- DOEACC "B" or "C" level.

**Physical Education:** B.P.Ed/BPE/D.P.Ed (as per NCTE norms) **and** a 2-year M.P.Ed or M.A. Physical Education.

**Fine Arts:** Master's in Fine/Visual Arts with 50%, or 10+2 plus a 5-year full-time Fine Art diploma with 50%.

All qualifications must be complete by the closing date, 5 December 2026.

## Selection process and exam pattern

Selection is through a **computer based test (CBT) or written/OMR screening test**, followed by document verification. There's no interview mentioned.

| Part | Details |
|---|---|
| Questions | 120 multiple-choice questions |
| Marks | 120 (1 mark each) |
| Time | 1.5 hours |
| Subject part | Your subject, up to the level of the required qualification |
| General part | General knowledge (including Himachal GK and current affairs), everyday science, logical reasoning, social science, general English and Hindi of matric standard |

**Minimum qualifying marks:** 45% for General and 40% for SC/ST/OBC/WFF. If too few candidates qualify, HPRCA may relax this by up to 5%.

Multi-shift scores are normalised by the Z-score method. You get 7 days to object to the provisional answer key, at ₹200 per question. The detailed syllabus is in the download section of the HPRCA website.

## Salary

The pay is a **fixed ₹30,000 per month** for 10 months a year, so ₹3 lakh a year. The notice mentions no pay level, DA or increment. Compare that with [UP TGT 2026](/up-tgt-recruitment-2026), a regular post on Pay Level 7.

## How to apply for HPRCA PGT 2026

1. Go to the official HPRCA portal at [hprca.hp.gov.in](https://hprca.hp.gov.in) and click **Sign Up** to create a username and password.
2. Complete the **One-Time Registration (OTR)**: personal details, qualifications, experience (if any), photo, signature and documents.
3. Upload a passport-size photo **not older than one month** and your signature (JPG/JPEG/PNG, max 1 MB each). Enter your name and date of birth exactly as on your matric certificate.
4. Once your profile is complete, open the list of posts and click **Apply** next to the subject you want.
5. Pick your preferred exam district, check the auto-filled form and pay ₹800 online.
6. Save and print the submitted form. You'll need it, with original certificates, at document verification.

One form per post only. Regular government employees must inform their Head of Office in writing.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Applying without TET.** 12 of the 15 subjects need it by 5 December.
- **Expired category certificate.** OBC, EWS and BPL certificates must be valid on 5 December and at document verification. BPL ones last only six months.
- **Waiting for the last day.** Government portals slow down badly in the last 48 hours.

## Should you apply?

Honestly, it depends on where you are right now.

**Apply if** you have a master's, B.Ed and HP TET, and you're sitting at home or working in a low-paying private school. ₹30,000 a month in a government CBSE school is a clear step up. It also adds 5 years of government school teaching to your profile, which helps in later regular recruitments.

**Think twice if** you're already in a regular or better-paid job. This contract has no increments, no pension and no promise of regularisation.

The fee is ₹800 with no concession, so it's not cheap if you plan to apply for two subjects. Pick the one where your marks and TET are strongest.

If you're applying, start revising Himachal GK now. It's part of the general section, and many candidates from science and commerce backgrounds lose easy marks there.

Next step: sign up and finish your One-Time Registration profile on the HPRCA portal early, so you only have to click Apply and pay once the form opens on 10 November.
