## PM Kisan 24th installment date: what's confirmed and what isn't

Here's what is official:

- PM Kisan pays ₹2,000 every four months, in three periods: April–July, August–November and December–March.
- The 23rd installment (April–July 2026-27 period) was released by the Prime Minister on 20 June 2026 from Tarakeswar, Hooghly, West Bengal. [PIB says](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2275744) over ₹18,880 crore went to about 9.44 crore farmers, including 2.18 crore women.
- So the 24th installment is the one for the **August–November 2026** period.

What is **not** official: any exact date. Not 20 October (that day has passed with no release), not "before Diwali", not any date a YouTube thumbnail shows. When the date is fixed, it will appear on pmkisan.gov.in and in a PIB press release, usually a few days before a big event where the Prime Minister presses the button.

## Who gets the 24th installment

All landholding farmer families get PM Kisan, whatever the size of the land. A "family" here means husband, wife and minor children. Your state government verifies who is eligible.

**You are not eligible if anyone in your family is:**

- A present or former holder of a constitutional post, minister, MP, MLA, MLC, mayor of a municipal corporation or chairperson of a district panchayat
- A serving or retired central/state government or PSU employee (Group D, Class IV and Multi Tasking Staff are allowed)
- A retired pensioner getting ₹10,000 or more a month (again, Group D/Class IV/MTS are allowed)
- Someone who paid income tax in the last assessment year
- A registered doctor, engineer, lawyer, chartered accountant or architect who is practising

Institutional land holders are also out.

**Important:** the portal says payments for some "suspected cases" have been **held until physical verification**. Examples it gives are farmers who got land ownership after 1 February 2019, and families where more than one member is getting the benefit (like both husband and wife). If your last installment stopped without any reason, this could be why.

## How to complete PM Kisan e-KYC

e-KYC is mandatory. Without it, your installment won't be sent. There are three ways to do it, according to the official [e-KYC note](https://pmkisan.gov.in/Documents/Note-on-Modes-and-processes-of-ekyc-13th-Nov-English.pdf).

**1. OTP on the website (free)**

You need a mobile number linked to your Aadhaar.

1. Open [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in/).
2. Click **e-KYC** (top right).
3. Enter your Aadhaar number, then the OTP. Done.

**2. Face authentication on the app (free)**

Good if your mobile isn't linked to Aadhaar.

1. Install the **PM-KISAN** app and the **Aadhaar Face RD** app from the Google Play Store.
2. Log in with your PM Kisan registered mobile number.
3. On the beneficiary status page, if e-KYC shows "No", tap e-KYC, enter your Aadhaar and allow the face scan.

**3. Fingerprint at a CSC (₹15)**

Go to your nearest Common Service Centre or State Seva Kendra with your Aadhaar card and your Aadhaar-linked mobile. The official fee is **₹15**. If the operator asks for ₹100 or ₹200, that's more than the official fee. You can find a CSC at [locator.csccloud.in](https://locator.csccloud.in/).

The new status shows up in your beneficiary status **after 24 hours**, whichever way you do it. Don't panic if it still shows "No" the same evening.

## How to check your PM Kisan status

1. Go to [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in/) and click **Know Your Status**.
2. Enter your registration number. Don't know it? Click **Know Your Registration No.** and search by mobile or Aadhaar.
3. Enter the OTP sent to your registered mobile.
4. You'll see your eligibility, e-KYC status and the payment details of each installment (bank, UTR number, date credited).

You can also check through the PM-KISAN app, or ask the Kisan e-Mitra chatbot on the portal.

## Common problems and fixes

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| e-KYC shows "No" | Do OTP or face e-KYC, or visit a CSC. Recheck after 24 hours. |
| Bank account not Aadhaar-seeded | Visit your bank branch with Aadhaar and ask them to seed it for DBT. Money is only sent to an Aadhaar-seeded account. |
| Mobile number changed | Use **Update Mobile Number** in the Farmers Corner on the portal. |
| Name or details wrong | Use **Update Missing Information**, or contact your village agriculture officer. |
| "Held for physical verification" | Contact your state's PM Kisan nodal officer (listed under Contact Us on the portal) and keep your land papers ready. |
| Got the 23rd but not the 24th, after the date | Wait 2–3 days, then raise a complaint in **Helpdesk - Query Form** on the portal. |

## Beware of fake links and agents

You'll see many "PM Kisan 24th kist" videos and WhatsApp messages in the next few weeks. Keep these in mind:

- The only official site is **pmkisan.gov.in**. Not .com, not .in with extra words.
- No one needs your bank PIN, ATM card or UPI PIN to "release" your installment.
- e-KYC is free online. At a CSC it costs ₹15, nothing more.
- If someone says "pay ₹500 and your pending installments will come", it's a scam.
