**RRB Paramedical recruitment 2026 (CEN 05/2026) is open for 590 posts, and the last date is 14 October 2026 at 11:59 pm.** That's one week from today. If you hold a GNM or B.Sc Nursing, B.Pharm, DMLT, a radiography diploma or a similar paramedical qualification, you can apply.

Nursing Superintendent alone has 365 posts at Level 7 (₹44,900 starting basic pay). Selection is a single computer-based test (CBT), then document verification and a medical test. No interview.

## Eligibility: qualification for each post

You must have the full qualification by **14 October 2026**. If you're still waiting for your final result, the notice says clearly that you should not apply.

- **Nursing Superintendent:** Registered Nurse and Midwife with a 3-year GNM course from an Indian Nursing Council recognised institution, **or** B.Sc Nursing.
- **Pharmacist (Entry Grade):** B.Pharm (Allopathic) or Pharm.D, **and** registration as a pharmacist under the Pharmacy Act, 1948.
- **Health & Malaria Inspector Gr II:** B.Sc with Chemistry, plus a 1-year Health/Sanitary Inspector diploma or an NCVT trade certificate in Health Sanitary Inspector.
- **Radiographer X-Ray Technician:** 10+2 with Physics and Chemistry plus a 2-year diploma in Radiography / X-Ray Technician / Radiodiagnosis Technology. Several B.Sc radiography and imaging degrees are also accepted.
- **Lab Assistant Grade II:** 10+2 Science plus DMLT (at least 1 year, full time), or a B.Sc in Medical Lab Technology.
- **ECG Technician:** 10+2 or a science degree with a certificate, diploma or degree in ECG or cardiology technology (at least 1 year).
- **Optometrist:** B.Sc Optometry or a 3–4 year Ophthalmic Technician diploma, with council registration.
- **Dialysis Technician:** B.Sc plus a Haemodialysis diploma or 2 years of haemodialysis experience. B.Sc Dialysis Technology also works.
- **Audiologist & Speech Therapist:** BASLP with Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI) registration.

### Age relaxation

Age is counted on **1 January 2027**. The main relaxations on the upper limit:

| Category | Relaxation |
|---|---|
| SC / ST | 5 years |
| OBC (non-creamy layer) | 3 years |
| PwBD | 10 years (UR/EWS), 13 (OBC), 15 (SC/ST) |
| Ex-servicemen | 3 / 6 / 8 years after deducting service |

Serving railway Group C staff, and widowed or divorced women, get separate higher limits. Those are listed in Para 5.2 of the notice.

### Medical standard

Each post has a medical standard (B1, C1 or C2), mainly about eyesight. Nursing Superintendent and ECG Technician need C1. Pharmacist needs C2. Radiographer, Lab Assistant, Optometrist, Dialysis Technician and Audiologist need **B1**, which is the strictest of the three and includes colour vision and night vision tests. If you fail the medical, you don't get a different post instead, so check this before you apply.

## Selection process and exam pattern

1. **CBT (one stage only):** 100 questions, 90 minutes, objective type.
2. **Document verification:** candidates equal to the number of vacancies (1:1) are called, by merit.
3. **Medical examination.**

| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| Professional ability (your subject) | 70 | 70 |
| General Awareness | 10 | 10 |
| Arithmetic, General Intelligence & Reasoning | 10 | 10 |
| General Science (up to Class 10) | 10 | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** |

There's **negative marking of 1/3 mark** per wrong answer. The minimum qualifying marks are 40% for UR and EWS, 30% for OBC and SC, and 25% for ST. You can write the exam in English, Hindi or 13 regional languages, including Telugu, Tamil, Kannada and Malayalam.

The 1:1 shortlisting is the part people miss. There's no extra list of 2 or 3 times the vacancies for document verification, so your CBT rank has to be inside the vacancy count of your chosen RRB.

## How to apply for RRB Paramedical 2026

1. Go to [rrbapply.gov.in](https://www.rrbapply.gov.in/). If you made an account for any RRB exam in 2024, 2025 or 2026, **log in with the same account**. Don't create a new one.
2. If you're new, click "Create an Account". Enter your name and date of birth exactly as on your Class 10 certificate. These details, your mobile number and your email **can never be changed** later.
3. Verify your identity with Aadhaar or DigiLocker. The notice warns that other photo IDs get stricter checks at every stage.
4. Open the CEN 05/2026 form, choose **one RRB**, and give your post and zone preferences. Posts you don't tick are treated as "not interested".
5. Capture a **live photo** through your webcam or phone's front camera. Don't upload an old photo. Wear dark, non-white clothes and no glasses, cap or mask.
6. Upload your signature: black ink on white paper, cursive (not capital letters), JPG, 30–49 KB.
7. SC/ST candidates who want the free train pass for the exam should upload their caste certificate (PDF, under 400 KB).
8. Submit, then pay the fee online by UPI, card or net banking. Your application is accepted only after payment, and the last date for payment is **16 October 2026**.

Stuck? The official helpline is **rrb.help@csc.gov.in** or **9592001188 / 0172-5653333** (10 am to 5 pm, working days). You can also apply through your nearest Common Service Centre (CSC).

## Should you apply?

If you're a GNM or B.Sc Nursing holder under 40, honestly, yes. 365 Nursing Superintendent posts at Level 7 is a good number for railways, and the exam is 70% your own nursing subjects. That suits people who studied seriously in college more than people who only practise general aptitude.

Pharmacists should apply too. 118 posts is decent, though they're spread thinly across RRBs. Check the RRB table first.

For the small posts, think about it carefully. There's 1 Dialysis Technician post (at Chennai), 1 Audiologist post (at Chandigarh) and 2 Optometrist posts (both at Patna). If you don't live near that RRB, remember the notice says no transfer request is even registered for the first 5 to 10 years.

Two common mistakes to avoid:

- **Applying twice** "to be safe". Both get rejected and you can be banned from all RRB exams.
- **Typing a name or date of birth different from your Class 10 certificate.** It can't be fixed later, not even in the correction window.

## What happens next

The CBT date hasn't been announced yet. As a rough guide, in the previous cycle (CEN 03/2025, 434 posts) the CBT happened several months after applications closed. Use the gap to revise your core subjects.

After you apply, watch for the application status, then the exam city slip and the admit card. In recent RRB exams, the city slip came about 10 days before the exam and the admit card about 4 days before. Everything will be on [rrb.indianrailways.gov.in](https://rrb.indianrailways.gov.in/).
