**OTT releases this weekend (13 to 15 November 2026) bring two big October theatre films home.** Rajinikanth's **Jailer 2** lands on Prime Video on Thursday, 12 November, and Vijay Deverakonda's **Ranabaali**, the film that opened against it a day later in theatres, arrives on Netflix on Friday.

Netflix also has Meghna Gulzar's **Daayra**, with Kareena Kapoor Khan and Prithviraj Sukumaran. JioHotstar has the new **Student of the Year** series, and fantasy fans get **The Rings of Power Season 3** on Prime Video from today (11 November). Everything below is spoiler-free.

Ranabaali's date and languages were announced by Netflix on its own social media accounts. Student of the Year: After Dark's date came with JioHotstar's teaser. The rest are as reported by news outlets, so open the app once before you plan the evening. OTT dates sometimes shift by a day.

## Indian OTT releases this weekend

### Jailer 2 (Tamil, Prime Video)

Rajinikanth is back as "Tiger" Muthuvel Pandian, the retired jailer who is pulled into a fight again when a crime syndicate goes after his family. Nelson Dilipkumar directs again, and Sun Pictures produces.

- **Cast:** Rajinikanth, SJ Suryah, Mithun Chakraborty, Vidya Balan
- **Audio:** Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, Malayalam, Kannada (as reported)
- **In theatres:** 15 October 2026

We covered its [opening day box office](/jailer-2-box-office-collection-day-1) last month. That's about four weeks from theatre to OTT. If you skipped it in theatres because of the crowds, this is the easiest pick of the week.

### Ranabaali (Telugu, Netflix)

A period action drama set in the 1870s, with Vijay Deverakonda as a rebel warrior taking on British forces in South India. Rashmika Mandanna plays the lead opposite him, and Rahul Sankrityan (*Shyam Singha Roy*) directs.

- **Audio:** Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada and Malayalam (confirmed by Netflix)
- **In theatres:** 16 October 2026, one day after Jailer 2

It lost the box office clash badly, as we wrote in our [Jailer 2 vs Ranabaali](/jailer-2-vs-ranabaali-box-office-clash) post. That doesn't make it a bad watch. A big-canvas period film often finds a second life on Netflix, especially in the Hindi dub.

### Daayra (Hindi, Netflix)

Meghna Gulzar's (*Raazi*, *Talvar*) crime drama starts with a brutal crime and an alleged police encounter that follows it. Kareena Kapoor Khan plays a DCP who asks whether the encounter was justified; Prithviraj Sukumaran plays the DCP whose actions are under question.

- **In theatres:** 18 September 2026
- **Heads-up:** the story deals with sexual violence. It's not a light family watch.

If you like slow, argument-driven films like *Talvar*, this one is for you.

### Student of the Year: After Dark (Hindi series, JioHotstar)

Not a sequel to the films, but a darker take on the same school world. Shanaya Kapoor makes her OTT debut in a double role: Aayesha, the school's queen bee who is found dead, and her twin Aditi, who joins the Student of the Year contest to find out what really happened. Reema Sengupta directs, and Karan Johar's Dharmatic Entertainment produces.

It's a teen thriller, so set your expectations around *Class*, not *Paatal Lok*.

### Bandra (Malayalam, ZEE5)

Dileep and Tamannaah Bhatia star in Arun Gopy's action drama, a mystery set around a 1990s Bollywood actress. ZEE5 is reported to stream it from 13 November.

### Mrigaya 1.5: The Hunt Continues (Bengali, ZEE5)

A Kolkata Police crime thriller with Ritwick Chakraborty and Koushani Mukherjee. Good pick if you watch Bengali procedurals.

## International OTT releases this weekend

### The Rings of Power Season 3 (Prime Video)

The *Lord of the Rings* prequel series jumps forward in the Second Age, with the war between the Elves and Sauron at the centre. Charlie Vickers (Sauron), Morfydd Clark (Galadriel) and Robert Aramayo (Elrond) return. It started on 11 November. Check the Prime Video app for the weekly episode schedule.

If you haven't watched the first two seasons, start there; this season picks up years later and assumes you know the characters.

### Christmas Switch and Alicia's Law (Netflix)

**Christmas Switch** is a light English holiday comedy, reported for 12 November; it's the season for these. **Alicia's Law** is a Spanish-language legal drama with Fernanda Castillo, about a woman rebuilding her career as a lawyer after her husband's corruption scandal.

## What's still unknown

- **Ramayana on OTT:** no platform or date has been announced. It's still in its first week in theatres and doing huge numbers (see our [Ramayana box office](/ramayana-part-1-release-date) page). Don't expect it on OTT before late December.
- **Jailer 2's Hindi dub:** reported in the list of languages, but check the audio menu in the app once it's live.

## My pick for the weekend

If you only have one evening, watch **Jailer 2** on Prime Video. It's the one most people around you will be talking about, and Rajinikanth films play better with family on a big TV than alone on a phone.

If you have Netflix and want something serious, go for **Daayra**. For a binge, **Student of the Year: After Dark** is the easy one.
