**J&K Bank recruitment 2026 is open for 1,397 posts: 343 Probationary Officers (PO) and 1,054 Banking Associates (clerk).** Any graduate aged 20 to 30 can apply, but **only domiciles of Jammu and Kashmir or Ladakh**. **Apply online by 26 November 2026.**

IBPS will run the online exams. That means the papers will feel a lot like IBPS PO and Clerk, which is good news if you've already been preparing for those.

## J&K Bank recruitment 2026: vacancy details

The bank has released four separate notifications, one for each post in each UT. You apply under the one that matches your domicile.

| Post | UT of J&K | UT of Ladakh | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Probationary Officer | 323 | 20 | 343 |
| Banking Associate | 1,033 | 21 | 1,054 |
| **Total** | **1,356** | **41** | **1,397** |

Reservation in the J&K notifications follows the J&K Reservation Rules, 2005. The current split given in the notification is:

| Category | Reservation |
|---|---|
| Scheduled Castes | 8% |
| Scheduled Tribes | 20% (10% + 10% for the groups added in 2024) |
| OBC | 8% |
| Residents of areas near ALC/IB | 4% |
| Residents of Backward Areas | 10% |
| EWS | 10% |
| Persons with Disabilities | 4% (horizontal) |

The notification gives these percentages, not a post-wise category table. Ladakh follows its own UT rules.

## Eligibility

### Domicile

This is the big filter. **You must be a domicile of the UT of J&K** to apply under the J&K notifications, or **of the UT of Ladakh** for the Ladakh ones. You'll need to show the domicile certificate at document verification.

If you're from any other state, this recruitment isn't for you, however good your preparation is.

### Age limit (as on 1 January 2026)

- **Minimum:** 20 years
- **Maximum:** 30 years
- You must be born **not earlier than 1 January 1996 and not later than 1 January 2006**.

Upper age relaxation:

| Category | Relaxation |
|---|---|
| SC / ST | 5 years |
| Residents of areas near ALC/IB | 3 years |
| Persons with Disabilities | 10 years |
| EWS | None |

For Banking Associate, candidates who have finished an apprenticeship with J&K Bank and are domiciles of the UT get 1 more year of age relaxation, plus extra marks equal to 2.5% of the online exam's maximum marks.

### Qualification

- **Graduation in any discipline** from a university recognised by the Government of India, completed **by 26 November 2026** (the last date of registration).
- You need the marksheet or degree certificate as proof by that date. You'll also have to enter your graduation percentage, to two decimals, in the form.
- No minimum percentage is required. If your university gives CGPA, convert it to a percentage and keep the university's conversion certificate ready.

## Selection process and exam pattern

### Banking Associate: one online exam

Banking Associates are selected through **one online exam plus document verification**. There's no mains and no interview.

| Test | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| English Language | 30 | 30 | 20 minutes |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 35 | 30 | 20 minutes |
| Reasoning Ability | 35 | 40 | 20 minutes |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **60 minutes** |

You must clear the cut-off in each test and the total. The minimum aggregate is 40% for General and 35% for reserved categories.

### Probationary Officer: prelims, mains and interview

**Preliminary exam** (qualifying only): same pattern as above, 100 questions, 100 marks, 60 minutes. The bank shortlists **10 times the vacancies** in each category for mains.

**Mains exam:**

| Test | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reasoning | 40 | 60 | 45 minutes |
| General / Economy / Banking / Digital / Financial Awareness (incl. RBI circulars) | 50 | 60 | 35 minutes |
| English Language | 40 | 20 | 35 minutes |
| Data Analysis & Interpretation | 40 | 60 | 45 minutes |
| **Objective total** | **170** | **200** | **160 minutes** |
| Descriptive (essay + comprehension) | 2 | 25 | 30 minutes |

Then **3 times the vacancies** go to the **interview** (100 marks; 40% to qualify, 35% for reserved). The final merit list combines mains and interview in an **80:20 ratio**.

**Negative marking:** a quarter of the marks for that question is cut for every wrong answer, in both prelims and mains. Questions you leave blank cost nothing.

### Exam centres

You must choose 6 centres, and **at least 3 must be outside your UT**. Centres inside J&K are Srinagar, Jammu, Pulwama, Baramulla and Samba. Ladakh candidates have Leh and Kargil. Outside centres include Delhi NCR, Mohali, Patiala, Amritsar, Jalandhar, Ludhiana, Pathankot, Phagwara, Mumbai (with Navi Mumbai and Thane), Pune and Bengaluru.

## Salary and service conditions

| Post | Starting basic pay | Pay scale |
|---|---|---|
| Probationary Officer | ₹48,480 | 48,480 to 85,920 (JMGS-I) |
| Banking Associate | ₹24,050 | 24,050 to 64,480 |

Allowances and perks come on top of the basic pay.

Read these conditions before you apply, because they're stricter than in most bank jobs:

- **5-year zone lock:** you must stay in the zone you're first posted to for at least 5 years. No transfer out of it for any reason.
- **Bond:** POs sign a ₹2 lakh bond to serve at least 2 years. Banking Associates sign a ₹1 lakh bond for 6 months.
- **Probation:** 2 years for POs, with a qualifying evaluation exam before confirmation. 6 months for Banking Associates.
- You must join within 45 days of the appointment letter (PO).

## How to apply for J&K Bank recruitment 2026

1. Go to [jkb.bank.in](https://jkb.bank.in/career) and open the **Careers** tab.
2. Open the right notification (PO or Banking Associate, J&K or Ladakh) and read it fully.
3. Click the apply link. It takes you to the IBPS registration page.
4. Register with your name, mobile number and email. Note down the registration number and password.
5. Fill in your personal details, category, domicile, graduation percentage and 6 exam centre choices.
6. Upload your photo, signature, left thumb impression and handwritten declaration in the sizes given.
7. Check every detail. **Category can't be changed after you register.**
8. Pay the fee online and save the e-receipt and the application printout.

Registration is complete only after the fee is paid. Don't leave it for 25 or 26 November, because the IBPS site slows down near deadlines.

## Should you apply?

If you're a J&K or Ladakh domicile graduate under 30, **yes, apply for both posts if you can afford both fees.** Local-only bank recruitment with 1,000+ clerk posts doesn't come every year, and you're only competing with people from your UT.

Two things to think about honestly:

- **The 5-year zone lock is real.** If you're hoping to move to Jammu or Srinagar city after a year, it won't happen. Pick your choices knowing that.
- **The PO bond is ₹2 lakh.** If you're also writing SBI or IBPS PO and might switch, factor that in.

For prep, use your IBPS PO and Clerk material. The prelims pattern is the same 100-question, 60-minute format. For PO mains, give extra time to **RBI circulars and J&K-specific banking news**, since the awareness section mentions them by name. If you're also writing IBPS this season, see our [IBPS Clerk admit card guide](/ibps-clerk-admit-card-2026) for how those exam days work.

Next step: get your domicile certificate, graduation marksheet and category certificate ready this week, then apply before 20 November so you have time to fix any upload problems.
