**The Adobe University Hackathon 2026 winners are out.** Team **Overclocked&Underpaid** from **Shiv Nadar University (SNU), Greater Noida**, won the Grand Finale at Adobe's Noida office on **16 October 2026**. Each member takes home a **MacBook Pro**.

**IIT Guwahati's Team Cyclops** came second (a MacBook Neo for each member), and **IIIT Delhi's Team 0xAlpha** was announced third. That's out of more than 3 lakh registrations on Unstop and 8 teams in the finale.

These results were announced at the finale and shared with us by a finalist team. Adobe hadn't posted an official results list online when we published this. We'll add the link here when it does.

A small note on 3rd place. Adobe's official prize list only ever had two prizes: the winner and the first runners-up. So IIIT Delhi's team was announced third on stage, but there's no MacBook for 3rd. They still get the same finalist kit as everyone else (below).

## A private university beat two IITs

This is the part worth talking about. The winning team isn't from an IIT. Shiv Nadar University is a private university in Greater Noida, and its team finished ahead of IIT Guwahati and IIIT Delhi.

It's the same lesson as Team Nexora from Aditya University, Surampalem, reaching the final 8. Rounds 3 and 4 were about building a working product and showing it in a 5-minute demo. The judges looked at what you built, not where you study.

So if you're from a college that isn't on the "top 10" lists, don't assume this hackathon is out of reach.

## Team Nexora, Andhra Pradesh's finalists

Team Nexora (G Sriraj, Dhanush K and A Ajarya Paul) from the Department of CSE, Aditya University, Surampalem, was the only team from Andhra Pradesh in the Grand Finale. They didn't make the top 3, but being one of 8 teams out of 3 lakh registrations is a big result on its own.

## What happens next: PPIs and internships

The prizes are done. The bigger thing for many students is still pending: the **top 50 participants** get a chance at a pre-placement interview (PPI) and an Adobe internship with a stipend of **₹1,10,000 per month**.

- Only the **2028 batch** is eligible for the internship PPIs, as per Adobe.
- It's "a chance at" a PPI, not a confirmed offer.
- Adobe hasn't shared dates yet. Keep an eye on the email you registered with and the Unstop page.

We'll update this post when the PPI process starts.

## My take: should you try next year?

Yes, if you're in 2nd or 3rd year of B.Tech. This year showed that a private university team can beat IITs, and that a team from Kakinada district can reach the last 8.

Three things to do before registrations open (they opened in July this year):

- **Build one real project with a working interface.** Rounds 3 and 4 asked exactly for this.
- **Practise a 5-minute demo.** Show the main feature in the first 2 minutes.
- **Keep your DSA basics ready** for the Round 1 MCQ and coding test.
