Apple's next event is called **"Pro and Tell"** and it's on **Wednesday, 28 October 2026**, in Los Angeles. It starts at **10 am Pacific Time**: that's 1 pm in New York, 5 pm in London and 10:30 pm in India.

Apple hasn't said what it will launch. But Bloomberg reports point to the biggest Mac change in years: Apple's **first touchscreen MacBook Pro with an OLED display**, plus new M6 Macs and an OLED iPad mini.

## What Apple has actually confirmed

Very little, and that's on purpose.

- Apple sent invites to press for a "launch experience" in Los Angeles on 28 October, with the tagline "Pro and Tell".
- Greg Joswiak, Apple's marketing chief, posted an animated teaser on X saying he "can't wait to pro and tell next week".
- The name "Pro" strongly hints at MacBook Pro, but Apple hasn't named a single product, chip or price.

This comes just two weeks after Apple's home launches on 13 October, when it announced a new Apple TV 4K, a new HomePod mini and Apple HomeView on its newsroom. So Apple is clearly doing smaller, focused launches this autumn instead of one big show.

## Is there a livestream?

Apple **hasn't announced a livestream**. Reports describe this as an in-person event for journalists and creators, not a big keynote like the iPhone event in September.

Most likely, Apple will post press releases and a product video or two on apple.com. Check these when the event starts:

- [apple.com/newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/) for press releases
- [apple.com/apple-events](https://www.apple.com/apple-events/) and Apple's YouTube channel, if a video is posted

## What Apple is expected to launch on 28 October

Everything below comes from reports, mainly Bloomberg's Mark Gurman. None of it is confirmed by Apple.

### 1. Touchscreen MacBook Pro with OLED display

This is the big one. Apple has refused to put touchscreens on Macs for years. Reports say that ends now:

- **14-inch and 16-inch** sizes
- **OLED display** (Apple's first OLED screen on a Mac)
- **Touch support**, and a Dynamic Island like the iPhone
- A **lighter, redesigned** body

Reports differ on the chip. Some say M5 Pro and M5 Max, others expect M6 versions. Prices are expected to be higher than today's MacBook Pro.

### 2. M6 MacBook Pro (current design)

A regular 14-inch MacBook Pro with the newer M6 chip is also expected. It would keep the current design, without touch or OLED. Right now, Apple sells the 14-inch MacBook Pro with M5, M5 Pro or M5 Max chips.

### 3. M6 iMac

The iMac is expected to move to the M6 chip. Don't expect a big design change.

### 4. OLED iPad mini

Reports say the iPad mini gets an OLED screen, better speakers and a front camera placed for video calls with the iPad held sideways.

## What it means for you

**If you're a student planning to buy a MacBook:** a touchscreen OLED MacBook Pro will be expensive. For college work and coding, a MacBook Air or the current MacBook Pro does the job. The good news: when new models launch, older models often get price cuts at big retailers and online stores (Amazon, Best Buy, Flipkart and others).

**If you're about to buy a MacBook Pro right now:** wait 8 days. Either you get the new model, or the current one gets cheaper.

**If you use Windows:** touchscreen laptops aren't new for you. What's new is Apple joining in, which may push Windows laptop makers to improve their own OLED and touch models.

Honestly, this event matters more for what it signals than for what most people will buy. A touchscreen Mac is a big change in Apple's thinking. But first-generation designs usually come with high prices and small problems. If you need a laptop for studies or work, buy what's good value today, not the newest thing.
