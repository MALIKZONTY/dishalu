For AE and JE, you can pick **only one engineering branch**. Read the exact qualification for your post in the advertisement; a few posts accept specific extra degrees.

### Age limit (as on 1 January 2026)

- Minimum 18 or 21 years, depending on the post.
- Maximum **40 years** for General, and **45 years** for SC, ST, OBC and women (Chhattisgarh domicile).
- Candidates from outside Chhattisgarh (allowed only for some Class II posts): maximum **30 years**.
- Fire posts have tighter limits: Fire Attendant 18 to 25 (30 for reserved), other fire posts 21 to 30 (35 for reserved).

## Selection process and exam pattern

Everyone writes a **computer based test (CBT)**:

| Part | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| Professional subject (your branch/trade) | 80 | 80 |
| GK, English, Hindi, reasoning, quantitative ability | 20 | 20 |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** |

- Duration: **2 hours**, in English and Hindi
- **Negative marking: 0.25** for each wrong answer
- Qualifying marks: 40% for UR and OBC, 30% for SC and ST

After the CBT, the steps depend on the post:

| Post | After the CBT |
|---|---|
| AE, Accounts Officer, Medical Officer, Programmer, Shift Chemist | Document verification, interview |
| Fire Officer, Security Officer | Physical test, interview |
| Sub Fire Officer, Security Inspector/Sub Inspector, Fire Attendant | Physical test, document verification |
| Junior Engineer, Attendant, Law/IR officers, medical support posts | Document verification |
| Stenographer | Shorthand and typing test, document verification |
| Data Entry Operator | Hindi and English typing test, document verification |

So for JE and Attendant, the CBT score decides almost everything. **80% of the paper is your own subject**, which means your diploma or ITI basics matter far more than GK.

## The bond and security deposit (read this)

This part surprises people. If you're selected, you sign a **service bond for at least 4 years**, backed by a refundable **security deposit**:

| Post group | Security deposit |
|---|---|
| Class II (AE and equivalent) | ₹3,00,000 |
| Class III, Junior Engineer | ₹2,00,000 |
| Class III, other than JE | ₹1,00,000 |
| Class IV, Attendant / Fire Attendant | ₹50,000 |

If you're writing other exams too (say, a central PSU or SSC JE), think about this before you join. Leaving inside 4 years could cost you the deposit, as per the bond terms in the advertisement.

## How to apply for CSPTCL recruitment 2026

1. Go to [cspc.co.in](https://cspc.co.in/) and open the recruitment link for Advertisement 1/2026 or 2/2026.
2. Download and read the advertisement for your post (it's around 25 pages, with a corrigendum). Check domicile and qualification first.
3. Register with your mobile number and email, then log in.
4. Fill in your details, choose **one post** (and one branch for AE/JE), and upload your photo, signature and documents.
5. Pay the fee online.
6. Submit before **26 November 2026, 11:55 PM**, and save the printout.

Don't wait for the last two days. State power company sites are slow near the deadline.

## Should you apply?

**If you're from Chhattisgarh with an electrical diploma, yes, without thinking twice.** 167 JE Electrical posts across three companies is a big number for one state, and the paper is 80% core subject.

For DEO, 609 seats sounds like a lot, but expect very heavy competition because any graduate can apply. Practise Hindi typing too, not just English; there's a typing test for both.

If you're from outside Chhattisgarh, this is mostly an AE-only option, and only if you're 30 or younger. Even then, compare it with GATE-based PSU jobs, which pay more and don't ask for a ₹3 lakh deposit.

ITI holders from outside Chhattisgarh can't apply for the Attendant posts, so look at [RRC NR Apprentice 2026](/rrc-nr-apprentice-recruitment-2026) instead: 4,651 seats, no exam, open till 11 December.
