**Bank of Baroda recruitment 2026 is open for 837 contract posts in its Wealth Management Services department.** Almost all of them, 791, are for **Senior Relationship Manager (Deputy Manager)**. **Apply online by 24 November 2026** on the bank's careers page.

Before you get excited, know this: it isn't a fresher job and it isn't a permanent PO-type post. Every position needs **3 to 12 years of wealth management or investment experience**, and the job is a 5-year contract.

## Eligibility: qualification and experience

**Graduation in any discipline** from a recognised university is the basic qualification for all 5 positions. An MBA (Finance, Marketing or Banking), a 2-year PG diploma in management, CFP or CFA is listed as "preferred", not compulsory.

What really decides eligibility is experience and certification:

| Position | Minimum experience (after graduation) | Mandatory certification |
|---|---|---|
| Group Head | 12 years as Relationship Manager in wealth management, investment advisory or portfolio management, including 5 years as Team Lead for RMs | None mandatory (NISM V-A, V-D, XIII, XXI-A and IRDA IC38 preferred) |
| Head Investment | 10 years in direct investment roles, including 5 years in a senior leadership role (mutual funds, SIF, AIF, PMS, research, SEBI compliance) | Any one of NISM V-A, V-D, XIII or XXI-A |
| Territory Head | 8 years as Relationship Manager in wealth management, investment advisory or portfolio management | None mandatory (same list preferred) |
| Investment Product Manager | 5 years as Relationship Manager, including 3 years as an Investment Counsellor in wealth management | Any one of NISM V-A, V-D, XIII or XXI-A |
| Senior Relationship Manager | 3 years as Relationship Manager in wealth management, investment advisory or portfolio management | NISM V-A / NISM V-D and/or IRDA IC38 |

The experience must be with a public, private or foreign bank, a broking firm, a securities firm, an asset management company or another financial services company.

Three rules that will knock many people out:

- **Clerical experience doesn't count.** If you've been a bank clerk or customer service associate, those years won't be counted.
- **Teaching and training experience doesn't count.**
- **Any job shorter than 6 months is ignored** when the bank adds up your experience.

You can apply for **only one position**. If you submit more than one form, the bank keeps only the latest complete one, and the other fee is lost.

### Age relaxation

The age limits above are for General category. Relaxation in the upper age limit:

| Category | Relaxation |
|---|---|
| SC / ST | 5 years |
| OBC (non-creamy layer) | 3 years |
| PwD | 10 years (Gen/EWS), 13 years (OBC), 15 years (SC/ST) |
| Ex-servicemen / commissioned officers with 5+ years of service | 5 years (Gen/EWS), 8 years (OBC), 10 years (SC/ST) |

Note that relaxation applies only to posts where a seat is actually reserved for your category. For example, Head Investment and Investment Product Manager have only UR seats, so a reserved-category candidate gets no age relaxation there.

## Selection process

The bank hasn't fixed a single process. The advertisement says selection "may comprise" these stages:

1. **Scrutiny of applications** and shortlisting based on qualification, experience and skills. The shortlisting criteria are internal and won't be shared.
2. **Online test**, psychometric test or any other test, if the bank decides to hold one.
3. **Personal interview.**

If an online test is held, you need a minimum score in each section and in total to be called for the interview. In the interview, the **minimum qualifying mark is 60% for General and EWS** and **55% for reserved categories**. Final merit is based on the test and interview scores together.

No exam date, syllabus or test pattern has been announced. With 791 seats for one role, don't be surprised if the bank holds an online test. Keep checking your registered email, because call letters and interview dates are only sent there and on the website.

## Salary and nature of the job

There's no fixed pay scale in the advertisement. The bank says pay will be based on your **qualifications, experience, last drawn salary and the market benchmark** for the role. That's why it asks for a CTC break-up and your latest salary slips with the application.

The contract details:

- **Initial term of 5 years** from your date of joining.
- It can be extended by up to **5 more years**, if your performance is good and the bank agrees.
- The contract ends at **age 60** or at the end of the term, whichever comes first.

Look at the job description too. The Senior Relationship Manager role is a sales job: acquiring new Affluent and HNI clients, growing AUM, increasing CASA and investment business, cross-selling insurance and loans, and meeting targets.

## How to apply for Bank of Baroda recruitment 2026

1. Go to [bankofbaroda.bank.in](https://bankofbaroda.bank.in/career/current-opportunities), open **Career**, then **Current Opportunities**, and find "Recruitment of Professionals on Contractual Basis in Wealth Management Services Department: BOB/HRM/REC/ADVT/2026/20". The direct [apply link](https://bankappsweb.bankofbaroda.bank.in/BobRecruitment/) is on that page.
2. Register with a personal email ID and mobile number. Keep both active till the process ends.
3. Fill in your details and pick **one** position.
4. Upload your bio-data, photo and signature.
5. Upload your documents: date of birth proof (10th mark sheet or certificate), graduation certificate, certification proofs (NISM/IRDA), experience letters, **CTC break-up** and **latest salary slips**.
6. Answer the questions on any FIR, court case or departmental enquiry honestly. Disclosing a case doesn't automatically disqualify you, but hiding one can cancel your selection later.
7. Pay the fee online: **₹850** for Gen/EWS/OBC, **₹175** for SC/ST/PwD/ESM/Women, plus gateway charges.
8. Check everything before you click **Submit**. No changes are allowed after that. Save the e-receipt and the application printout.

If you work in a government office, PSU or nationalised bank, you'll need a **No Objection Certificate** from your employer at the interview.

Read the [official advertisement PDF](https://bankofbaroda.bank.in/-/media/Project/BOB/CountryWebsites/India/Career/2026/26-11/Advertisement-Contractual-04-11-2026-04-21.pdf) fully before you start, especially the location list on page 15.

## Should you apply?

**Apply if** you're a Relationship Manager at a private bank, broking firm or AMC with 3+ years of experience, you're 35 or under, and you hold NISM V-A or IRDA IC38. A public sector bank brand helps with HNI clients, and you can negotiate pay based on your current CTC. With 791 seats, this is one of the biggest wealth management hiring drives you'll see from a PSU bank.

**Think twice if** you want job security. It's a contract, not a permanent officer post, and the role comes with sales targets. Also check the location list. If your city has 2 or 3 seats, the bank may post you somewhere else.

**Skip it if** you're a fresher, a bank clerk hoping to move up (clerical years don't count), or someone preparing for IBPS PO or SBI PO. This drive won't help with those.

Preparing for regular bank exams instead? See the [IBPS Clerk admit card](/ibps-clerk-admit-card-2026) post. For a PSU job open to graduates without experience, [CWC Recruitment 2026](/cwc-recruitment-2026) is open until 15 November.
