## The big theatre releases, one by one

### Jailer 2 (15 October)

Rajinikanth returns as "Tiger" Muthuvel Pandian, with Nelson Dilipkumar directing again and Anirudh on music. It's a Sun Pictures film, releasing in Tamil, Telugu and Hindi (as *Rajini The Jailer 2*). We've covered the trailer links, cast and booking in detail in our [Jailer 2 release date post](/jailer-2-release-date-trailer).

It opened big: about **₹59.70 crore net in India** and **₹125 crore gross worldwide** on day 1, about ₹199.90 crore net after 4 days, and **₹317.20 crore net in India and ₹582.45 crore gross worldwide after 7 days**, as per Sacnilk's estimates (not official numbers from Sun Pictures). That makes it one of the fastest films to cross ₹300 crore net in India, though the weekday drop is now steep (₹46.80 crore on day 6, ₹28.15 crore on day 7).

Public talk is mixed to positive: people like Rajinikanth's mass scenes and Anirudh's score, and many say the second half drags. The language-wise and state-wise numbers are in our [Jailer 2 box office collection post](/jailer-2-box-office-collection-day-1).

### Ranabaali (16 October)

Vijay Deverakonda and Rashmika Mandanna in a period action drama set in Rayalaseema during British rule, directed by Rahul Sankrityan (*Shyam Singha Roy*). It's one of Vijay's biggest films so far. With Vishwambhara out of 16 October, its main competition in the Telugu states is Jailer 2's second day.

It released on **16 October**. Sacnilk estimates about **₹24.05 crore net in India in 3 days** (₹9.25 crore, ₹7.30 crore, ₹7.50 crore), and **₹42.05 crore net in India after 6 days**, with ₹59.65 crore gross worldwide. It's holding at around ₹5.5 crore a day on weekdays, which is steady but well below Jailer 2 even in the Telugu states.

The film has a **U/A certificate with no cuts**, as reported by Telugu media. The runtime hasn't been confirmed by the makers (reports range from about 2.5 to 3 hours), so check the show length on your ticketing app.

### Raftaar and Prahaar (16 October)

An unusual one: **two Rajkummar Rao films on the same Friday**.

- **Raftaar**, directed by Aditya Nimbalkar, co-stars Keerthy Suresh. It's a drama about two co-founders of an ed-tech start-up, and it's the first theatrical production of Rajkummar and Patralekhaa's banner, Kampa Film.
- **Prahaar: The Untold Story of Ujjwal Nikam**, directed by Avinash Arun, is a courtroom drama with Rajkummar playing the well-known public prosecutor.

Dates for both were moved to 16 October earlier this year. If one of them shifts again, I'll update this table.

## Booking tips for the Dussehra rush

- **Jailer 2's second weekend** (24–25 October) will still be busy in Tamil Nadu, but weekday shows are much easier to get now that collections have dropped.
- Book only on the official ticketing apps or at the theatre counter. "Bulk ticket" sellers on WhatsApp and Instagram are a common scam during big releases.
- Weekday morning shows in the second week are usually much cheaper than opening-day evening shows.
- Don't watch or share piracy links or leaked clips. Besides being illegal, many of those sites push malware.
