### Ranabaali (16 October)

Vijay Deverakonda and Rashmika Mandanna in a period action drama set in Rayalaseema during British rule, directed by Rahul Sankrityan (*Shyam Singha Roy*). It's one of Vijay's biggest films so far, and it opens against Vishwambhara on the same day. That's a real Telugu box office clash.

### Raftaar and Prahaar (16 October)

An unusual one: **two Rajkummar Rao films on the same Friday**.

- **Raftaar**, directed by Aditya Nimbalkar, co-stars Keerthy Suresh. It's a drama about two co-founders of an ed-tech start-up, and it's the first theatrical production of Rajkummar and Patralekhaa's banner, Kampa Film.
- **Prahaar: The Untold Story of Ujjwal Nikam**, directed by Avinash Arun, is a courtroom drama with Rajkummar playing the well-known public prosecutor.

Dates for both were moved to 16 October earlier this year. If one of them shifts again, I'll update this table.

The live-action film of the video game, directed by Kitao Sakurai. The Indian angle: **Vidyut Jammwal plays Dhalsim**, his Hollywood debut, alongside Noah Centineo, Andrew Koji and Jason Momoa. It releases in English, Hindi, Tamil and Telugu.

### Hope (23 October)

The new film from Korean director Na Hong-jin (*The Wailing*), a sci-fi thriller about what happens after a strange object is found. It was meant to release on 25 September but was pushed to 23 October during censor certification. The English version is certified. The Hindi, Tamil and Telugu versions were still being certified when the new date was announced.

### Still in theatres: Drishyam: The Conclusion

Ajay Devgn's Drishyam: The Conclusion opened on 2 October. Trade reports put its opening weekend among the biggest of the year for a Hindi film, so expect it to keep shows through October. If you missed it, there's still time.

## New on OTT this week

**Mandaadi (Netflix, 8 October).** Soori's Tamil sports drama about traditional sailboat racing, directed by Mathimaran Pugazhendhi, with Suhas in his first Tamil film. Netflix has confirmed it in Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam. It reaches OTT less than a month after its theatre run, so it's a good pick if you missed it in theatres.

**Epic: First Semester (Netflix, 9 October).** Anand Deverakonda and Vaishnavi Chaitanya (the *Baby* pair) in a Telugu romantic drama about a student finding his way abroad, directed by Aditya Haasan. Streaming in Telugu with four dubbed versions.

**Reporting Live (Netflix, 9 October).** A Hindi Netflix film with Parineeti Chopra as a young reporter who goes after an illegal sand-mining network. Directed by Dhruv Tripathi, with Adil Hussain, Amol Palekar and Shekhar Suman.

**Khalifa (9 October).** Prithviraj Sukumaran's Malayalam action thriller, directed by Vysakh. In India it streams on Prime Video, aha, ManoramaMAX and Lionsgate Play, so you probably already have at least one of these.

All four need a paid plan on that platform. None are on a free tier.

## Booking tips for the Dussehra rush

- **Jailer 2, Vishwambhara and Ranabaali opening within two days of each other means first-weekend shows will fill fast**, especially in AP, Telangana and Tamil Nadu. Book as soon as your theatre opens booking.
- Book only on the official ticketing apps or at the theatre counter. "Bulk ticket" sellers on WhatsApp and Instagram are a common scam during big releases.
- Weekday morning shows in the second week are usually much cheaper than opening-day evening shows.
- Don't watch or share piracy links or leaked clips. Besides being illegal, many of those sites push malware.
