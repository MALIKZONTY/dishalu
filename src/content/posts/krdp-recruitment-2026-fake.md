## Is KRDP recruitment 2026 real? What KVIC said

KVIC put two notices on its homepage on 29 October 2026.

The first is short and clear. It says the KRDP recruitment advertisement is fake, unauthorised and not linked to KVIC.

The second is a longer public notice titled "Caution against Fake / Unauthorised Recruitment Advertisement(s) Purportedly Issued in the Name of KVIC". In simple words, it tells candidates:

- **Don't trust a recruitment notice** unless you can verify it on KVIC's official website.
- **Don't pay anyone** (no deposit, transfer or fee) unless a genuine KVIC notification asks for it.
- **Don't share** Aadhaar, PAN, bank details, OTPs, passwords or ID copies on unverified websites or links.
- KVIC **won't be responsible** for money lost to fake ads.
- If you were affected, **keep screenshots, URLs, phone numbers and payment receipts**, and report it to the police.

KVIC also says it may take legal action, including criminal action, against those misusing its name.

News reports say PIB Fact Check has also flagged the ad. But the KVIC notice is the one that matters, because KVIC is the body the ad pretends to recruit for.

## What the fake KRDP notification claims

We're listing these only so you can recognise the ad if it reaches you. **None of this is real.**

| The fake ad claims | Reality |
|---|---|
| 12,878 vacancies (3,602 Group B, 9,276 Group C) | KVIC says no such recruitment exists |
| Posts like Khadi Development Officer, Khadi Inspector, Account Officer, Technical Officer, Junior Assistant, Driver, MTS | Not notified by KVIC |
| Registration from 26 October to 25 November 2026, final applications 26–30 November | No official dates exist |
| Apply at krdp.org.in | KVIC's site is kvic.gov.in |
| Age 18–40, 10th pass to master's degree | Made up to attract the widest crowd |

Why does it look believable? Because **KRDP (Khadi Reform and Development Programme) is a real KVIC programme**. It was set up to revive khadi institutions and support spinners and weavers. It is not a recruitment agency. Scammers borrowed a real name and attached a huge, fake vacancy number to it.

## How to spot a fake government job notice

Fake recruitment ads keep coming back with new names. These checks take two minutes:

1. **Check the website address.** Central government bodies use .gov.in or .nic.in. A new .org.in or .com site claiming thousands of posts is a warning sign.
2. **Find the notice on the department's own site.** For KVIC, that's kvic.gov.in. If it isn't there, it isn't real.
3. **Check Employment News.** Big central recruitment is usually advertised in [Employment News](https://employmentnews.gov.in).
4. **Watch for unusual fees.** Asking for a "security deposit", "verification charge" or "training fee", or asking you to pay a person by UPI, is a scam sign.
5. **Too good to be true?** Huge vacancies, easy eligibility, no exam or "direct selection" are classic hooks.
6. **Only shared on WhatsApp, Telegram or YouTube?** Real notices have an official PDF on a .gov.in site with a reference number and date.

## Already applied on krdp.org.in? Do this now

Don't panic. Registering alone usually doesn't cost you money. What you do next matters:

- **Don't pay** any fee, deposit or "document charge", even if they call or email you saying you're selected.
- **Paid already?** Call **1930** (National Cyber Crime Helpline) as soon as possible and file a complaint on [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in). The sooner you report, the better the chance of stopping the money. Tell your bank too.
- **Shared Aadhaar, PAN or bank details?** Keep an eye on your bank messages. You can lock your Aadhaar biometrics on the UIDAI website or mAadhaar app.
- **Used a password you use elsewhere?** Change it on your email and other accounts.
- **Keep proof**: screenshots of the site, the ad, payment receipts and phone numbers. KVIC's notice asks for the same.
- **Warn others.** If you saw it in a WhatsApp group, reply with the KVIC notice so others don't fall for it.

## My take

Honestly, this one should have been easy to catch: 12,878 posts from a body whose real ad right now is for 12 posts, on a .org.in website instead of kvic.gov.in. But the ad copied the style of real notifications very well, and many job portals repeated it as real for days.

So here's a simple habit. **Before you apply anywhere, open the official site yourself** (type it, don't click a forwarded link) and find the notice there. If you can't find it, wait.
