## IBPS recruitment 2026: the three posts

The advertisement doesn't say how many people IBPS will hire for each post. It only says selected candidates will be appointed "against the existing vacancies" and a wait list will stay valid for 6 months. For a small, in-house drive like this, expect a handful of seats, not hundreds.

Here's what each job actually involves, in plain words:

- **Server Administrator (Grade D):** running IBPS's Linux servers, network switches and firewalls, backups and the MS Exchange email system. IBPS says clearly this is real system administration, "NOT just system performance/health monitoring" and not software development.
- **Assistant (Grade B):** checking exam results for accuracy before they're published, handling large datasets in Excel, keeping records, and keeping candidate data confidential.
- **Programming Assistant (Grade B):** working with data files on Unix/Linux (including the `vi` editor), running programs on them, pulling out reports into Excel, and basic coding in FORTRAN or Python.

So two of the three jobs are basically about IBPS's own exam data. If you've ever wondered who checks bank exam results before they go live, this is part of that team.

## Eligibility: age, qualification and experience

Age is counted **as on 1 October 2026**.

| Post | Age | Qualification | Minimum experience |
|---|---|---|---|
| Server Administrator | 25 to 33 years (born 2 Oct 1993 to 1 Oct 2001) | B.E./B.Tech in Electronics, Computer Science, IT or equivalent | 3 years, hands-on Linux server admin (RHEL or similar) plus at least basic network management (L3 switch) |
| Assistant | 23 to 30 years (born 2 Oct 1996 to 1 Oct 2003) | Bachelor's degree with **60% aggregate**, preferably Science, Commerce, Statistics, Maths, Economics, Computer Applications/IT/Data Analytics or BBA/Management, plus advanced MS Excel | 2 years in data validation, MIS reporting, record management or Excel-based data work |
| Programming Assistant | 23 to 30 years (born 2 Oct 1996 to 1 Oct 2003) | Full-time B.Sc IT / BCA / B.Sc Computer Science, or an engineering degree in CS/IT | 2 years handling applications in Unix/Linux, file handling, data entry/transfer, basic programming, good Excel |

A few rules that will knock people out, so read them twice:

- **No distance or open university degrees.** IBPS says degrees through correspondence, distance or open university "are not acceptable".
- **The experience must come after your degree.** Work done before you finished the qualification doesn't count.
- **The 60% for Assistant is strict.** 59.99% is treated as below 60%.
- **Govt or PSU employees** need a No Objection Certificate from their employer at the selection stage.

The advertisement doesn't mention any category-wise age relaxation for these posts. If you're counting on one, don't assume it.

For the Assistant post, Excel is the real test. The advertisement names VLOOKUP, INDEX-MATCH, nested IFs, pivot tables, data cleaning and basic macros or Power Query. If those words make you nervous, that tells you something.

## Selection process and exam pattern

There are three stages, and they all happen at **IBPS House, Kandivali (East), Mumbai**:

1. **Scrutiny of applications.** IBPS shortlists from the forms, giving preference to Mumbai residents and to people with more qualification and experience than the minimum.
2. **Online exam** at IBPS, Mumbai.
3. **Personal interview**, on the same day as the exam (or the next day if the crowd is big).

Each online exam has **100 questions, 100 marks, 90 minutes**, and is in English only:

| Post | Sections |
|---|---|
| Server Administrator | Aptitude (30) + Professional Knowledge (70) |
| Assistant | General Awareness, English, Reasoning, Numerical Ability, Computer Knowledge (20 each) |
| Programming Assistant | Aptitude (50) + Professional Knowledge (50) |

There's a **negative mark of 0.25** for each wrong answer. You need a minimum score in every section, and then a high enough total to reach the interview. You travel and stay at your own cost.

Three things to weigh before you apply:

- **₹1 lakh service bond for 2 years.** Leave before 2 years and you pay IBPS ₹1 lakh. Days of loss of pay can stretch the bond period.
- **1-year probation**, plus a medical certificate and police verification at joining.
- **No housing.** IBPS says it gives no accommodation help and wants people ready to settle in Mumbai for the long term.

## How to apply for IBPS recruitment 2026

1. Go to [ibps.in](https://www.ibps.in) and open the link for "Direct Recruitment of Server Administrator, Assistant & Programming Assistant on Regular Basis", or go straight to the [apply page](https://ibpsreg.ibps.in/ibpsafeb26/).
2. Click **New Registration**, fill in your basic details, and note the provisional registration number and password.
3. Upload your photo, signature, left thumb impression and handwritten declaration. You'll also capture a live photo through webcam or phone.
4. Upload a **short resume (PDF)**, your **10th certificate**, and proof of age, qualification and experience.
5. Pay **₹250** online. Applying for two posts? You need two separate forms and two fees.
6. Check everything with **Save and Next**, then click **Complete Registration**. Nothing can be changed after that.
7. Save the e-receipt and application printout.

If you don't get an email and SMS with your registration number, IBPS says to assume the form didn't go through. Check before 12 November, not after.

Read the [official advertisement PDF](https://www.ibps.in/wp-content/uploads/Advertisement-for-Server-Administrator-Programming-Assistant-and-Assistant-Oct._Nov.-2026.pdf) once fully before you start. The list of documents to carry is long.

## Should you apply?

**Apply if** you're a Linux or network admin with 3+ years in Mumbai and your current CTC is below ₹14 lakh. For that profile, this is a stable job at an institution that isn't going anywhere, with no transfers outside Mumbai.

**Apply for Assistant if** you've spent 2 years doing MIS or data reconciliation work and you're genuinely fast in Excel. The paper is general (reasoning, English, numbers), but the interview will check the Excel claims on your resume.

**Skip it if** you're a fresher, you don't want to live in Mumbai, or you got your degree through distance mode. Also skip it if you're mainly preparing for bank PO or clerk. This won't help with that, and the bond ties you down for 2 years.
