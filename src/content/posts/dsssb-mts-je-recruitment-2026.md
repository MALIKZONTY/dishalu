**DSSSB has opened applications for 1,260 posts under Advertisement No. 06/2026:** 1,075 Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS) posts for 10th pass candidates and 185 Junior Engineer (Electrical) posts. You can apply on dsssbonline.nic.in from **12 November to 11 December 2026** (11:59 pm).

Most of the MTS posts are in Delhi government schools. 757 are MTS (General Duty) and 239 are MTS Aaya (female only) in the Directorate of Education. The exam date isn't announced yet.

## DSSSB MTS 2026 eligibility

**MTS (all departments):**
- **Qualification:** 10th pass (matriculation) from a recognised board.
- **Age:** 18 to 27 years.
- **MTS Aaya:** only women can apply.

**Junior Engineer (Electrical):**
- **Delhi Transco:** degree or diploma in Electrical Engineering.
- **NDMC and MCD:** a degree in Electrical Engineering, **or** a diploma plus 2 years' professional experience.
- **Delhi Agricultural Marketing Board:** diploma in Electrical Engineering plus 2 years' installation or maintenance work.
- Check the age for each department in the table above. NDMC allows up to 32. The rest stop at 27.

Your age, qualification and experience are all counted on **11 December 2026**, the last date. So if your result comes out after that, you can't apply.

**Age relaxation:**

| Category | Relaxation |
|---|---|
| SC / ST | 5 years |
| OBC | 3 years |
| PwBD | 10 years (15 for SC/ST, 13 for OBC) |
| Ex-servicemen | Military service + 3 years |
| Delhi govt employees | Up to 40 years (45 for SC/ST, 43 for OBC), subject to conditions |

A note on OBC: reservation and relaxation for OBC apply only if your caste is on the **Delhi OBC list**. An OBC certificate from another state is accepted only in specific cases explained in the advertisement. Read that section before you tick OBC.

## Exam pattern

**MTS (one tier, 200 marks, 2 hours):**

| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| General Awareness | 40 | 40 |
| General Intelligence and Reasoning | 40 | 40 |
| Arithmetic and Numerical Ability | 40 | 40 |
| Hindi Language and Comprehension | 40 | 40 |
| English Language and Comprehension | 40 | 40 |
| **Total** | **200** | **200** |

**JE Electrical (two tiers):**

- **Tier I (2 hours, 200 marks, qualifying only):** Section A is general (5 subjects, 20 marks each). Section B is 100 questions of Electrical Engineering. The minimum marks rule applies to Section B.
- **Tier II (2.5 hours, 200 marks, decides merit):** Part I is 100 one-mark questions on Electrical Engineering. Part II is 50 two-mark questions that test applying concepts and solving problems.

**Rules for both:**
- **Negative marking:** 0.25 marks per wrong 1-mark answer, and 0.50 per wrong 2-mark answer.
- Questions are in Hindi and English, except the language sections.
- **Minimum qualifying marks:** 40% (General, EWS), 35% (OBC Delhi), 30% (SC, ST, PwBD). Ex-servicemen get 5% relaxation, but not below 30%.
- There's no re-evaluation of answer sheets.

The Hindi section is where many candidates from South India lose marks. If Hindi isn't your strong language, start practising Hindi grammar and comprehension now. It's 40 of the 200 marks.

## Application fee

- **₹100** for General, OBC and EWS men.
- **No fee** for women, SC, ST, PwBD and ex-servicemen.
- Pay only through **SBI e-pay**. Other payment modes aren't accepted, and the fee is not refunded.

## How to apply for DSSSB MTS 2026

1. Go to **dsssbonline.nic.in**.
2. Register if you haven't before. It's one-time, so if you applied for any earlier DSSSB exam, use the same login.
3. Log in and choose Advertisement 06/2026, then post code **810/26** (MTS) or **809/26** (JE). For MTS, select the departments you want.
4. Fill in your details, upload your photo and signature, and pay the fee through SBI e-pay.
5. Submit and save a printout of the form.

The full advertisement is on [dsssb.delhi.gov.in](https://dsssb.delhi.gov.in/sites/default/files/DSSSB/circulars-orders/final_advt_06_2026_j_e_electrical_mts.pdf). The exam date will be announced only on the DSSSB website.

## Should you apply?

**For MTS: yes, if you're 18–27 and can work in Delhi.** 1,075 posts with only a 10th-pass requirement is a big number for one board, and the Aaya posts (239) are reserved for women. The salary is the same as central government MTS, and the work is mostly in schools, with fixed hours.

Expect heavy competition, though. Many graduates also apply for MTS. Use the 2–3 months before the exam for daily practice on arithmetic and reasoning, which are the easiest marks to secure.

**For JE: apply if you have an Electrical degree or diploma.** Put Delhi Transco first. The Level 8 pay makes it far better than the rest. If you're also preparing for [SSC JE](/ssc-je-admit-card-2026), the technical syllabus overlaps a lot.

Your next step: register on dsssbonline.nic.in this week and keep scanned copies of your 10th certificate, caste certificate (Delhi OBC if applicable), photo and signature ready. Don't wait for 11 December.
