## How to download the IBPS Clerk admit card 2026

It takes about two minutes if you have your login details ready.

1. Go to the official website, [ibps.in](https://www.ibps.in/).
2. Open the **CRP CSA-XVI** (Clerk) section.
3. Click the link for the **Online Preliminary Exam Call Letter**.
4. Enter your **registration number or roll number**.
5. Enter your **password or date of birth** and fill in the captcha.
6. Your call letter opens as a PDF. Download it and take a clear printout.

**Forgot your registration number?** It's in the confirmation email and SMS you got when you applied. Search your inbox for "IBPS" or "CRP CSA".

**Tip:** save the PDF on your phone and in your email too. If the printout gets lost or damaged on exam day, you can print it again near the centre.

## What to check on your call letter

Open the PDF and check every line. Mistakes are rare, but you want to find them now, not at the exam gate.

- Your **name**, spelled exactly as on your photo ID
- **Roll number** and **registration number**
- Your **photograph and signature** (clear, not blurred)
- **Exam date and shift**
- **Reporting time** (this is earlier than the exam start time)
- **Exam centre name and full address**
- The **instructions** printed on the call letter

If anything is wrong, contact IBPS through the candidate support details on ibps.in straight away. Don't wait until the day before the exam.

## Documents to carry on exam day

You won't be allowed in without these:

- **The printed call letter**
- **An original, valid photo ID** (Aadhaar, PAN card, passport, driving licence, voter ID or similar). The name must match the call letter.
- **A passport-size photograph**, if your call letter asks for one
- Any other document mentioned in the instructions on your call letter

Read the instructions page of your call letter carefully. If it asks for a photocopy of your ID or a photo pasted somewhere, do it the day before.

**Do not carry** mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators or any electronic device into the exam hall.

## Exam-day plan

- **Visit your centre a day early** if it's in an unfamiliar area. Exam centres are often outside the city, and Google Maps doesn't always get the gate right.
- **Reach before the reporting time** on your call letter. Late candidates are not allowed in.
- Eat something light. The exam is only 60 minutes, but the security check and waiting can take an hour or more.
- Keep your ID and call letter in a clear folder so they don't get crushed.

## IBPS Clerk prelims exam pattern 2026

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| English Language | 30 | 30 | 20 minutes |
| Numerical Ability | 35 | 35 | 20 minutes |
| Reasoning Ability | 35 | 35 | 20 minutes |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **60 minutes** |

Each section has its **own 20-minute timer**. When the time ends, the section locks and you move to the next one. You can't go back.

There is **negative marking of 0.25** for every wrong answer. Blank answers carry no penalty.

So your score = correct answers − (0.25 × wrong answers). For example, 70 correct and 12 wrong gives you 70 − 3 = **67 marks**.

## What to do in the last 7 days

*This section was for the week before the prelims. If you've written the exam, skip to the next section.*

You can't learn new topics this week. You can make sure you score what you already know.

- **Take one full mock every day** in the same time slot as your real shift. Your brain gets used to that time.
- **Analyse every mock for 30 minutes.** Where did you lose time? Which questions should you have skipped?
- **Fix your section order inside each 20 minutes.** For example, in Reasoning, do the inequality, syllogism and direction questions first, then the puzzles.
- **Practise skipping.** In a 20-minute section, one hard puzzle can eat 6 minutes. Leave it and come back if time is left.
- **Revise, don't start fresh.** Go over your error notebook, simplification tricks and English vocabulary from past mocks.
- **Sleep properly** for the last two nights. A tired brain makes silly calculation mistakes.

## Should you worry if your mocks are low?

Honestly, no. Mock tests are often harder than the real exam, and many people who score average in mocks clear the prelims.

What matters most is accuracy. With 0.25 negative marking, 65 attempts at 90% accuracy beat 85 attempts with lots of guesses. Attempt what you're sure about, and skip blind guesses.

## IBPS Clerk prelims 2026 is over: what now?

1. **Prelims result:** IBPS will publish it on ibps.in. The date hasn't been announced yet. The scorecard with your marks usually follows a few days after the result.
2. **Mains exam:** scheduled for **27 December 2026** for candidates who qualify.
3. **Provisional allotment:** final selection for Clerk is based on mains marks. There's no interview.

**About answer keys:** IBPS doesn't usually publish an official answer key for the Clerk prelims. The "answer keys" and "expected cut-offs" you see on coaching sites are memory-based and unofficial. Use them only for a rough idea.

**Work out your rough score** with the marking scheme above: correct answers minus 0.25 × wrong answers. Do it once, honestly, and then stop checking cut-off videos every hour.

**Start mains prep now, not after the result.** 27 December is about 11 weeks away. If you wait for the result, you lose 3 to 4 of those weeks. The mains has a separate General/Financial Awareness section, so start reading banking and economy news daily from this week.
