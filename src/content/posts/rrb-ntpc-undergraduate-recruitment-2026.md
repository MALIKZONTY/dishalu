**RRB NTPC Undergraduate 2026 applications open on 15 October and close on 13 November 2026 (11:59 PM).** Railways has announced **1,688 posts** for 12th pass candidates under **CEN 07/2026**, at pay Level 2 and Level 3. Both dates are marked "tentative" in the official notice.

One thing to know before you read any other site: the **detailed notification isn't out yet**. As of the morning of 14 October, RRB has only published a short "indicative notice". Post-wise vacancies, age limit and fee will come in the detailed CEN. Anything you see quoted as final today is a guess, so I've marked clearly below what's official and what's from the last cycle.

## What the official RRB NTPC UG 2026 notice says

The indicative notice is a one-page newspaper advertisement from the Chairpersons of the RRBs. Here's everything in it:

- **Posts:** "Various posts of Non-Technical Popular Categories (Under Graduate)"
- **Pay level:** Level 2 and Level 3
- **Vacancies:** 1,688, tentative, for all RRBs together
- **Medical standard and age:** "as per the detailed CEN"
- **Dates:** opening 15.10.2026, closing 13.11.2026 at 23:59, both tentative
- **Aadhaar:** verify your details using Aadhaar while filling the form, or face detailed checks at every stage

It also says clearly that all eligibility rules and terms will be "strictly as per the detailed CEN No. 07/2026", published only on the common RRB website.

The notice doesn't list post names yet. In the last undergraduate cycle (CEN 06/2024), there were 4 posts. Expect a similar list, but confirm when the detailed CEN is out:

DA, HRA and transport allowance come on top of the basic pay, so the in-hand salary is higher than the figures above.

## Eligibility (what to expect)

### Educational qualification

The posts are for **12th pass (10+2) or equivalent**. In the last cycle (CEN 06/2024), candidates needed:

- **at least 50% marks** in 12th, except SC, ST, PwBD, ex-servicemen and those with higher qualifications, who only needed a pass
- **typing proficiency** in English or Hindi on a computer for the two "cum Typist" posts

Graduates can also apply for the UG posts. If you have a degree, apply for **both** the Graduate and the Undergraduate notifications. They are separate CENs, so applying to both is allowed.

### Age limit (as on 1 January 2027)

The official notice only says age is counted as on **1 January 2027** and the limit will be "as per the detailed CEN".

In CEN 06/2024, the normal age limit for these posts was **18 to 30 years**. That cycle added a one-time 3-year COVID relaxation, making it 33. If the 2026 limit is 18 to 30 again, you'd need to be born between **2 January 1997 and 1 January 2009** (UR/EWS). OBC-NCL normally get 3 extra years and SC/ST get 5.

Don't count on the COVID relaxation this time. It was announced as a one-time measure.

## Application fee and exam pattern

The fee isn't in the indicative notice. For comparison, the current **Graduate CEN 06/2026** charges ₹500 for General/OBC/EWS (₹400 refunded after you write CBT 1) and ₹250 for SC, ST, women, ex-servicemen, PwBD, transgender, minorities and EBC (fully refunded after CBT 1). RRB usually keeps the same fee across CENs in a year.

The NTPC exam pattern has been the same for years. Here's the pattern in the current Graduate CEN 06/2026:

| Stage | Questions | Time | General Awareness | Maths | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CBT 1 (screening) | 100 | 90 min | 40 | 30 | 30 |
| CBT 2 (merit) | 120 | 90 min | 50 | 35 | 35 |

There's 1/3 mark negative marking for each wrong answer. CBT 1 is only for shortlisting, and CBT 2 decides your rank.

**Typing test:** in 2024, Accounts Clerk and Junior Clerk had a typing skill test after CBT 2. The Graduate CEN 06/2026 **dropped** its typing test and moved typing checks to after joining. Whether the UG posts follow the same rule will be clear only in the detailed CEN.

## How to apply for RRB NTPC UG 2026

The link should go live on [rrbapply.gov.in](https://www.rrbapply.gov.in) from 15 October. Steps, based on how RRB applications work this year:

1. **Before anything, fix your Aadhaar.** RRB's notice says your name and date of birth in Aadhaar must match your 10th certificate **100%**, and your Aadhaar photo and fingerprints should be updated. If there's a mismatch, get it corrected now. It can take a week or more.
2. Go to rrbapply.gov.in. **Create an account**, or log in with your old account if you applied for any RRB exam in 2024–2026.
3. Verify your details with **Aadhaar**. Non-Aadhaar-verified forms face detailed checks at every stage.
4. Choose **one RRB** and rank the posts. You can apply to only one RRB under CEN 07/2026.
5. Take the live photo, upload your signature, and pay the fee online.
6. Save the confirmation page.

**Don't apply on day one if the detailed CEN isn't out.** Read the eligibility first, so you don't pay for a post you can't take. You have till 13 November.

## Should you apply?

If you're 12th pass and in the age range, yes. The fee is small, part of it comes back after CBT 1, and the maths and reasoning syllabus overlaps a lot with SSC CHSL and SSC GD. Your preparation for one helps the others.

But be honest with yourself about the numbers. 1,688 posts across India means some RRBs will have only a handful of seats in your category. Don't put all your hopes on this one exam. Keep [SSC CHSL 2026](/ssc-chsl-2026-last-date) and state-level exams going in parallel.

Use the next few days well. Fix your Aadhaar, find your 10th and 12th certificates, and get your caste or EWS certificate updated if needed. Those are the things that cause rejections, not the exam.
