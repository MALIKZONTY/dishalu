**APPSC Group 1 recruitment 2026 is open for 166 posts**, including Deputy Collector, DSP, District Educational Officer and Divisional Development Officer. Apply on psc.ap.gov.in from **6 October to 27 October 2026 (11:59 pm)**. You need a bachelor's degree in any subject (except DSP Communications, which needs an ECE-type BTech), and for most posts you must be 18 to 44.

The prelims (Screening Test) is on **24 January 2027**. That's about 15 weeks from today. That's the number to plan your preparation around.

## APPSC Group 1 eligibility

### Educational qualification

For 12 of the 13 posts, a **bachelor's degree in any subject** from a recognised university is enough. BA, BCom, BSc, BTech, all fine. No minimum percentage.

The exception is **DSP (Communications)**. It needs a **BE/BTech in Electronics and Communication, Electronics and Telecommunication, or Radio Engineering**.

Your degree must be complete **as on the date of the detailed notification (6 October 2026)**. So if you're in final year right now, you can't apply this time.

### Age limit (as on 1 July 2026)

| Post | Minimum | Maximum |
|---|---|---|
| DSP (Civil) | 21 | 30 |
| DSP (Communications) | 18 | 30 |
| All other 11 posts | 18 | 44 |

Age relaxation on top of the maximum:

- **SC, ST, BC, EWS:** 5 years
- **Persons with Benchmark Disabilities:** 10 years
- **Ex-servicemen and NCC instructors:** 3 years plus length of service
- **Regular AP State Government employees:** length of service, up to 5 years (corporation and municipal staff don't get this)
- **Retrenched census employees (6+ months' service):** 3 years

### Physical standards (DSP posts only)

For both DSP posts, men need a height of at least **167.6 cm** and chest of **86.3 cm** with 5 cm expansion (ST men: 164 cm and 83.8 cm). Women need **152.5 cm** height and **45.5 kg** weight. There are eyesight standards too, and PwBD reservation does not apply to the DSP posts.

### Local and non-local

All posts except DSP (Communications) follow the **95% local reservation** under the Presidential Order 2025. Most posts are split into Multi-Zone I (Srikakulam to Krishna) and Multi-Zone II (Guntur to Kurnool and Sri Sathya Sai). Assistant Audit Officer posts are zonal. Your local status depends on where you studied from Class 4 to Class 7, so keep your study certificates ready.

## APPSC Group 1 exam pattern 2026

There are three stages: Screening Test, Mains, Interview.

### Stage 1: Screening Test (prelims), 24 January 2027

| Paper | Subjects | Questions | Time | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paper I: General Studies | History & Culture, Polity & Social Justice, Indian and AP Economy, Geography (30 marks each) | 120 | 120 min | 120 |
| Paper II: General Aptitude | Mental Ability & Administrative/Psychological Ability (60), Science & Tech (30), Current Events (30) | 120 | 120 min | 120 |

**Negative marking: 1/3 of the marks for every wrong answer.** It's an offline OMR exam, and you must write both papers. The prelims only shortlists you. Its marks don't count in the final merit.

APPSC calls **50 candidates per vacancy** to Mains. With 166 posts, that's roughly 8,300 seats in Mains.

### Stage 2: Mains (descriptive)

| Paper | Marks | Counts for merit? |
|---|---|---|
| Telugu | 150 | No, qualifying only |
| English | 150 | No, qualifying only |
| Paper I: General Essay | 150 | Yes |
| Paper II: History, Culture and Geography of India and AP | 150 | Yes |
| Paper III: Polity, Constitution, Governance, Law and Ethics | 150 | Yes |
| Paper IV: Economy and Development of India and AP | 150 | Yes |
| Paper V: Science, Technology and Environmental Issues | 150 | Yes |

Each paper is 3 hours. To pass the Telugu and English papers you need 40% (OC, EWS, sports quota), 35% (BC) or 30% (SC, ST, PwBD). You can write Papers I to V in English, Telugu or Urdu, but all five must be in the same medium.

### Stage 3: Interview

**75 marks**, at a ratio of 1:2 (two candidates per post). Final merit = Mains (750) + Interview (75) = **825 marks**.

The **Screening Test** is held in all 26 districts, from Srikakulam to Kurnool. You pick **3 preferences** in the form.

**Mains** is held only in **4 cities: Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Tirupati and Ananthapuramu**. You pick one, and you can't change it later. Choose the one you can reach without an overnight trip, because Mains runs over several days.

## How to apply for APPSC Group 1 2026

1. **Create or update your OTPR.** Go to [psc.ap.gov.in](https://portal-psc.ap.gov.in/HomePages/RecruitmentNotifications.aspx) and register for One Time Profile Registration if you're new. If you already have an OTPR, check your name, date of birth, community and white card details now. These fields **can't be corrected** after you apply.
2. **Log in** with your OTPR ID and password, then click **Online Application Submission**.
3. Click **Fill Application** against Notification No. 07/2026.
4. Fill in local/non-local status, qualification, Screening Test centres (3) and Mains centre (1).
5. Click **Save & Submit**. Clicking only "Save" doesn't submit your application, and the payment link appears only after Save & Submit.
6. Pay the fee online and download the filled application PDF.

Certificates to arrange before you apply:

- **BC candidates:** Non-Creamy Layer certificate issued **on or after 1 April 2026** and before 27 October.
- **EWS candidates:** EWS certificate for FY 2025-26, in digital format, issued **on or after 1 April 2026**.
- Study certificates from Class 1 to 7 (for local status).

If you get a BC or EWS certificate after 27 October, it won't count. Apply at your Tahsildar office this week.

## Should you apply?

Yes, if you're a graduate from AP and you can give it serious time. 166 posts is about double the last round, and these are the best state jobs a graduate can get without UPSC. A Deputy Collector or DDO career goes much further than most private jobs.

But be honest with yourself about the work. Mains has five descriptive papers plus two language papers, and AP-specific history, economy and culture carry a lot of weight. If you start only after the prelims result, you'll be late. Most toppers prepare for prelims and Mains together from day one.

If you're aiming for DSP (Civil) and you're close to 30, this may be your last chance. Apply even if you feel underprepared.

If you're applying to central jobs too, the [Supreme Court JCA recruitment](/supreme-court-jca-recruitment-2026) closes the next day (28 October), and its General Studies overlaps.

**Your next step:** open your OTPR today and check the community and white card fields. Then apply before 20 October, because government portals tend to slow down in the last few days.
