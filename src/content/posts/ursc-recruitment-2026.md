Post code 01 accepts any of these ITI trades: Electronics Mechanic, Technician Power Electronic Systems, Mechanic Consumer Electronic Appliances, Instrument Mechanic or Mechanic Industrial Electronics.

**Notice the SC and OBC gaps.** There's no OBC seat outside codes 01, 02 and 11, and no ST seat outside codes 01 and 02. In a post with no seat for your category, you compete as unreserved, with no age relaxation.

A few seats are at **MCF Hassan** (Master Control Facility, Karnataka), not Bengaluru: 2 Electrician, 1 Refrigeration & AC and 2 Heavy Vehicle Driver.

### Ex-Servicemen and PwBD seats

- **Ex-Servicemen:** 21 Technician 'B' seats and 8 Driver seats (2 LVD, 6 HVD) at URSC, so 29 in all, of which 24 are backlog. One HVD seat at MCF Hassan is also for an Ex-Serviceman.
- **PwBD:** in code 01, 2 seats for visually impaired (one backlog), 1 for orthopedically handicapped and 1 backlog for autism spectrum disorder. Fitter has 1 for hard of hearing, Electrician 1 backlog for ASD, and Refrigeration & AC 1 backlog for hard of hearing. You need at least 40% disability for the benefit.

## Eligibility for URSC recruitment 2026

### Qualification

| Post | What you need |
|---|---|
| Technician 'B' (01 to 08) | SSLC/SSC/Matriculation + ITI/NTC/NAC in the matching trade from **NCVT** |
| Light Vehicle Driver 'A' (09) | 10th pass + 3 years as an LVD + valid LVD licence |
| Staff Car Driver 'A' (10) | 10th pass + 3 years as an LVD + valid LVD licence |
| Heavy Vehicle Driver 'A' (11) | 10th pass + 5 years driving (at least 3 as HVD, rest LMV) + valid HVD licence + Public Service Badge where required |

Things that trip people up:

- **Results pending? Don't apply.** URSC says students awaiting their final result need not apply. Everything must be in hand by 13 November 2026.
- **Driver experience only counts after you got your licence.** The certificate must come from a government or semi-government body, registered company, society or trust. Certificates from individuals and part-time driving are not accepted.
- **Licence from another state?** Any extra Karnataka Motor Vehicle Act requirement (and the Public Service Badge for HVD, if your state doesn't need one) must be met within 3 months of joining.
- **HVD job includes light vehicles.** People selected as HVD 'A' will drive both, as needed.

### Age limit (as on 13 November 2026)

All posts: **18 to 35 years.** SC/ST candidates get **5 years** extra and OBC **3 years**, but only against seats reserved for those categories. Central government staff, Ex-Servicemen, PwBD, meritorious sportspersons and some widowed or divorced women get relaxation as per government orders.

## Selection process and exam pattern

First, URSC does an **initial screening** based on your academic marks and form details. Only those screened in get the written test.

The written test can be online (CBT) or offline, in Bengaluru or other cities. **The skill test is in Bengaluru only.** There's no interview.

### Technician 'B'

| Stage | Details | Pass marks (UR / reserved) |
|---|---|---|
| Written test | 80 MCQs, 1.5 hours, +1 right, −0.33 wrong, syllabus based on your ITI curriculum | 32/80 / 24/80 |
| Skill test | 100 marks, qualifying only | 50/100 / 40/100 |

Shortlisting for the skill test is 1:5, with at least 10 candidates. If written scores are tied, URSC looks at your ITI/NTC/NAC marks, and **NAC holders get preference** over those without a NAC score.

### Drivers (LVD, Staff Car, HVD)

The written test has 100 MCQs in 2 hours, +1 for right and −0.25 for wrong:

| Part | Questions | Topics |
|---|---|---|
| A | 50 | Motor Vehicles Act 1988: licensing, registration, traffic control, insurance, offences, mandatory signs, accident claims |
| B | 15 | 8th-standard English: synonyms, antonyms, verbs, fill in the blanks |
| C | 15 | 10th-standard arithmetic: percentage, ratio, average, basic operations |
| D | 20 | General knowledge, current affairs, who is who, states and capitals, Indian geography |

You need **50% in Part A** and 50% in Parts B, C and D together. Reserved candidates get 25% relaxation in pass marks where a seat is reserved for them. Shortlisting for the driving test is 1:10. The driving test is out of 100, you need **60, with no relaxation for any category**.

## URSC salary 2026

| Post | Pay level | Starting basic |
|---|---|---|
| Technician 'B' | Level 3 | ₹21,700 a month + allowances |
| LVD 'A', Staff Car Driver 'A', HVD 'A' | Level 2 | ₹19,900 a month + allowances |

You'll be under NPS or the Unified Pension Scheme. URSC also lists free transport, limited housing, a medical scheme for you and dependants, LTC, group insurance and a subsidised canteen.

Downsides: **round-the-clock shifts** are possible, and you can be posted to any ISRO or Department of Space unit in India.

## Application fee and refund

| Who | Pay now | Refund after taking the written test |
|---|---|---|
| Women, SC/ST, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen | ₹500 | Full ₹500 |
| Everyone else (UR, OBC, EWS men) | ₹500 | ₹400 |

Pay online only through Bharatkosh (net banking, debit or credit card, UPI). **Skip the written test and you get nothing back.** The refund goes to your original payment method, or to the bank account you enter in the form if that fails, so type it carefully.

## How to apply for URSC recruitment 2026

Keep these ready:

- Passport-size photo with white background, not older than 6 months, 3.5 × 4.5 cm, JPG/JPEG, **100 KB to 200 KB**
- Signature, JPG/JPEG, **80 KB to 150 KB**
- 10th and ITI/NTC/NAC certificates, and driving licence and experience certificates for Driver posts
- SC/ST, PwBD or Ex-Servicemen certificate, if you want the full refund

1. Read the [official advertisement PDF](https://www.isro.gov.in/media_isro/pdf/recruitmentNotice/2026/Oct/URSClAdvt2410_24102026.pdf) for your post code.
2. Open the [URSC careers page on isro.gov.in](https://www.isro.gov.in/URSCRecruitment13.html) and click the online application link (it goes to the official form hosted on cdn.digialm.com).
3. Register with an email ID you check daily. Hall tickets and call letters come **only by email**, and URSC won't change your email or mobile later.
4. Fill in the form, upload documents and enter your bank details for the refund.
5. Pay ₹500 through Bharatkosh and keep your registration number and a printout.

A submitted form can't be edited. If you made a mistake, apply again; URSC keeps the latest paid application. If you're in a government or PSU job, inform your employer now and bring an NOC to the skill test, or you won't be allowed to take it.

Questions go to **cbt@ursc.gov.in**. URSC doesn't take phone calls about the recruitment.

## Should you apply?

**ITI Electronics Mechanic holders: yes.** 24 seats in one trade is a lot for an ISRO centre, and the paper is from your own ITI syllabus. Revise trade theory properly, because the written score alone decides who reaches the skill test.

**Fitter, Electrician and Refrigeration & AC: apply, but know the numbers.** 8, 4 and 5 seats, and some are at Hassan. If you're also eligible for [ISRO SDSC SHAR recruitment 2026](/isro-sdsc-shar-recruitment-2026), which has 17 Fitter seats and closes on 9 November, fill that one first.

**Turner, Machinist, Motor Vehicle Mechanic, Electroplater: only if you're unreserved or EWS.** These have 1 or 2 seats each, all UR except one EWS seat in Machinist.

**Drivers: apply if your experience papers are clean.** The Motor Vehicles Act part is half the paper, so study it section by section.
