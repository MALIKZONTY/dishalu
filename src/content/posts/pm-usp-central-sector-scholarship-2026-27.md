The **PM-USP Central Sector Scholarship 2026-27 last date is 30 November 2026**. It gives **₹12,000 a year** during graduation and **₹20,000 a year** during post-graduation to students who scored in the **top 20% of their Class 12 board** and come from families earning **up to ₹4.5 lakh a year**.

You apply on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP). Applying is free. But your college must also **verify your form by 15 December 2026**, or it won't count.

## Eligibility for a fresh application

You can apply as a **fresh** student only if all of these are true:

- **You passed Class 12 in the last academic year** (so, for 2026-27, generally the 2026 batch). The FAQ says students who took a drop year after Class 12 are not eligible.
- **You scored above the 80th percentile** of students who passed in your stream (Science, Commerce or Humanities) from your board. In simple words, you were in the top 20% of your board's pass-outs in that stream.
- **Your board has put your name on the merit list.** State boards, CBSE and CISCE send the list of top 20th percentile students. If your name isn't on it, the scheme won't show up for you on NSP.
- **You're in a regular degree course.** Correspondence, distance and diploma courses are not covered.
- **Your college is recognised** by AICTE, UGC or the relevant regulatory body and has an **active AISHE code**. Private colleges are fine if they meet this.
- **Family income is up to ₹4.5 lakh a year.**
- **You don't take any other scholarship, fee waiver or fee reimbursement**, central or state.

### Who is NOT eligible

- Diploma and polytechnic students
- Students in distance or open learning courses
- Students who took a gap year after Class 12
- Students already getting a state fee reimbursement or another merit scholarship
- 2nd, 3rd or 4th year students who never got this scholarship before (they can't apply fresh)

That last point matters in states with large fee reimbursement schemes. If your state already pays your college fee, you have to choose one. For most B.Tech students in private colleges, the state fee reimbursement is worth far more than ₹12,000 a year, so don't give it up for this.

## How much you get

| Level | Amount per year |
|---|---|
| Graduation, years 1 to 3 | ₹12,000 |
| Post-graduation | ₹20,000 |
| B.Tech / B.E. | ₹12,000 in years 1 to 3, ₹20,000 in year 4 (scholarship ends at graduation) |
| 5-year integrated or professional course | ₹20,000 in the 4th and 5th year |

The money goes straight to your **Aadhaar-seeded bank account** by DBT. The total duration can't be more than 5 years.

So a student who does a 3-year B.Sc and a 2-year M.Sc, and renews every year, can get ₹36,000 + ₹40,000 = **₹76,000** in total. A B.Tech student can get ₹56,000 over 4 years.

## How the 82,000 scholarships are split

The guidelines cap fresh scholarships at **82,000 a year**. Here's how they're divided:

- **Between boards:** each state board gets slots based on the state's population aged 18 to 25, after CBSE and ICSE get their share based on how many students pass from them.
- **Boys and girls:** 50% of the slots are for girls.
- **Streams:** Science, Humanities and Commerce in the ratio **3 : 3 : 1**. Commerce students compete for fewer seats.
- **Categories:** 15% SC, 7.5% ST, 27% OBC, plus 5% horizontal reservation for students with benchmark disabilities (40% or more).
- **J&K and Ladakh:** of the slots for these two UTs, 3% are for Ladakh.

If a category, gender or state doesn't get enough applications, the empty slots can be moved to others, so more eligible students get the money.

Meeting the eligibility rules does not guarantee selection. The selection list is made on merit within these slots and shown on NSP.

## Documents you'll need

**Fresh applicants:**

- Class 12 marksheet
- Family income certificate
- Caste or category certificate (SC, ST, OBC students)
- Disability certificate (if it applies)
- Aadhaar number, and a **bank account in your own name that is seeded with Aadhaar**
- Your college's AISHE code (ask the college office)

**Renewal applicants:** the previous year's marksheet. The income certificate is needed only for fresh applications.

Keep copies ready. Your college's Institute Nodal Officer may ask to see them during verification.

## How to apply on NSP, step by step

1. Go to [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in/).
2. Complete **One Time Registration (OTR)** with your mobile number and Aadhaar, and finish e-KYC. You get an OTR number that stays with you for your whole college life. Lost it? Use **Forgot OTR Number** with your Aadhaar.
3. Log in with your Aadhaar or OTR number. Your personal details come from the OTR form.
4. Select your **domicile state** and the **Post-Matric** option. This creates your Application ID, which you'll see under **My Application**.
5. Fill in general details, category and academic details. **Your name, father's name and Class 12 roll number must match your marksheet exactly**, or the scheme won't appear.
6. Pick your **institute carefully using its AISHE code**. Many colleges have similar names. If you pick the wrong one, your college can't verify you and NSP won't fix it later.
7. Pick the right **course and course duration**. A wrong duration causes errors at renewal time.
8. Select **PM-USP Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship for College and University Students**, upload documents and click **Confirm and Submit** before **30 November 2026**.
9. Tell your college scholarship desk. **Institute verification closes on 15 December 2026.** After that, the state nodal officer verifies it by 31 December.

You can save the form and finish it in more than one sitting. Once you click Confirm and Submit, you can only edit it if a verifier marks it "defective".

## Renewal rules (2nd year onwards)

You don't get the money automatically every year. You must **apply for renewal on NSP each year** before the deadline.

To renew, you need:

- **At least 50% marks** in the previous year's annual exam
- **At least 75% attendance**
- No complaints of indiscipline, criminal behaviour or ragging (these lead to losing the scholarship)
- No other scholarship from another department or ministry

Students who got a fresh scholarship in any of the last 4 academic years can renew. If you missed renewal one year, you can apply again the next year if you still meet the rules. That missed year's money is gone, though.

Changing college in 2nd or 3rd year? Contact your **State Nodal Agency at least 2 weeks before the NSP closing date** with your old and new college ID cards, college names and AISHE codes, and course details. The FAQ says requests in the last 7 days before closing won't be taken.

## How to check status and payment

- **Application status:** log in on NSP, go to **My Application**, and click **Check Your Status**.
- **Payment:** use **Know Your Payment** on the PFMS portal with your Aadhaar, bank account number or NSP Application ID. You can also see it in your NSP login.
- If PFMS shows "Success" but no money arrived, check which bank account your Aadhaar was seeded to at that time.

## Why CSSS applications get rejected

- **College didn't verify by the cut-off.** An application not verified by the institute or the state is treated as invalid. This is the biggest one, so follow up.
- **Name mismatch** between Aadhaar and Class 12 marksheet (for example "M.P. Singh" on Aadhaar but "Mahendra Pratap Singh" on the marksheet). Fix Aadhaar to match the marksheet.
- **Wrong institute or course selected.** It can't be corrected after final submission.
- **College's AISHE status is inactive.** Payment isn't released for such colleges. Check it on the AISHE portal.
- **Bank account not seeded with Aadhaar.** Check this with your bank or on NPCI's BASE page.
- **Taking a state fee reimbursement or another scholarship at the same time.**

"Defective" and "Rejected" are different. Defective means you can correct and resubmit. Rejected is final.

## Never pay an agent

The FAQ is clear: **applying on NSP is free**. The Ministry and NIC only contact you by SMS and email from NSP. Nobody can "get your name added" to the board merit list or speed up approval for money. Don't share your OTR login or OTP with any cyber café or WhatsApp group.

For grievances, use the **Grievance Registration** tab inside your NSP login. Scheme questions can go to the Department of Higher Education's scholarship division at ns1-scholarship@gov.in or 011-20862360 (from the official FAQ).

## What about PM-USP's J&K and Ladakh scheme?

PM-USP has a second component, the **Special Scholarship Scheme for J&K and Ladakh**. The 2026-27 NSP list shows it as open from 22 October to **30 November 2026**, run by AICTE.

It is a separate scheme for domicile students of J&K and Ladakh who study outside the two UTs, with family income up to ₹8 lakh. It pays academic fee up to a ceiling plus ₹1 lakh a year for maintenance.

The latest specification document on NSP is still labelled 2025-26, so check the [scheme document](https://scholarships.gov.in/public/schemeGuidelines/AICTE/PM_USPY(SSSJKL)SchemeSpecifications.pdf) and the AICTE portal for this year's details.

## My take

If you're a 2026 Class 12 pass-out in the top 20% of your board, from a family under ₹4.5 lakh, and **not** getting a state fee reimbursement, apply this week. ₹12,000 a year isn't huge, but it's free money that renews for 3 to 5 years if you keep 50% marks and 75% attendance.

If your state already pays your full college fee, compare first. You can't take both, and the state scheme is usually bigger.

Already a beneficiary? Your renewal is the easy money most people lose. Log in, renew, and then make sure your college verifies it before 15 December.
