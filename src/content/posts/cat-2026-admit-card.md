**Update (6 November 2026):** The **CAT 2026 admit card download begins today, 6 November 2026,** on [iimcat.ac.in](https://iimcat.ac.in). The advertisement had said 4 November, but IIM Indore's [media release of 4 November (PDF)](https://g03.tcsion.com//per/g03/pub/726/EForms/image/ImageDocUpload/71161/7/9901914301.pdf) moved the window to **6–29 November**. It also says admit cards have been generated for all eligible candidates, and a free mock test is live on the CAT website from 6 November.

You can download the card any time until **29 November**, which is also the exam day. IIM Indore is the convening IIM this year.

So if you registered by the 15 September deadline, keep your login ready. Download the card on the first or second day, not the night before.

## CAT 2026 admit card release date and time

The advertisement first gave the window as "November 04 to 29, 2026". On 4 November, IIM Indore put out a media release saying admit cards can be downloaded **between 6 November and 29 November 2026**, only from iimcat.ac.in with your CAT login ID and password. The home page now lists "Nov 06, 2026: CAT 2026 Admit Card Download Begins".

No time is given. In past years the link went live in the afternoon of the release day, so if it isn't there in the morning, wait.

The same release says IIMs have added extra security this year: **face matching** at the centre and **mobile jammers** at select locations. So make sure the photo on your card looks like you on exam day, and don't carry a phone into the hall expecting to use it.

On release day the site is usually slow because lakhs of candidates log in at once. If it doesn't load, try again after an hour. Nothing changes if you download on day 1 or day 3. What matters is that you check the details early, while there's time to fix a problem.

## How to download the CAT 2026 admit card

1. Open [iimcat.ac.in](https://iimcat.ac.in).
2. Click **Registered Candidate Login**.
3. Enter your **User ID** and **password** (both are in the registration email you got in August–September).
4. Type the captcha and log in.
5. Click the **Admit Card** link on your dashboard and download the PDF.
6. Take a colour print on **A4 paper**, and keep one spare copy.

Forgot your password? Use the "Forgot password" option on the login page. It sends a reset link to your registered email and phone. This is why the advertisement asks every candidate to keep the same email and mobile number throughout the selection process.

## Details to check on your CAT admit card

Look at these the moment you download it:

- Your name, photo and signature (they must match your ID card)
- CAT registration number
- Exam date, **session** and reporting time
- **Test centre name and full address**
- Whether you get extra time or a scribe (only PwBD candidates who asked for it)
- The exam-day instructions printed on the card

Your test centre will be in one of the 5 cities you picked, in your order of preference. But the advertisement says the city list can change "based on the discretion of the CAT authorities". So check the city and the address even if you assume it's your first choice.

If your photo, name or category is wrong, contact the CAT helpdesk through the "Contact Us" page on iimcat.ac.in right away. Note that the advertisement says **no change in category** is allowed after registration.

## CAT 2026 exam pattern (as in CAT 2025)

The official advertisement doesn't list the pattern. The detailed pattern is on the CAT website. For reference, this is how **CAT 2025** was set:

| Section | Questions (CAT 2025) | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Verbal Ability and Reading Comprehension (VARC) | 24 | 40 minutes |
| Data Interpretation and Logical Reasoning (DILR) | 22 | 40 minutes |
| Quantitative Ability (QA) | 22 | 40 minutes |
| **Total** | **68** | **120 minutes** |

- **Marking (CAT 2025):** +3 for a right answer, −1 for a wrong MCQ, no negative marking for TITA (type-in-the-answer) questions.
- **Sectional timing:** you can't move between sections. When the 40 minutes for a section are up, the next section starts on its own.

If IIM Indore changes anything for 2026, it will be in the instructions on your admit card and on iimcat.ac.in. Read them once, fully.

## What to carry on exam day

- **Printed admit card** (colour, A4)
- **Original photo ID**: Aadhaar, PAN card, passport, driving licence or voter ID. The name must match your admit card exactly.
- **PwBD candidates:** your disability certificate, and the scribe's documents if you use a scribe.

Don't carry a phone, smartwatch, calculator or any paper. The test screen has an on-screen calculator, and the centre gives you a scribble pad. Reach well before the reporting time on your card. Late entry is not allowed once the gate closes.

## What comes after the exam

- **Response sheet and provisional answer key:** these came out in early December in past years, with a short objection window. No 2026 dates have been announced.
- **Result:** first week of January 2027 (tentative), as per the advertisement.
- **Shortlisting:** each IIM publishes its own shortlisting criteria for interviews. The advertisement is clear that meeting the minimum eligibility alone "will not ensure consideration for shortlisting".
- **Non-IIM colleges:** many business schools accept CAT 2026 scores. Their selection is their own, and IIMs have no role in it.

Also thinking about a government job alongside the MBA plan? The [SSC CGL answer key 2026](/ssc-cgl-answer-key-2026) is due this week, and many CAT takers also write CGL.

## My take

With about 3.5 weeks left after the admit card comes out, this is mock-test time, not new-topic time. Give 2 full mocks a week, in your actual session slot if you can, and spend twice as long reviewing each mock as you spent writing it.

And print the admit card tomorrow, not on 28 November. Fixing a wrong photo takes days. Finding out at the centre gate doesn't help anyone.
