**ONGC Apprentice recruitment 2026 has 3,047 seats** across 24 work centres, for ITI, diploma, graduate and some 10th/12th pass candidates. If your trade comes under **NAPS**, you can still apply until **29 November 2026**. ONGC extended that date on 19 November. **NATS** trades (engineering and commerce graduate "executive" seats) closed on **20 November**.

There's **no exam and no fee**. Selection is on the marks in your qualifying exam, and the result is due on **15 December 2026**.

## The domicile rule (read this first)

You can apply for a work centre **only if you live in, or studied your qualifying course in, a district linked to that centre**. Annexure 1 of the advertisement lists them. A few examples:

- **Andhra Pradesh:** Kakinada takes all AP districts. Rajahmundry takes only Krishna, East Godavari and West Godavari.
- **Tamil Nadu:** Chennai takes all districts. Karaikal takes only Cuddalore, Mayiladuthurai, Nagapattinam, Ariyalur, Tiruvarur, Thanjavur, Trichy, Pudukkottai and Ramanathapuram, plus Karaikal (Puducherry).
- **Gujarat:** Ahmedabad, Ankleshwar, Vadodara, Cambay and Mehsana take all districts. Hazira takes only Surat, Navsari, Tapi, Dang and Valsad.
- **Maharashtra:** Mumbai, Panvel, Nhava and Uran take all districts (Uran prefers Raigad).
- **Assam:** Jorhat, Sivasagar and Silchar take all districts. **Bokaro** takes only Bokaro district.

Apply for **one trade at one work centre**. If you apply in more than one, ONGC allots just one, its choice.

## Eligibility: age and qualification

**Age:** 18 to 24 years as on **29 November 2026**, so you must be born between 29 November 2002 and 29 November 2008.

| Category | Upper age relaxation |
|---|---|
| SC / ST | 5 years |
| OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) | 3 years |
| PwBD | 10 years (15 for SC/ST, 13 for OBC) |

**Qualification by trade (main groups):**

| Trade group | Qualification needed | Scheme |
|---|---|---|
| Electrician, Fitter, COPA, Diesel Mechanic, Welder, Instrument Mechanic, Machinist, Stenographer and other ITI trades | ITI in that trade | NAPS |
| Fire Safety Technician (Oil & Gas) | 12th pass, or 2-year ITI after 10th | NAPS |
| Library Assistant | 10th pass | NAPS |
| Secretarial Office Assistant | Any graduate | NAPS |
| Executive HR / Executive Finance | BBA / BCom | NAPS |
| Lab Chemist/Analyst | BSc Chemistry | NAPS |
| Computer Science, Electrical, Mechanical, E&T, Instrumentation Executive (Oil & Gas) | 3-year engineering diploma | NAPS |
| Accounts Executive, Store Keeper | BCom / any graduate | NATS (closed) |
| Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, Electronics, CS, Instrumentation Executive (Graduate) | BE/BTech in that branch | NATS (closed) |
| Petroleum Executive | Graduate with Geology | NATS (closed) |

**You can't apply if:**
- You've already done, or are doing, an apprenticeship under the Apprentices Act
- **3 years have passed** since you passed your qualifying exam (counted on 29 November 2026)
- For Technician Apprentice seats, you have 1 year or more of training or job experience after your qualification

There's no TA/DA, boarding or lodging. So if you choose a centre far from home, rent comes out of this stipend.

## Selection process

There is **no written exam or interview**.

1. ONGC downloads all applications from NAPS and NATS and sorts them by trade and work centre.
2. It checks eligibility: qualification, age, domicile, scheme.
3. A **merit list** is made from the marks in your qualifying exam. **If marks are tied, the older candidate goes first.**
4. Shortlisted candidates are called for **document verification**, with originals.
5. A final merit list is made after verification and reservation rules are applied.
6. Results go up on ongcapprentices.ongc.co.in, and selected candidates are emailed. You then finish the contract on NAPS/NATS.

Documents for verification: 10th certificate (date of birth proof), marksheets and certificates of your qualifying course, caste or PwBD certificate if you claim one, ID proof, and your NAPS/NATS registration details. If your marksheet doesn't show the result date, bring a certificate from your college principal that does, because the 3-year rule depends on it.

## How to apply (NAPS trades, till 29 November)

1. Go to the NAPS portal, [apprenticeshipindia.gov.in](https://apprenticeshipindia.gov.in), and register as a candidate (Aadhaar, mobile, email). Complete your profile with your education details.
2. Search for **ONGC** apprenticeship openings, or go through the link on [ongcapprentices.ongc.co.in](https://ongcapprentices.ongc.co.in/).
3. Pick **one trade at one work centre** that matches your domicile district.
4. Apply, and save a screenshot or PDF of the confirmation.

Keep these ready: email ID, mobile number, ITI/diploma/degree certificate, 10th and 12th marksheets, caste certificate if any, PAN if you have one, and Aadhaar. Questions go to **ongc_skilldev@ongc.co.in**.

## Should you apply?

**Yes, if you're in an ITI trade or did a diploma or general degree recently** and your district matches a centre. It costs nothing, takes 20 minutes, and an ONGC certificate looks good on a CV for private oil, gas and refinery jobs.

Be clear on the limits. It's a **12-month training contract, not a job**. ONGC says plainly it has no obligation to hire you afterwards. The stipend is modest, and you pay your own stay.

If you have good marks, apply in your home district rather than a bigger centre further away. Merit is compared only with others who applied for the same trade at the same centre.

Want a regular PSU job instead? Look at [NLC India's executive recruitment](/nlc-india-executive-recruitment-2026), which is open till 19 December.
